📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 3 Animal World

Get ready · 热身
— Do you often go to the zoo? What is your favourite animal?
— I like butterflies. They are insects. They can fly.

Lesson 1 · 课文短文（节选）
Lily and her classmates are going to see the animals in the zoo.
First, they are going to see the sharks. Sharks are fish. They can swim under the water. They are very big and like to eat small animals in the sea.
Then they want to see the snakes and the butterflies. Snakes are reptiles. They are very long and cold. Butterflies are beautiful animals. They are insects. They have six legs, and they can fly.
Next, they want to see some mammals, such as monkeys, pandas and tigers. Mammals give milk to their babies.
At last, they are going to see the penguins. Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They live on the ice. Look, how cute they are!

Lesson 1 · 分类知识（节选）
Reptiles have cold blood. They have scales. Most reptiles have four legs. All insects have six legs. Mother insects lay eggs. Most insects can fly.

Lesson 2 · 问答对话（节选）
— What kind of animals are monkeys?
— They are mammals.
— What can monkeys do?
— They can climb trees very well.
— What do monkeys like to eat?
— They like to eat peaches and bananas.

Lesson 3 · 短文 The Elephant（节选）
Elephants are mammals. They can be four metres tall. They don't eat meat. They only eat fruit, leaves and grass. They need a lot of food, so they spend most of their time eating. The elephant has two long, white teeth called tusks. It is the only animal with a trunk — a special long nose. The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut. The elephant is also the mammal with the biggest ears. They look like two big fans. Elephants live in Africa and Asia. They are very strong, very smart and of course, very, very big.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写in·sect，词根sect-意为“切”。✏️口诀：身体“切”成三节的虫，就是insect昆虫
音·发音/ˈɪn.sekt/ 重音在前。
🎧 跟读：butterflies are insects · all insects have six legs
意·释义昆虫🦋（蝴蝶、蚂蚁、蜜蜂、甲虫；注意：蜘蛛八条腿，不是insect！）
用·句型① Butterflies are insects. They have six legs, and they can fly.（课本原句）
② All insects have six legs.（所有昆虫都有六条腿。）
③ Mother insects lay eggs.（昆虫妈妈产卵。）
④ Bees are insects.（蜜蜂是昆虫。）
拓·同族词insect→insects（复数）；同根词：section部分 · dissect解剖；同单元分类词：reptile · mammal

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
分类Bees and ants are insects.
特征All insects have six legs.
生命周期Mother insects lay eggs.
找茬A spider is not an insect — it has eight legs.
功能句总结 · all / most + 复数

数腿句：All insects have six legs.

能力句：Most insects can fly.（大多数昆虫会飞。）

否定对比：A spider isn't an insect — it has eight legs.

⚠️ all / most后面接复数名词，谓语动词用复数！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

课本说“All insects have six legs”。带孩子去小区数虫腿：蝴蝶、蚂蚁6条是insect；蜘蛛8条不是——这个“找茬”游戏能一次记住分类标准，顺带把Mother insects lay eggs（卵生）也讲了。

📖 微型故事《Six-Legged Club》

🐜 草坪上有个秘密俱乐部，门口牌子上写着：Six-Legged Club（六腿俱乐部）。蚂蚁来了：“Six legs!”蝴蝶来了：“Six legs, and I can fly!”蜘蛛爬过来也想进，蚂蚁摇摇头：“Sorry, you have eight legs. Insects only!”蜘蛛委屈地走开，去成立了Eight-Legged Club。毛毛虫在旁边小声问：“那我什么时候能进？”蝴蝶妈妈笑：“Every insect starts as an egg — you'll get your six legs soon!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 俱乐部为什么叫Six-Legged Club？(昆虫有六条腿)
• 蜘蛛为什么不能进？(八条腿，不是昆虫)
• 问问孩子：Find an insect near our home — how many legs does it have?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 标准 ── six legs六条腿 · 身体分三节 ├── 家族 ── butterfly蝴蝶 · ant蚂蚁 · bee蜜蜂 · ladybird瓢虫 ├── 生命周期 ── lay eggs产卵 · most can fly会飞 ├── 不是昆虫 ── spider蜘蛛(8腿) · snail蜗牛(没腿) └── 词根 ── sect-切：insect分节虫 · section部分

🏛️ 词源故事

insect来自拉丁语insectum，意思是“切开的”——因为昆虫的身体像被切成了头、胸、腹三节。和它同根的section（部分）今天还在数学课本里出现。🎯 讲给孩子：看一只蚂蚁，等于读一首“切开”的小诗。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
insectin-（进入）+ sect（切）→ 切开分节的虫
sectionsect（切）+ -ion → 部分、截面
dissectdis-（分开）+ sect → 解剖、剖析
insecticideinsect + -cide（杀）→ 杀虫剂

📜 名言 & 谚语

“As busy as a bee.”（忙得像蜜蜂一样。）——英语经典习语，夸人勤奋就说这句。蜜蜂是insect家族里最勤劳的“劳动模范”。

🎵 歌词里的 insect

🎵 Dave Matthews Band《Ants Marching》(1994)：90年代名团代表作，“蚂蚁行军”的节奏就像一队蚂蚁排队出发（知名度中上 已标注）。
🎵 与reptile页、mammal页同主线互链——六条腿小家伙是动物分类课的第三站。

⚡ 冷知识

蚂蚁没有肺，靠身体侧面的小气孔呼吸；世界上已知的昆虫超过100万种，是动物界最庞大的家族；蜻蜓的一只大眼睛由上万只小眼组成，几乎能看到四面八方。
