Lesson 1 · B. Let's play
— We're going to the zoo. What animals do you want to see? Why?
— I want to see tigers. They're big.
— I want to see pandas. They're cute.
— I want to see elephants. They're big and tall. I want to see their long noses.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and answer
— Hi, Linda. What are you doing?
— I'm drawing a picture of my favourite animal. It's a big, grey animal.
— Yes, my favourite animal is the elephant.
— What's your favourite animal? — Guess!
— Wow, that's a beautiful picture! Let me see. Mmm, your favourite animal is the horse?
— No. Look. My favourite animal is the bee. — It's lovely!

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（节选）
Different children love different animals.
Bob likes elephants. An elephant eats leaves and grass. It lives on the grassland or in the forest. It is big and strong. It has two long teeth called tusks and a big nose called a trunk.
Amanda loves pandas. A panda lives in a bamboo forest and eats bamboo there. It's black and white.

📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写love + ly = lovely——名词 love 直接加后缀 ly 变形容词
✏️ 口诀：有爱就可爱，love 加 ly
音·发音/ˈlʌvli/ 重音在前：LOVE-ly
🎧 跟读：a lovely panda · What a lovely day! · It's lovely!
意·释义可爱的；美好的（英式口语的万能好评：a lovely day 好天气）
用·句型① It's lovely! (它真可爱！——课本原句)
② What a lovely panda! (多可爱的熊猫！)
③ Have a lovely day! (祝你今天愉快！)
④ Look at my lovely little sister. (看我可爱的小妹妹。)
拓·同族词love 爱 → lovely 可爱的 → lovelier 更可爱的；同款后缀：friend→friendly、week→weekly

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
夸赞It's lovely!
感叹What a lovely panda!
祝愿Have a lovely day!
词族love → lovely → lovelier
功能句总结 · -ly 后缀的两副面孔

名词+ly → 形容词：love→lovely, friend→friendly, week→weekly

形容词+ly → 副词：slow→slowly, quick→quickly

同是加 ly，方向相反：接名词变形容词，接形容词变副词！

⚠️ lovely 是形容词不是副词：It's lovely! ✔（不能说 run lovely）

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

lovely 是课本 Lesson 2 的原句用词——夸小蜜蜂 It's lovely! 五年级要让孩子掌握它背后的词法规律：名词+ly→形容词（love→lovely, friend→friendly），与"形容词+ly→副词"（slow→slowly）方向相反，是高频考点。建议玩"后缀变形"游戏：说一个名词，孩子变形容词并造句。

📖 微型故事《The Lovely Contest》

🐝 动物园举办"最可爱大赛"。熊猫说："I'm black and white, lovely!" 企鹅说："I waddle, lovely!" 小蜜蜂嗡嗡飞过来："I'm small, I make honey, and I help flowers!" 裁判山羊爷爷问大家："Who makes your food sweeter?" 全场安静了。"The bee!" 大家把奖杯递给小蜜蜂："Small can be lovely, too!" 小蜜蜂举着比自己还大的奖杯，嗡嗡地笑了。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 谁赢了"最可爱大赛"？(The bee)
• 熊猫怎么夸自己？(I'm black and white)
• 问问孩子：In your eyes, which animal is the loveliest? Why?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 词族 ── love 爱 → lovely 可爱的 → lovelier 更可爱
├── 后缀规律 ── 名词+ly→形容词（friendly / weekly）
├── 用法 ── It's lovely! · What a lovely day! · Have a lovely day!
├── 课文 ── My favourite animal is the bee. It's lovely!
└── 近义 ── cute 可爱的 · pretty 漂亮的

🏛️ 词源故事

lovely = love + ly，中古英语里就有。有趣的是 -ly 有两副面孔：接在名词后变形容词（lovely、friendly），接在形容词后变副词（slowly、quickly）。在英式口语里 lovely 是万能好评——天气好 "Lovely weather!"、饭好吃 "Lovely meal!"，英国人一天能说八遍。
🎯 讲给孩子：学会一个 lovely，等于拿到"夸夸万能钥匙"。

📜 名言 & 谚语

"Love makes the world go round."（爱让世界转动。）—— 经典英语谚语；而 lovely 正是"用爱的目光看一切"的样子。

🎵 歌词里的 lovely

🎵 Billie Eilish & Khalid《Lovely》(2018)：全球流媒体爆款，歌名即本词——与 love 页词族互链；与 bee 页互链：课本原句 "It's lovely!" 夸的就是小蜜蜂！

⚡ 冷知识

录《Lovely》时 Billie Eilish 才 16 岁，这首歌作为美剧《十三个原因》第二季原声发行，流媒体播放量早已破十亿。英语里想夸"可爱到心都化了"还可以说 "That's adorable!"——adorable 比 lovely 程度更高，夸小熊猫、小蜜蜂都适用。
