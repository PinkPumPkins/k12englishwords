📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 3 Animals

Lesson 1 · B. Let's play
— We're going to the zoo. What animals do you want to see? Why?
— I want to see tigers. They're big.
— I want to see pandas. They're cute.
— I want to see elephants. They're big and tall. I want to see their long noses.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and answer
— Hi, Linda. What are you doing?
— I'm drawing a picture of my favourite animal. It's a big, grey animal.
— Yes, my favourite animal is the elephant.
— What's your favourite animal? — Guess!
— Wow, that's a beautiful picture! Let me see. Mmm, your favourite animal is the horse?
— No. Look. My favourite animal is the bee. — It's lovely!

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（节选）
Different children love different animals.
Bob likes elephants. An elephant eats leaves and grass. It lives on the grassland or in the forest. It is big and strong. It has two long teeth called tusks and a big nose called a trunk.
Amanda loves pandas. A panda lives in a bamboo forest and eats bamboo there. It's black and white.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写a·ni·mal 三音节，重音在最前：AN-i-mal；词根 anim- 表"气息/生命"——有气息的才是动物
✏️ 口诀：有气(anim)才是活物
音·发音/ˈænɪml/ 中间弱读 /ɪ/，别读成 an-i-MAL
🎧 跟读：wild animals · farm animals · my favourite animal
意·释义动物 🦁（泛指一切动物；口语里也常指"除人以外的兽类"）
用·句型① What animals do you want to see? (你想看什么动物？——课本原句)
② My favourite animal is the panda. (我最喜欢的动物是熊猫。)
③ Different children love different animals. (不同的孩子喜欢不同的动物。——课本原句)
④ There are many animals on the farm. (农场上有许多动物。)
拓·同族词词根 anim-（生命/气息）：animate 使有活力 · animation 动画（"让画活起来"！）
分类词：pets 宠物 / wild animals 野生动物 / farm animals 农场动物

功能句总结 · 介绍动物三件套

是什么：My favourite animal is... (我最喜欢的动物是……)

什么特征：It's big and strong. / They're cute.

为什么喜欢：Because it's lovely! (因为它很可爱！)

⚠️ favourite 拼写：英式 favourite，美式 favorite——课本用英式！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

五年级上册 Unit 3 是动物主题单元。建议在家玩"我最喜欢的动物"问答：What's your favourite animal? Why do you like it? 让孩子用"喜欢什么+什么特征+为什么"三步回答。重点纠正 favourite 拼写和 animal 的重音位置（重音在最前）。

📖 微型故事《The Animal Class Vote》

🐾 英语课上投票选"班级最爱动物"。老师说："What's your favourite animal?" 小明说："My favourite animal is the panda. It's black and white!" 小红举手："Mine is the bee!" 全班愣住了。小红接着说："It's small, but it can fly and it makes honey!" 老师笑了："Different children love different animals — just like our class!" 最后蜜蜂和熊猫平票，大家决定把两种动物都画上墙报。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 小红最喜欢的动物是什么？(The bee)
• 熊猫长什么样？(It's black and white.)
• 问问孩子：What's YOUR favourite animal? Why?

🏛️ 词源故事

animal 来自拉丁语 animalis（有气息的），词根 anima 意为"呼吸、灵魂"。古人的逻辑很朴素：能呼吸的就是活物。所以"动物"和"动画"（animation，让画动起来）是亲兄弟——动画就是"被赋予了生命的画"。
🎯 讲给孩子：判断是不是 animal，先看它会不会呼吸。

📜 名言 & 谚语

"Animals are such agreeable friends — they ask no questions, they pass no criticisms."（动物是如此令人愉快的朋友——它们不发问，也不批评。）—— 英国作家乔治·艾略特的名句。

🎵 歌词里的 animal

🎵 Maroon 5《Animals》(2014)：歌名即本词，全球热单，副歌反复 "Animals" 一词极易上口。备选：Martin Garrix《Animals》(2013) 电音炸场。与 farm 页互链：农场动物都是 animals！

⚡ 冷知识

动画片 animation 一词就来自 animal 的词根——最早人们给静止的画画上腿，让它们"活"过来，所以叫"动画"（活起来的画）。另一个冷知识：科学上人也属于动物界 Animalia——课本说 "Different children love different animals"，原来我们自己是"会提问的动物"。
