📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

Let's Spell · -tion / -ture 的读音
station · transportation · dictionary · picture · furniture · temperature · nature · culture · future · action

Fun Time · Good to know（达·芬奇）
Leonardo da Vinci 画了《蒙娜丽莎》与人体、动物素描，还画下直升机、坦克的构想图；他 1502 年设计的桥，直到 2001 年才在挪威建成。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写po·em —— 和 poet 差一个字母：poet 是人，poem 是作品！
✏️ 口诀：poet 尾巴 t 是诗人，poem 尾巴 m 是诗。
音·发音/ˈpəʊ.ɪm/ 两个音节，结尾 /m/ 闭上嘴收音
🎧 跟读：recite a poem · 'In the Quiet Night' is a poem.
意·释义诗；诗歌 📜（一首一首的诗，可数名词：a poem / many poems；诗歌总称用 poetry，不可数）
用·句型① He wrote many famous poems. (他写了许多著名的诗。)
② One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'. (他的名作之一是《静夜思》。)
③ Let me recite a poem. (我来背一首诗。)
④ Can you write a short poem in English? (你能用英文写首小诗吗？)
拓·同族词poem 一首诗（可数）→ poetry 诗歌总称（不可数）→ poet 诗人；a poem by Li Bai 李白的一首诗

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.
朗诵Let me recite a poem for you.
喜好My favourite poem is 'In the Quiet Night' by Li Bai.
对比poem 可数 / poetry 不可数：He writes poems. I love poetry.
📋 功能句总结 · poem 与 poetry

可数：a poem 一首诗 / many poems 许多首诗——能数得清的用 poem。

不可数：I love poetry. 我爱诗歌（总体）。

…写的诗：a poem by Li Bai（用 by 不用 of）：The poem is by Li Bai.

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

课本 Lesson 1 点名了《静夜思》：One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'. 陪学最顺手的材料就是这首诗：先背中文，再读英译名 In the Quiet Night，孩子立刻理解 poem 是“一首诗”。进阶玩法：用英文两句“翻译”任意一首学过的古诗，不求完美，只求敢开口。

📖 微型故事《四十种月光》

小满发现图书馆里有好几本李白诗集英译本：“Mum, the same poem has different English words!” 妈妈说：“《静夜思》的英译本有几十种——每个译者都有自己的月光。” 小满试着造自己的：“The moon is a lamp in the sky. I look up, and I think of home.” 妈妈把它抄在便签上：“Now you made a new poem. 世上又多了一种月光。”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• How many English versions does the poem have? (Dozens / 几十种。)
• What did Xiaoman's translation say? (The moon is a lamp in the sky...)
• 问问孩子：如果让你翻译“举头望明月”，你会怎么说？

🏛️ 词源故事

poem 来自希腊语 poiema——“做出来的东西”，和 poet（制作者）同根。在古希腊，诗是被唱出来的：琴一响，诗人开嗓，听的人流泪。所以 poem 的老祖宗里就带着“表演”基因，recite（朗诵）正是它的老习惯。
🎯 讲给孩子：诗不是印在纸上的，是做出来、唱出来的。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Two roads diverged in a wood, and I—I took the one less traveled by.” 🌲
林中有两条路，我选择了人迹更少的一条。—— 美国诗人弗罗斯特《未选择的路》：一首 poem，安慰了无数做选择的人。

🎵 歌词里的 poem

🎵 Don McLean《Vincent (Starry Starry Night)》（1971）：一首歌同时致敬画的诗与诗的画——梵高把夜空画成了 poem，Don McLean 把画唱成了 poem。
🔗 与 poet 页互链：poet（诗人）写 poem（诗歌），两页唱同一首《Vincent》。

⚡ 冷知识

《静夜思》全诗只有 20 个字，英译本却有几十种——同一句“床前明月光”，有人译 moonlight，有人译 moonbeams，连“床”是睡床、井栏还是胡床，学者们都讨论了上百年。一首小 poem，够开一场研讨会。
