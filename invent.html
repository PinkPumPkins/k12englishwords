📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

Let's Spell · -tion / -ture 的读音
station · transportation · dictionary · picture · furniture · temperature · nature · culture · future · action

Fun Time · Good to know（达·芬奇）
Leonardo da Vinci 画了《蒙娜丽莎》与人体、动物素描，还画下直升机、坦克的构想图；他 1502 年设计的桥，直到 2001 年才在挪威建成。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写in·vent —— in + vent（来）：走进去“遇见”新点子；比 inventor 少个 or
✏️ 口诀：invent 是动作，inventor 是人。
音·发音/ɪnˈvent/ 两个音节，重音在后 vent
🎧 跟读：invent a machine · Edison invented the light bulb.
意·释义发明 🛠️（创造出前所未有的东西：Edison invented the light bulb. 爱迪生发明了电灯泡）
用·句型① He invented a lot of things. (他发明了很多东西。)
② Who invented the paper? (谁发明了纸？)
③ I want to invent a homework machine. (我想发明一台写作业机器。)
④ The machine was invented in 1900. (这台机器是 1900 年发明的。)
拓·同族词invent 发明 → invention 发明物 → inventor 发明家；对比 discover 发现（本来就有的东西）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.
名人问答Who invented the light bulb? — Edison did.
梦想I want to invent a machine that waters plants.
对比invent 创造新事物 / discover 发现已有事物：Edison invented the bulb; Newton discovered gravity.
📋 功能句总结 · invent 与 discover

invent + 全新的东西：Edison invented the light bulb.（电灯泡以前不存在。）

discover + 本来就有的东西：Newton discovered gravity.（引力一直在那儿。）

课本句：He's famous for his inventions.（他因发明而闻名。）be famous for + 名词。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

invent 是动词根，inventor 是人——先玩“动词找主人”游戏：说 invent 让孩子抢答 inventor。再讲两个英语易混词：invent（发明，造新东西）和 discover（发现，找到本来就有的东西）——爱迪生 invent 电灯，牛顿 discover 引力。最后留个开放题：如果你能 invent 一样东西帮妈妈做家务，会是什么？

📖 微型故事《从“为什么”开始》

奶奶开门总喊腰疼，小柯盯着门缝看了一晚上：“Why not a door stopper?” 他用橡皮和木块做了个楔子，塞进门底——门稳稳停住。“You invented something!” 奶奶直夸。小柯在本子上写下第一页：“Invention No.1: door stopper. For Grandma.” 原来伟大的发明，都从小小的“为什么”开始。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Xiaoke invent? (A door stopper for Grandma.)
• What was it made of? (A rubber and a piece of wood.)
• 问问孩子：家里谁最需要一个小发明？帮 TA 解决什么问题？

🏛️ 词源故事

invent 来自拉丁语 invenire：in（走进）+ venire（来）——“走进去，遇见它”。古人觉得发明不是凭空造物，而是“与好点子相遇”。
🎯 讲给孩子：发明家第一课，是走进问题里去“遇见”答案。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
invention发明（物）：The bulb was a great invention.
inventor发明家：Edison was a great inventor.
discover发现：Columbus discovered America.
create创造（更宽泛）：create a picture / create a new game

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Necessity is the mother of invention.” 🛠️
需要是发明之母。—— 源自柏拉图《理想国》的经典谚语：先有“真需要”，后有“好发明”。

🎵 歌词里的 invent

🎵 Daft Punk《Harder Better Faster Stronger》（2001）：电子传奇 Daft Punk 的机械循环神曲——“Work it harder, make it better, do it faster, makes us stronger”，发明就是这样一圈圈升级的。
🔗 与 inventor 页、invention 页互链：invent + or = inventor，invent + ion = invention，三页唱同一首“机器进化曲”。

⚡ 冷知识

不少伟大发明来自“翻车现场”：便利贴的胶水本是失败的强力胶，粘不住反而成了卖点；微波炉的发明者是雷达工程师——口袋里的巧克力被雷达烤化了，他才起了心思。至于名言“我没有失败，只是找到了一万种行不通的办法”，常被归到爱迪生名下，出处存疑——但精神确凿：发明就是反复试。
