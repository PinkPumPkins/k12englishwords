📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 3 Animal World

Get ready · 热身
— Do you often go to the zoo? What is your favourite animal?
— I like butterflies. They are insects. They can fly.

Lesson 1 · 课文短文（节选）
Lily and her classmates are going to see the animals in the zoo.
First, they are going to see the sharks. Sharks are fish. They can swim under the water. They are very big and like to eat small animals in the sea.
Then they want to see the snakes and the butterflies. Snakes are reptiles. They are very long and cold. Butterflies are beautiful animals. They are insects. They have six legs, and they can fly.
Next, they want to see some mammals, such as monkeys, pandas and tigers. Mammals give milk to their babies.
At last, they are going to see the penguins. Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They live on the ice. Look, how cute they are!

Lesson 1 · 分类知识（节选）
Reptiles have cold blood. They have scales. Most reptiles have four legs. All insects have six legs. Mother insects lay eggs. Most insects can fly.

Lesson 2 · 问答对话（节选）
— What kind of animals are monkeys?
— They are mammals.
— What can monkeys do?
— They can climb trees very well.
— What do monkeys like to eat?
— They like to eat peaches and bananas.

Lesson 3 · 短文 The Elephant（节选）
Elephants are mammals. They can be four metres tall. They don't eat meat. They only eat fruit, leaves and grass. They need a lot of food, so they spend most of their time eating. The elephant has two long, white teeth called tusks. It is the only animal with a trunk — a special long nose. The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut. The elephant is also the mammal with the biggest ears. They look like two big fans. Elephants live in Africa and Asia. They are very strong, very smart and of course, very, very big.

Story Time · 一句话导览
导盲犬 Bobo 陪着失明的 Sharon 走遍街道；聪明猫 Precious 在火灾里咬醒 Daniel——His clever cat saved him!

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写smart，s-m-ar-t；联想smile（微笑）也是sm开头——聪明的孩子爱笑。✏️口诀：smart聪明又机灵
音·发音/smɑːt/ 单音节；美音/smɑːrt/带卷舌。
🎧 跟读：very strong, very smart and very, very big
意·释义adj. 聪明的；机敏的（英式英语里还指“衣着光鲜”：a smart suit）
用·句型① They are very strong, very smart and of course, very, very big.（它们很强壮、很聪明，当然，还非常非常大。——课本原句）
② Elephants are smart animals.（大象是聪明的动物。）
③ You are so smart!（你真聪明！）
④ My dog is very smart — it can open the door.（我的狗很聪明，会开门。）
拓·同族词smarter / smartest比较级最高级；smartphone智能手机；同义词clever（与clever页互链）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句They are very strong, very smart and very, very big.
夸人You are so smart!
动物My dog is very smart.
衣着You look smart in the new suit.
功能句总结 · very + 形容词三连

课本句型：very strong, very smart and very, very big

夸人句：You are so smart!（so比very更有惊喜感）

比较级：The dolphin is smarter than the fish.

⚠️ 夸聪明也可以说clever（英式常用）——smart更口语化！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

smart是夸孩子的高频词，但要夸具体行为：“You solved it — so smart!”比空夸聪明更有效。课本句的三连very（strong, smart, big）可以套到任何动物身上练口语（与clever页互链，smart/clever同义辨析）。

📖 微型故事《The Smartest Answer》

🐘 动物学校考试，题目是：“Who is the smartest animal?”小猴举手：“Me! I can open boxes!”乌鸦说：“I can use tools!”这时大象Andy慢慢站起来：“Smart is not only solving problems — it's knowing when to help.”说着他用trunk把摔倒的小兔扶起来，又用tusk挑开挡路的大石头。老师批卷：“Andy's answer is the smartest — smart hands, smart heart.”大家鼓掌：聪明，也长在手和心上。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 谁的答案最smart？(Andy/大象)
• Andy用什么扶起小兔？(His trunk)
• 问问孩子：What does “smart heart” mean to you?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 义1聪明 ── a smart student · a smart answer ├── 义2光鲜 ── a smart suit（英式：衣着光鲜） ├── 课本句 ── very strong, very smart and very, very big ├── 比较 ── smarter / smartest · 同义词clever └── smart家族 ── smartphone智能手机 · smart robot智能机器人

🏛️ 词源故事

smart的原义竟然是“疼痛”——来自古英语smeart（使刺痛），如smarting pain（刺痛）。因为反应快得像被针扎了一下，它先变成“机敏、麻利”，后来才有了“聪明”。smartphone的smart也是这个“机敏”。
🎯 讲给孩子：smart是一个从“疼”进化到“聪明”的词。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“The important thing is not to stop questioning.”（重要的是不要停止提问。）——爱因斯坦1955年接受《生活》杂志采访时说的话。会提问，正是smart的第一步。

🎵 歌词里的 smart

🎵 Kelly Clarkson《Mr. Know It All》(2011)：冠军单曲，“什么都懂先生”——smart到让人想听他多说两句。

⚡ 冷知识

大象能认出镜子里的自己——这叫“镜子测试”，通过测试的动物屈指可数；章鱼有9个“脑”（1个主脑+8条腕足上的神经节）；乌鸦真的会把石子丢进瓶里喝水——伊索寓言里的桥段被实验证实了。
