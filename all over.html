Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Nancy 成长自述）
My name is Nancy. I was born on April 5th, 2004. I learned to speak at six months old. I learned to climb when I was eight months old. I climbed out of my bed and hurt my head. I learned to walk when I was one year old. One day I walked out of my home and into the street! I learned to eat with chopsticks when I was three. I got food all over my face! I learned to swim when I was four, but I only went swimming in the bath. When I was five I learned to wash clothes, but I got myself all wet! In grade four I learned to cook – I cooked eggs, but they tasted very bad!! Now I'm in grade five.

Story Time · The Ugly Duckling（丑小鸭）
Once there was a mother duck. She was sitting on seven little eggs. One by one, six little yellow ducklings came out of the eggs. ... Two days later, out came a duckling. But this duckling wasn't yellow. It was grey, and it had a long, thin neck. ... Summer came. The ducklings all grew bigger, and the ugly duckling was the biggest. ... Finally, spring came. 'Look at the swan,' she said, 'It's beautiful!' ... He was a swan, a white swan with a long neck. He wasn't ugly. He was beautiful!

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写all + over —— 两个最简单的词合体，却有“到处/遍及/结束”三副面孔
✏️ 口诀：all over 盖满地，脸上身上全是它
音·发音/ˌɔːl ˈəʊvə/ all 读 /ɔːl/；over 重读 /ˈəʊvə/
🎧 跟读：food all over my face · all over the world
意·释义① 到处、满脸满身：food all over my face 满脸都是饭；② 遍及：all over the world 全世界；③ 结束：The game is all over. 比赛结束了
用·句型① I got food all over my face! (我吃得满脸都是！——Nancy 名句)
② His books are all over the floor. (他的书扔得满地都是。)
③ The rain is all over. Let's play! (雨停了，去玩吧！)
④ I want to travel all over China. (我想游遍中国。)
拓·同族词近义 everywhere（与 everywhere 页同曲互链）；同框短语：all over the world 全世界 · all right 好的

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
到处The puppy ran all over the house.
结束Class is all over at five.
遍及We have friends all over the world.
浑身I got wet all over in the rain.
功能句总结 · all over 三副面孔

① 到处：I got food all over my face.（满脸都是）
② 遍及：all over the world（全世界）
③ 结束：The game is all over.（比赛结束了）

判断：看语境——有“盖满”的画面是到处/遍及，说活动停下是结束。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

all over 是高频小词组，建议在生活里抓现场：孩子吃完饭满脸饭粒，笑着来一句 You've got food all over your face!——正是 Nancy 的名句现场版。顺带讲清三义：到处/遍及/结束，并用 The rain is all over（雨停了）这类暖心例句收尾。

📖 微型故事《The Spaghetti Monster》

三岁的 Nancy 第一次自己吃意大利面。她挥舞叉子，面条飞上眉毛、粘上鼻子、挂到耳朵——妈妈进门一看笑弯了腰：Nancy! You've got food all over your face! Nancy 对着镜子照了照，郑重宣布：I'm a spaghetti monster! 从那天起，全家都学会了这句话——只要谁吃得满脸都是，餐桌就响起笑声和这句英文。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Nancy eat for the first time? (Spaghetti)
• What did Mum say? (You've got food all over your face!)
• 问问孩子：Who gets food all over their face in your family?

🏛️ 词源故事

all 来自古英语 eall（全部），over 来自古英语 ofer（上方）——all over 字面是“全部压在上方”，盖满每一寸，于是有了“到处”的意思。over 又能表示“翻篇”——比赛翻篇了，就是 The game is all over。一个小词组，把“盖满”和“翻篇”讲成了两句话。🎯 讲给孩子：all over 就是“全盖上”——盖在脸上是狼狈，盖在世界上是豪情。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“All the world's a stage.” 🎭
全世界是一个舞台。——莎士比亚《皆大欢喜》名句。all the world 与 all over the world 是同一份豪迈：满世界都和你有关。

🎵 歌词里的 all over

🎵 Fleetwood Mac《Everywhere》(1987)：传奇乐队黄金时期热单，副歌一声声 I wanna be with you everywhere 轻盈上口——“到处都有你”正是 all over 的浪漫版。

🎵 互链：与 everywhere 页互链（同曲《Everywhere》，all over 与 everywhere 互为近义）。

⚡ 冷知识

英语里 It's all over but the shouting（大局已定只剩欢呼）出自美国赛场解说文化，all over 表“结束”的用法正是从赛场跑出来的；而 all over the world 的豪迈版则成了地理课最爱——短语虽小，装得下操场，也装得下地球。
