📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Feelings（情绪）

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number（小狗连环画，情绪九宫格）
My dog climbs on the table and eats Lily's lunch, so I am angry.（狗偷吃午餐→生气）
He's ill, and I'm worried.（狗病了→担心）
My dog suddenly runs over to Yaoyao, so Yaoyao is scared.（狗冲向瑶瑶→害怕）
I can't find my dog. It makes me cry. → I am very sad.（找不到狗→哭→难过）
He comes back home. I am very excited.（狗回家了→激动）
My dog has a running race. He runs very fast and wins the race. I am proud.（赢赛跑→自豪）

Lesson 1 · B. Let's talk（情绪接龙）
— I am going to sing for my class.（自豪）
— My grandma is ill.（担心）
— My cousin takes away my model plane.（生气）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（老照片里的为什么）
— You look sad in this picture. Why?
— I was sad because I was ill. I had to stay in bed.
— I was happy because Andy came to see me and helped me with my maths.
— I was worried because I wasn't good at maths. I didn't like maths tests.
— Did you do well in the maths test? — Yes. Look, I got an A. I was so proud. — Congratulations!

Lesson 2 · B. Choose and talk（because 句型操练）
— You look sad. Why?
— I am sad because I am ill. I can't play with my friends. I have to stay in bed.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Andy 的日记两则）
Wednesday：I got an A on my maths test. I felt very happy. … After school we went to a singing competition. We sang together and won first place, so I felt very proud.
Friday：My grandmother was ill. She went to hospital, and I was very worried. … I played basketball with my friend Jiayi. I hurt my leg, so I was sad.

Fun Time · Good to know（颜色与情绪）
To 'see red' means to get very angry.（see red＝勃然大怒）
In English, you 'feel blue' when you are sad.（feel blue＝心情低落）
但在中国，红色是喜庆的颜色：春节穿红衣、发红包。

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写hurt —— 三个字母一气呵成；三态同形：hurt-hurt-hurt，摔多狠都不变脸
✏️ 口诀：hurt 一身犟骨头，过去式跟现在长得一模一样！
音·发音/hɜːt/ 一个音节，ur 读长音 /ɜː/
🎧 跟读：hurt my leg · my leg hurts · it hurts!
意·释义弄伤；（部位）疼 🤕（hurt my leg 弄伤了腿；My leg hurts 我腿疼；也指“伤害感情”）
用·句型① I hurt my leg, so I was sad. (我弄伤了腿，所以很难过。)
② My leg hurts. (我的腿疼。)
③ Be careful — don't hurt yourself! (小心，别伤到自己！)
④ His words hurt her feelings. (他的话伤了她的感情。)
拓·同族词hurt → injury/injure（较正式的“受伤”）；痛感家族：ache 隐痛（headache 头疼）· pain 疼痛

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
描述疼痛My leg hurts.
安全提醒Don't run down the stairs. You may get hurt.
安慰Does it still hurt?
对比hurt 弄伤（意外）/ injure 受伤（较正式）。
📋 功能句总结 · hurt 的两副面孔

hurt + 身体部位（弄伤）：I hurt my leg.（我弄伤了腿。）

部位 + hurts（部位疼）：My leg hurts.（腿疼。）

hurt oneself（伤到自己）：Be careful, or you'll hurt yourself.（小心伤到自己。）

⚠️ 三态同形 hurt-hurt-hurt；课本原句：I hurt my leg, so I was sad.

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

hurt 是安全教育的好入口。孩子磕碰后先教一句 My knee hurts!——能说出疼在哪，家长处理更快。运动前复习 Be careful! Don't hurt yourself! 结合课本周五日记：打篮球受伤而 sad，但故事最后总是“会长好”的。

📖 微型故事《篮球场上的跛脚英雄》

周五放学，小林和佳怡在球场约战三局。第二局他飞身救球，膝盖蹭在地上：“Ouch! I hurt my knee!” 佳怡扶他到场边，递上创可贴：“Don't move. Does it still hurt?” 小林龇牙咧嘴又骄傲：“A little. But our team won the third game!” 一瘸一拐回家的路上他总结：伤是比赛的邮票——贴着它，说明你来过。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What happened to Xiaolin? (He hurt his knee.)
• Who helped him? (Jiayi.)
• 问问孩子：What should you say when a friend gets hurt? (Are you OK? / Does it hurt?)

🗺️ 思维导图

hurt 弄伤
├── 变形 ── hurt → hurt → hurt（三态同形）
├── 两副面孔 ── hurt + 部位（弄伤）/ 部位 + hurts（疼）
├── 搭配 ── hurt oneself · get hurt

└── 安全 ── Be careful! Don't hurt yourself!

🏛️ 词源故事

hurt 来自古法语 hurter（撞击、碰撞），可能更早来自日耳曼语——最初说的是“撞”这个动作，撞着撞着就“伤”了。法语邻居至今还留着表亲 heurter（碰撞）。从“咚的一声”到“哎哟一声”，hurt 全程在场。
🎯 讲给孩子：hurt 的祖先是个“撞击声”——先有咚，后有疼。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Time heals all wounds.” ⏳
时间治愈一切伤口。—— 英语古谚：腿上的伤会好，心里的伤也是——都需要一点时间。

🎵 歌词里的 hurt

🎵 Johnny Cash《Hurt》（2002）：翻唱 Nine Inch Nails（1994）的封神之作——72 岁的“黑衣人”用沙哑嗓音把“痛”唱成了传奇的回望；MV 看哭全球乐迷，拿奖无数。

⚡ 冷知识
