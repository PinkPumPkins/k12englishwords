Get ready · 热身
— Do you often go to the zoo? What is your favourite animal?
— I like butterflies. They are insects. They can fly.

Lesson 1 · 课文短文（节选）
Lily and her classmates are going to see the animals in the zoo.
First, they are going to see the sharks. Sharks are fish. They can swim under the water. They are very big and like to eat small animals in the sea.
Then they want to see the snakes and the butterflies. Snakes are reptiles. They are very long and cold. Butterflies are beautiful animals. They are insects. They have six legs, and they can fly.
Next, they want to see some mammals, such as monkeys, pandas and tigers. Mammals give milk to their babies.
At last, they are going to see the penguins. Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They live on the ice. Look, how cute they are!

Lesson 1 · 分类知识（节选）
Reptiles have cold blood. They have scales. Most reptiles have four legs. All insects have six legs. Mother insects lay eggs. Most insects can fly.

Lesson 2 · 问答对话（节选）
— What kind of animals are monkeys?
— They are mammals.
— What can monkeys do?
— They can climb trees very well.
— What do monkeys like to eat?
— They like to eat peaches and bananas.

Lesson 3 · 短文 The Elephant（节选）
Elephants are mammals. They can be four metres tall. They don't eat meat. They only eat fruit, leaves and grass. They need a lot of food, so they spend most of their time eating. The elephant has two long, white teeth called tusks. It is the only animal with a trunk — a special long nose. The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut. The elephant is also the mammal with the biggest ears. They look like two big fans. Elephants live in Africa and Asia. They are very strong, very smart and of course, very, very big.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写trunk，tr-开头让人想起tree（树）。✏️口诀：trunk是树的“主干”，也是大象的“鼻子主干”
音·发音/trʌŋk/ 单音节，注意鼻音/ŋk/收尾。
🎧 跟读：the elephant's trunk · a car trunk
意·释义n. 象鼻🐘 / 树干🌳 / 后备箱·行李箱🧳——一词三义！
用·句型① It is the only animal with a trunk — a special long nose.（大象是唯一有象鼻的动物——一个特别的长鼻子。——课本原句）
② The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut.（课本原句）
③ The trunk of the tree is very thick.（树干很粗。）
④ Put the bag in the trunk.（把包放进后备箱。）
拓·同族词trunks复数；swimming trunks泳裤（又一义，永远复数！）；trunk road干道；同单元：elephant · tusk

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a peanut.
树干The trunk of the tree is very thick.
后备箱Put the bag in the trunk.
本领The elephant uses its trunk to drink water.
功能句总结 · use...to...用……做……

课本句型：The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a peanut.

仿写：I use my hands to write. / We use clocks to tell time.

一词三义：elephant's trunk象鼻 / tree trunk树干 / car trunk后备箱

⚠️ 语境定词义：看到elephant是象鼻，看到tree是树干！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

trunk一词三义是绝佳的“语境猜词”教材。玩“trunk三连拍”：指大象照片说elephant's trunk，指小区大树说tree trunk，开后备箱说car trunk。一词多义练熟后，孩子读长文章会自动切换词义（与elephant页互链）。

📖 微型故事《The Multi-tool Nose》

🐘 大象Andy的trunk是全草原最多功能的“鼻子”。小猴掉进河里，Andy用trunk把他卷上来：“My trunk is a crane!”（吊车）干旱了，Andy用trunk吸水浇花：“My trunk is a shower!”（花洒）搬家日，Andy用trunk扛起行李：“My trunk is a car trunk!”（后备箱）小猴笑问：“你的鼻子到底有几个身份？”Andy喷出一道彩虹：“One nose, many superpowers!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Andy的trunk当过哪三样工具？(吊车/花洒/后备箱)
• 把小猴卷上岸用的是哪个本领？(力气大)
• 问问孩子：If your nose could do one extra thing, what would it do?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 象鼻 ── pick up卷东西 · drink water喝水 · spray喷水 ├── 树干 ── tree trunk树的“主干” ├── 后备箱 ── car trunk放行李 ├── 另一义 ── swimming trunks泳裤（永远复数！） └── 课本句 ── use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut

🏛️ 词源故事

trunk来自拉丁语truncus（躯干、主干），14世纪进入英语指“树干”；后来人的“躯干”、大象的长鼻子、汽车的“后备箱”都因为“像主干、像大箱子”陆续借用了它。最早的行李箱叫traveling trunk（旅行大木箱）。
🎯 讲给孩子：一个词从“树干”出发，长出了好几根枝。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
trunk拉丁语truncus（主干）→ 树干/躯干/象鼻/后备箱
truncatetrunc（切短）+ -ate → 截断（数学、编程常用词）
swimming trunks泳裤（trunk此义永远用复数）
trunk road干线公路——“交通的主干”

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Great oaks from little acorns grow.”（参天橡树，生于小小橡果。）——英语谚语。每一根粗壮的tree trunk，都是从一粒种子开始长成的。

🎵 歌词里的 trunk

🎵 Henry Mancini《Baby Elephant Walk》(1961)：传奇配乐家名曲，小象摇摇摆摆，象鼻跟着节拍甩（纯音乐 已标注）。
🎵 与elephant页、tusk页同歌互链——trunk是大象身体里最忙的部分！

⚡ 冷知识

象鼻里没有骨头，却有约4万块肌肉——比整个人体的肌肉还多，既能拔起大树，又能捡起一颗小花生（课本原句说的正是这个反差）；非洲象鼻尖有两个“手指”，亚洲象只有一个；大象还会用鼻子互相缠绕打招呼。
