📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写in·ven·tor —— invent（发明）+ or（做…的人）；-or 和 -er 一样是“人”后缀
✏️ 口诀：会发明的人，叫 inventor！
音·发音/ɪnˈven.tə/ 三个音节，重音在中间 ven
🎧 跟读：a great inventor · Edison was an inventor.
意·释义发明家 🔧（发明全新东西的人；invention 是“发明物”，invent 是动词“发明”）
用·句型① He was an inventor. (他是一位发明家。)
② Edison was a great inventor. (爱迪生是伟大的发明家。)
③ Who invented the light bulb? (谁发明了电灯泡？)
④ I want to be an inventor in the future. (我将来想当发明家。)
拓·同族词invent 发明（v.）→ invention 发明（n.）→ inventor 发明家；同后缀：actor 演员 · visitor 参观者 · doctor 医生

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
职业介绍My uncle is an inventor. He has a small lab at home.
名人问答Who's that man? — He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
梦想I want to be an inventor and make a flying bike.
对比invent（发明新东西）≠ discover（发现已有的东西）：Columbus discovered America.
📋 功能句总结 · -or：做这行的人

动词 + or = 职业：invent 发明 → inventor 发明家；visit 参观 → visitor 参观者；act 表演 → actor 演员。

名人问答句：— Who's that man?（那位男士是谁？）— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.（他是爱迪生，以发明闻名。）

⚠️ be famous for + 名词：以……而闻名，for 后面接名词。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

六年级名人单元第一词。在家玩“职业猜猜猜”：家长说 invent（发明），孩子抢答 inventor（发明家）；再联想 doctor、visitor，体会 -or 后缀。顺带讲：爱迪生试了上千种材料才做出灯泡——发明家不是聪明，是不放弃。最后留个开放题：如果让你发明一样东西，你想发明什么？

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《台灯下的试验》

小宇的台灯坏了，他没哭，翻出电池和电线：“I want to invent something useful!” 他试了三次都失败，爸爸想帮忙，他摇头：“Edison failed more than me. He was a great inventor.” 第四次，灯亮了！虽然只是一盏纸盒小台灯，全家人都鼓了掌。原来发明家的秘诀，就是失败后再来一次。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Xiaoyu want to make? (A useful little lamp.)
• How many times did he fail before success? (Three times.)
• 问问孩子：如果你是发明家，你想发明什么？为什么？

🏛️ 词源故事

invent 来自拉丁语 invenire：in（走进）+ venire（来）——“走进去，就遇上了”。在古人眼里，“发明”不是无中生有，而是“遇见”好点子。invent（动词）、invention（发明物）、inventor（发明家）是一家三口。
🎯 讲给孩子：发明家的第一步，是愿意走进问题里去“遇见”答案。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
invention发明（n.）：The light bulb was a great invention.
invent发明（v.）：Edison invented the light bulb.
visitor / actor同 -or 后缀家族：参观者 / 演员
discover发现（本来就有的东西）≠ invent（造出全新的东西）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Genius is one percent inspiration and ninety-nine percent perspiration.” 💡
天才是百分之一的灵感加百分之九十九的汗水。—— 课本名人爱迪生的名言：他一生拥有上千项专利，靠的就是失败一百次后再试一次。

🎵 歌词里的 inventor

🎵 Daft Punk《Harder Better Faster Stronger》（2001）：电子乐传奇二人的洗脑神曲，“Work it harder, make it better, do it faster, makes us stronger”——一遍遍改进、升级，正是发明家的工作日常。
🔗 与 invent 页、invention 页互链：invent → invention → inventor 一家三口，唱同一首“机器进化曲”。

⚡ 冷知识

爱迪生名下有 1093 项美国专利，至今仍是高产纪录保持者之一。另一个冷知识：英语里 invent（发明）和 discover（发现）分工严格——爱迪生 invent 了电灯泡，哥伦布 discover 了美洲。下次有人说“哥伦布发明了美洲”，你可以笑着纠正他。
