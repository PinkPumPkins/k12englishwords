📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写neighbour(邻居) + hood(身份/范围后缀) → 邻居们住的范围就是街坊；美式少个 u
✏️ 口诀：邻居加 hood，圈出一块好街坊！
音·发音/ˈneɪbəhʊd/ 四音节，重音在最前
🎧 跟读：in the neighbourhood · Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
意·释义街坊、社区 🏘️（家附近的一片地方，住着你的邻居们）
用·句型① Is there a post office in the neighbourhood? (这街坊里有邮局吗？)
② I walk around the neighbourhood after dinner. (晚饭后我在街坊里走走。)
③ There are two parks in our neighbourhood. (我们社区有两个公园。)
④ My neighbourhood is quiet and nice. (我的街坊安静又宜人。)
拓·同族词neighbour 邻居 · childhood 童年（同后缀 -hood）· in the neighbourhood of 大约（引申用法）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
问答Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
描述There is a park in our neighbourhood.
日常I often play football with my neighbours.
情感I love my neighbourhood.
功能句总结 · 用 There be 介绍街坊
问：Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
答：Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
介绍：There is a park in my neighbourhood. / There are two shops in my neighbourhood.
单数 is、复数 are！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

neighbourhood 是本单元最长的词，拆成 neighbour + hood 就好记。和孩子画一张“我家街区地图”：标出家、学校、post office、bank、grocery，边画边用 There is a … in my neighbourhood 介绍，一次画出整单元词表。

📖 微型故事《Our House，我们的街坊》

🏘️ 歌里唱过 “Our house is a very very fine house”——而乐乐的街坊正是这样：街角是 grocery，对面是 bank，邮局紧邻他家。每天傍晚，邻居们在院子里聊天，乐乐帮王爷爷取报纸。妈妈说：“That's what makes a neighbourhood — people who care.”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What is next to Lele's home? (邮局)
• What did Lele do for Grandpa Wang? (取报纸)
• 问问孩子：你的街坊里最想介绍给外国朋友的地点是哪里？

🗺️ 思维导图

neighbourhood 街坊 ├── 结构 ── neighbour(邻居) + hood(范围) ├── 地点 ── post office · bank · grocery · park ├── 句型 ── There is/are … in my neighbourhood ├── 拼写 ── 英式 neighbour- / 美式 neighbor- └── 互链 ── living room 页（同曲 Our House）

🏛️ 词源故事

neighbour 来自古英语 nēahgebūr：nēah（近，near 的祖先）+ gebūr（居住者、农夫，与 boor 同源）——字面就是“住在附近的人”。hood 是“身份/范围”后缀，childhood（童年）、brotherhood（兄弟情谊）同门。街坊 = 邻居们的地盘，词义和构词严丝合缝。

📜 名言 & 谚语

非洲谚语：“It takes a village to raise a child.”（养大一个孩子，需要整个村子。）街坊正是城市里的“村子”——每个孩子的成长，都有邻居们的一份力。

🎵 歌词里的 neighbourhood

🎵 Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young《Our House》(1970)：Graham Nash 写给与 Joni Mitchell 同住的温暖小屋，“Our house is a very very fine house” 传唱至今。与 living room 页同曲互链；本单元和 post office 页互链（邮局就在街坊里）。

⚡ 冷知识

美国有 Neighborhood Watch（邻里守望）计划：邻居们轮流留意社区安全，门口贴一块小牌子，1972年开始推行——“街坊”这个词，真的能保护街坊。
