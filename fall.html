📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）

— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写fall —— 双写 l；all、ball、tall 一家人
✏️ 口诀：f + all = fall，苹果从树上 fall！
音·发音/fɔːl/ 单音节；别和 full /fʊl/ 弄混（口型更大）
🎧 跟读：fall from the tree · fall down · in the fall 秋天
意·释义落下；秋天 🍂（动词：落、摔倒；名词：落下 + 美式“秋天”= autumn）
用·句型① He saw an apple fall from the tree. (他看见一个苹果从树上落下。)
② Be careful! Don't fall down. (小心！别摔倒。)
③ Leaves fall in the fall. (秋天树叶落。)
④ The leaves fall off the tree. (叶子从树上掉下来。)
拓·同族词fall-fell-fallen 三态；fall down 摔倒 / fall off 掉下 / fall behind 落后；fall（美）= autumn（英）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句He saw an apple fall from the tree.
安全Be careful on the stairs. Don't fall!

📋 功能句总结 · fall 家族短语

fall down：摔倒——Be careful, or you'll fall down.

fall off：从…掉下——The apple fell off the tree.

fall behind：落后——Study hard, or you'll fall behind.

看见…做：see sb do（看到全过程）：He saw an apple fall.

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

牛顿的苹果是 Lesson 3 的收官画面，fall 是主角。三件事：1) 看 see sb do sth 结构——He saw an apple fall（看见落下的全过程）；2) 秋天义：英国人说 autumn，美国人说 fall（落叶之时），同一个季节两个名字；3) 安全教育顺带做：楼梯、滑梯上“慢慢走，不 fall”。周末捡几片落叶，用英语说 Leaves fall in the fall.

📖 微型故事《苹果树下的下午》

周末去果园，小果在树下看书。“咚！”一个苹果砸在草地上。小果捡起来问：“Why do apples fall down, not up?” 爸爸说：“三百多年前，有个叫牛顿的人也在树下问了同样的问题。He saw an apple fall from the tree—and found out why.” 小果咬一口苹果：“So I'm eating a science question?” 全家笑作一团。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What fell from the tree? (An apple.)
• Who asked the same question long ago? (Newton.)
• 问问孩子：如果苹果飘上天，世界会变成什么样？

🏛️ 词源故事

fall 来自古英语 feallan，是英语最古老的动词之一。它当“秋天”讲是美国用法：来自 fall of the leaf（落叶之时）——17 世纪的英语两种说法都用，后来英国留下 autumn（来自法语），美国把 fall 留了下来。一片叶子，在大西洋两岸落出了两个名字。
🎯 讲给孩子：fall 的“秋天”义，就是把“落叶”说快了。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Pride goes before a fall.” 🍂
骄兵必败。—— 出自《圣经·箴言》16:18 的古谚：骄傲走在跌倒前面——牛顿看到苹果 fall，看到的是真理；骄傲的人 fall，看到的是地板。

🎵 歌词里的 fall

🎵 Alicia Keys《Fallin'》（2001）：格莱美年度歌曲，八周 Billboard 冠军——“I keep on fallin'”，fallin' 是 fall 的进行式缩写，/fɔːl/ 的发音跟着钢琴一遍就会。
🔗 与课本牛顿段呼应：牛顿看到苹果 fall；季节义与 autumn 同义互链。

⚡ 冷知识

英国人说 autumn，美国人说 fall——fall 来自“落叶之时”（fall of the leaf），17 世纪后英国改口 autumn，美国保留了 fall。牛顿老家的那棵苹果树（伍尔索普庄园）至今还活着，它的“后代”被嫁接种植到世界各地的大学和植物园——一棵 350 多岁的“科学之树”。
