Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and chant
Where is my grandmother? Where is she?
She is in the bedroom. She is watering the plants.
Where is my grandfather? Where is he?
He is in the living room. He is watching TV.
Where is my mother? Where is she?
She is in the kitchen. She is cooking for me.
Where is my father? Where is he?
He is in the study. He is reading a book.
Where is my sister? Where is she?
She is in the dining room. She is drinking juice.
Where is my brother? Where is he?
He is in the bathroom. He is washing his hands.
（本课单词：home · bedroom · living room · bathroom · dining room · study · kitchen）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and repeat
— Where is your aunt?
— She's in the kitchen.
— What's she doing?

Lesson 3 · A. Read and choose（租房小广告）
Linda's family wants to rent a new house.
【For Rent】
There are two bedrooms. One is big and one is small.
There is a kitchen, a dining room and a bathroom.

Fun Time
A fish's home is in the sea.
A bird's home is in a tree.
A duck's home is in a lake.
A tiger's home is in the forest.

Story Time（小猫 Trouble 捣蛋记）
On Monday, Trouble is in the bathroom. Oh, no!
On Wednesday, Trouble is in the kitchen. He makes a mess!
On Saturday, Trouble is in my bedroom. Oh, dear! My clothes!
Now you know why I call my cat “Trouble”!

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写home —— h-o-m-e，四字母短词；结尾的 e 不发音，却让 o 读长音 /əʊ/（魔法e）✏️ 口诀：h-o-m-e，家在我心窝
音·发音/həʊm/ 单音节，o 读长音 /əʊ/（美音 /oʊ/）；🎧 跟读：my home · go home · at home · East or west, home is best.
意·释义家 🏠（指“家的地方”多不可数：be at home；指“某人的家宅”可数：a beautiful home）；课本 Fun Time 里还能指动物的家：a fish's home 鱼的家
用·句型① This is my home. (这是我的家。)
② Welcome to my home! (欢迎来我家！)
③ I go home at five. (我五点回家。)
④ A fish's home is in the sea. (鱼的家在大海。)
拓·同族词go home 回家（go home 中间不加 to！）· at home 在家 · homework 家庭作业（home+work）· hometown 家乡；同单元房间词：bedroom / living room / kitchen

📋 功能句总结 · Where is...? 问位置

问：Where is my mother?（我妈妈在哪里？）

答：She is in the kitchen.（她在厨房。）

追问动作：What's she doing? — She's cooking.

⚠️ go home / come home 中间不加 to：go home ✔ / go to home ✘！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

三年级下册 Unit 6 是“我的家”主题单元。建议从孩子最熟悉的家开始：进家门说 We're home!，指认房间说 This is the bedroom / living room...；再玩“找家人”游戏：家长问 Where is Dad?，孩子找一找答 He's in the study.——把房间词和方位问答一次练熟。

📖 微型故事《Where Is My Home?》

📖 小鸭子跟妈妈出游，贪看蝴蝶走丢了。他着急地问：“Where is my home?” 小鱼冒泡说：“Your home is by the lake.” 小鸟说：“Follow the river!” 夕阳下，鸭妈妈正张着翅膀等他：“There you are! We're home!” 小鸭子扑进妈妈怀里：“Home is where Mum is.”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who got lost? (The little duck)
• Where is the duck's home? (By the lake)
• 问问孩子：你的 home 在哪里？用 This is my home. 介绍一句

🏛️ 词源故事

home 来自古英语 hām（村庄、住所），和 hamlet（小村庄）、德语 Heim（家）同宗。英语里 home 和 house 有分工：house 指“房子”这座建筑，home 是带着感情的家——所以 This is my home 比 This is my house 温暖得多。homework、hometown 都是用 home 拼出来的复合词。
🎯 讲给孩子：house 是砖瓦，home 是爱。

📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
go home回家（不加 to：go home ✔ / go to home ✘）
homework家庭作业（home+work）
hometown家乡（home+town）
at home在家；像在自己家一样自在（feel at home）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“East or west, home is best.” 东好西好，还是家最好——英语世界流传最广的思家谚语。作家罗伯特·弗罗斯特也写过：“Home is the place where, when you have to go there, they have to take you in.”（家就是当你不得不回去时，他们会收留你的地方。）

🎵 歌词里的 home

🎵 Michael Bublé《Home》(2005)：加拿大爵士歌王的代表作，“Let me go home, I'm just too far from where you are” 唱出所有游子的心声——home 的长音 /həʊm/ 在副歌里一遍遍回荡。
🎵 互链：与 go home 页互链（同曲同源《Home》）；与 stayed at home 页互链（《Sweet Home Alabama》歌名里也有 home）。

⚡ 冷知识

英语里 home 和 house 分工严格：house 是冷冰冰的建筑，home 是有温度的家——房地产广告卖的是 house，远行的人想的是 home。手机的主页叫 home page，球队主场叫 home game，手机上那颗按钮就叫 HOME 键——home 早已住进每个人的口袋。
