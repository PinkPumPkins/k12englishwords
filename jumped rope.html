📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写jump + ed —— jump 以 p 结尾，过去式直接加 ed（ed 读 /t/）
✏️ 口诀：jump 落地加 ed，jumped rope 跳得欢
音·发音/dʒʌmpt/ 词尾 /mpt/ 三连击，先鼻音后爆破
🎧 跟读：jumped rope · jumped high · jump rope
意·释义跳绳（过去发生）。rope 绳子；跳绳是一项经典运动
用·句型① I jumped rope in the park. (我在公园跳绳了。)
② She jumped rope 100 times! (她跳了100下！)
③ Let's jump rope together.
④ Did you jump rope after school?
拓·同族词jump → jumped → jumping（与 jumping rope 页同源互链）
英式：skipping rope · 边跳边数：one, two, three...

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
过去式I jumped rope for ten minutes.
运动I often jump rope after school.
比赛We had a jump rope game. I jumped 100 times!
询问Did you jump rope yesterday?
功能句总结 · 运动与计数

计数：I jumped rope fifty times!（我跳了五十下！）
鼓励：Keep jumping! You can do it!（继续跳！你可以！）
轮换：It's my turn to jump.（轮到我跳了。）

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

跳绳时全家报数：one, two, three...，跳完总结 I jumped rope ___ times! 数字和过去式一次练成。拼写上 jump 加 ed 干脆利落，让孩子对比 climbed 里的哑巴 b——过去式都加 ed，原词却各有脾气。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 听音起跳
家长说 jump，孩子原地跳；说 jumped，孩子落地定格喊 Done!
✏️ 关卡2 · 边跳边拼
每跳一下说一个字母，拼出 j-u-m-p-e-d，跳完单词刚好落地
✅ 关卡3 · 过去式填空
① Yesterday I ___ (jump) rope. → jumped
② Look, he is ___ rope. → jumping
🔍 关卡4 · 计数挑战
跳绳30秒，用英语报数 I jumped ___ times! 破纪录再战，得一颗星

📖 微型故事《One Hundred Jumps》

体育课比赛跳绳，Leo 才跳了二十下就绊住了。朋友说：Rope skipping is easy for me. Leo 不服气，每天放学练十分钟。一周后他一口气跳到一百下，全班鼓掌。Leo 擦着汗喊：I jumped one hundred times! 老师说：That's the power of practice. 原来绳子不会辜负坚持的人。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• How many times did Leo jump at first? (Twenty)
• What did Leo do every day? (He practiced for ten minutes)
• 问问孩子：What can you practice every day?

🏛️ 词源故事

jump 像个“跳出来”的新词：16世纪才在英语里冒头，词源学家至今说不清它的来历，猜是模仿起跳动静的拟声词（和 bump、thump 一个“声响家族”）。rope 来自古英语 rāp，就是绳子。🎯 讲给孩子：jump 是个没有“老家”的词——像跳绳一样，说跳就跳出来了。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义

jump in跳入；插话（引申义）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Practice makes perfect.” 🔁
熟能生巧。——英语谚语。从二十下到一百下，Leo 靠的不是天赋，是每天十分钟。

🎵 歌词里的 jumped rope

🎵 Van Halen《Jump》(1984)：Billboard 冠军，合成器前奏一响全场起跳——史上最著名的“起跳”指令之一。

🎵 与 jumping rope 页互链（同源曲目）；jumped 的每个音节都带着落地的顿挫感。

⚡ 冷知识

跳绳曾经是“男孩的运动”：19世纪末的美国，跳绳多由男孩进行；20世纪初进入学校体操课后，女孩也加入了，如今跳绳已是中国和许多国家学校的常规运动，还有正式的世界跳绳比赛。
