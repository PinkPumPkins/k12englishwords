📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写bank 只有4个字母：b-a-n-k，an 后面别忘了 n
✏️ 口诀：四个字母一座房，b 开头的存钱庄！
音·发音/bæŋk/ 单音节，n 和 k 之间有个后鼻音 ŋ
🎧 跟读：the bank · get some money from the bank
意·释义银行 🏦（存钱取钱的地方）；还有“河岸”的意思：the bank of a river
用·句型① Where is the bank? (银行在哪儿？)
② It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park. (在超市和星期日公园之间。)
③ Get some money from the bank. (从银行取些钱。)
④ There is a bank next to the school. (学校旁边有家银行。)
拓·同族词一词两义：bank 银行 / 河岸；piggy bank 存钱罐 · blood bank 血库；money 钱（本单元词，互链）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

答1：It's between the supermarket and the park.
答2：It's next to the restaurant. / It's across from the school.
where 问地点，between / next to / across from 全都能接！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

bank 一词两义（银行/河岸）是本页重点。带孩子读课本句 Where is the bank? — It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.，再把 piggy bank 存钱罐拿出来讲 bank 的“长凳”故事，孩子对钱的“家”会更有概念。

🏛️ 词源故事

bank 来自意大利语 banca（长凳）：中世纪的意大利钱商坐在长凳上摆开硬币做生意，“长凳”渐渐成了“银行”。更有趣的是 bankrupt（破产）一般认为也来自 banca rotta——“断了的长凳”：钱商赔了本，凳子被砸断。一条长凳上，坐着银行和破产两个词。

📜 名言 & 谚语

美国喜剧大师 Bob Hope 有句广为流传的银行段子：“A bank is a place that will lend you money if you can prove that you don't need it.”（银行就是：只有你证明你不需要钱，它才肯借钱给你的地方。）

🎵 歌词里的 bank

🎵 21 Savage《Bank Account》(2017)：hook 反复唱 “check my bank account”，说唱里最有名的“查账户”。与 money 页（Pink Floyd《Money》）互链，风格不同但都和钱有关。

⚡ 冷知识

存钱罐为什么长成猪的样子？流传最广的说法：古代一种做罐子的橙黄色陶土叫 pygg，工匠后来把 pygg 误当成 pig（猪），干脆把储钱罐捏成了小猪——这个解释很有趣，但词源学界并没有定论。
