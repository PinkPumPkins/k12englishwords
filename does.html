Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and chant
— He is big and he is tall. He is handsome, too. He's a great boy. I'm great, too.
— She is short and she is thin. She's pretty, too. She's a nice girl. I'm nice, too.
（本课单词：big 大的 · tall 高的 · pretty 漂亮的 · thin 瘦的 · short 矮的 · handsome 英俊的）

Lesson 1 · B. Let's play
— He is a boy. He is handsome.
— She is a girl. She is tall.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and repeat
— Dad, I have a new classmate. — Boy or girl? — A boy.
— What does he look like? — He is tall.
B. Guess and say
— I have a friend. She is a girl. — What does she look like? — She is tall and pretty. — She is Yaoyao.

Lesson 3 · A. Draw and say
This is my friend. His name is Steven. He is tall and big. He is handsome, too.
B. Listen and repeat（字母音）
i for ice cream · j for juice · k for kite · l for light

Story Time
— I'm hungry. — Oh, delicious... — This is for you. — Grrr!
— Elephant, help! There is a monster! — What does he look like? — He is big. He is tall.
— Look! He is Monkey! — I'm sorry. — That's OK. We are friends.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写d-o-e-s —— do 加 es，e 是“三单通行证”
✏️ 口诀：he/she/it 一来，do 就披上 es 战甲
音·发音/dʌz/ 单音节，读“达兹”，不读“多斯”！
🎧 跟读：What does he look like? · What does she like? · Yes, he does.
意·释义do 的第三人称单数（助动词：本身没实义，帮问句“搭架子”）
用·句型① What does he look like? — He is tall. (他长什么样？)
② What does she like? (她喜欢什么？)
③ Does he like apples? — Yes, he does. (他喜欢苹果吗？——是的。)
④ He doesn't like rain. (他不喜欢下雨。)
拓·同族词全家福：do（我/你/我们/他们用）→ does（他/她/它用）→ did（过去式）
否定：doesn't = does not；答句呼应：Does he...? — Yes, he does.

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
问外貌What does he look like? — He is tall.
问喜好What does she like? — She likes cats.
一般疑问Does he like dogs? — Yes, he does.
否定He doesn't like rain.
🧩 功能句 · does 的三单问句

主语是 he/she/it 时，问句要用 does：
What does he look like? (他长什么样？)
What does she like? (她喜欢什么？)

一般疑问与回答：Does he run fast? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

💡 口诀：三单主语 does 上场，后面动词要“脱衣”变原形（Does he like...? 不能说 Does he likes...!）

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

does 是“语法小螺丝钉”：主语是第三人称单数（he/she/it）时，问句和否定句都要请它出场，而且它后面的动词要变回原形（Does he like...? 不能说 Does he likes...）。家里操练法：指着家人问 What does he like? 让孩子答 He likes ...；再练否定 He doesn't like ...。孩子用对一次 does，就实名表扬一次。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · do/does 分拣
家长说主语，孩子喊 do 或 does：I→do · you→do · he→does · she→does · it→does，连对5次通关
✏️ 关卡2 · 三单变身
like→likes · run→runs · do→does，再练还原：likes→like，变身+还原全对得星

📖 微型故事《What Does the Monster Like?》

森林里传说有只怪兽。小兔问猫头鹰：“What does it look like?” “It is big.” 小兔又问：“What does it like?” “It likes... cakes!” 小兔壮着胆子带了一块蛋糕去。怪兽闻香而出：“Cake! My favourite!” 原来怪兽不是怪物，是一只爱吃蛋糕的大熊。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 小兔问了猫头鹰哪两个问题？(What does it look like? / What does it like?)
• “My favourite!” 是什么意思？(我的最爱！)
• 问问孩子：用 What does your dad like? 问妈妈，再转述 He likes ...

🏛️ 词源故事

does 是 do 的三单马甲。do 来自古英语 dōn，和德语 tun 是亲兄弟。英语的“三单税”规定：主语是 he/she/it 时动词一般加 s；而 do 以 o 结尾，要加 es 变 does，读音也从 /duː/ 整个变成 /dʌz/。否定式 doesn't = does not，读 /ˈdʌznt/。🎯 讲给孩子：do 全家福——do、does、did，主语是谁，谁的马甲就出场。

⚡ 冷知识

does 读 /dʌz/（达兹），不读“多斯”。它只在主语是 he/she/it 时出场，帮问句和否定句“搭架子”：What does he look like? / He doesn't like rain. 回答一般疑问句要呼应：Does he like cats? — Yes, he does. 三单加 s 是英语最基础的“税”，do 交得最特别：加 es，读音整个变。神曲《What Does the Fox Say?》把这道语法题唱成了全球热梗。
