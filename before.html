📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写before = be(在) + fore(前) → 在前面；forehead 额头同族
✏️ 口诀：be + fore 站在前，时间地点都能管！
音·发音/bɪˈfɔː/ 重音在后，美式读 /bɪˈfɔːr/
🎧 跟读：before 4:30 · before dinner
意·释义在…之前 ⏰（时间：before 4:30；连词：before you go）
用·句型① Get to the post office before 4:30. (四点半前到达邮局。)
② Wash your hands before dinner. (晚饭前洗手。)
③ Look both ways before you cross. (过马路前两边看看。)
④ Turn off the lights before you leave. (离开前关灯。)
拓·同族词反义 after；同族 forehead 前额 · forecast 预报；before long 不久之后

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本Get to the post office before 4:30.
时间Wash your hands before dinner.
安全Look before you cross the road.
引申I'll be back before long.
功能句总结 · before 的三顶帽子
① 介词：before 4:30 / before dinner（+名词）
② 连词：before you go（+句子）
③ 副词：I've seen this film before.（以前看过）
一顶帽子一个身份，都能表示“在前”。
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

before 出现频率极高，日常就能天天用：饭前 before dinner、睡前 before bed、出门前 before you go。把安全口诀教给孩子：Look before you cross the road（过马路前先看），单词和安全意识一起输入。

📖 微型故事《先看后走的过马路口诀》

🚸 小狐狸第一天自己上学，路口的猫头鹰警察教他口诀：“Look before you cross!” 小狐狸先看左、再看右，确认没有车才走。猫头鹰点头：“Good! Rules before fun——安全永远 before 好玩。” 小狐狸把口诀教给了全班同学。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who taught the fox the rule? (猫头鹰警察)
• What's the rule? (Look before you cross)
• 问问孩子：你过马路前的“三步口诀”是什么？

🗺️ 思维导图

before 之前 ├── 时间 ── before 4:30 · before dinner ├── 连词 ── before you go / before you cross ├── 副词 ── I've heard it before. ├── 反义 ── after；同族 forehead · forecast └── 互链 ── get to 页 · leave 页

🏛️ 词源故事

before 就是 be（在）+ fore（前面）：fore 是个古老的“前方”词根，forecast（预报）=提前算，forehead（前额）=头的前部。before 管时间也管位置，“在前面”两个字包圆了。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
beforebe(在) + fore(前) → 在…之前
forecastfore(提前) + cast(算) → 提前算出来的“预报”
foreheadfore(前) + head(头) → 前额

📜 名言 & 谚语

英语谚语：“Look before you leap.”（跳之前先看一眼——三思而后行。）它和过马路口诀 Look before you cross 是同一个句式，把 before 用活了。

🎵 歌词里的 before

🎵 Lewis Capaldi《Before You Go》(2019年末发行、2020年席卷全球)：苏格兰深情嗓的全球热单，唱的是“如果早一点说出来该多好”的遗憾，歌名本身就是一句 before。与 leave 页（Silk Sonic《Leave the Door Open》）互链。

⚡ 冷知识

英语年份缩写 BC = Before Christ（公元前）：秦统一六国是 221 BC，也就是“公元前的221年”。另一个缩写 AD 来自拉丁语 Anno Domini（公元），注意 AD 写在年份前面——A.D. 2026。
