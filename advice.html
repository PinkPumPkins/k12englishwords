📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

✅ 原文经教习网英语朗读宝全课时跟读页、电子课本与同步练习资源多源交叉核对，智慧山确认单元目录。advice 是 Lesson 3 的药方袋：琳达写道 I have some advice for you. First... Next... Finally...——本单元所有 should/shouldn't 的句子，都是 advice 的内容。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写advice（名词）和 advise（动词）只差一个字母：c 是名词，s 是动词！
✏️ 口诀：c 像盒子装建议（名词），s 像出声的嘴（动词）。
音·发音/ədˈvaɪs/ 名词结尾 /s/；动词 advise /ədˈvaɪz/ 结尾 /z/
🎧 跟读：some advice · a piece of advice · advice for you
意·释义建议、忠告（不可数名词！不能说 an advice）
用·句型① I have some advice for you. (我给你一些建议。)
② Let me give you a piece of advice. (让我给你一条建议。)
③ You should follow the doctor's advice. (你应该听医生的建议。)
④ Good advice is worth listening to. (好建议值得听。)
拓·同族词量词妙招：一条建议 = a piece of advice；两条 = two pieces of advice

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句I have some advice for you.
量词a piece of advice 一条建议
听从Follow your mum's advice.
拼写advice（n.）/ advise（v.）
📋 功能句总结 · 给建议三件套（本单元功能句汇总）
总起：I have some advice for you.
分条：First... Next... Finally...（课本琳达的信）
内容：You should... / You shouldn't...
⚠️ advice 不可数：不说 an advice / advices，要说 a piece of advice / two pieces of advice！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

advice 的不可数是本页唯一考试点。在家玩建议信游戏：孩子给家人写一句建议（Please exercise every day!），再学课本琳达用 First / Next / Finally 把三条建议连成小作文。口头纠错：谁说 an advice 就罚说 a piece of advice 三遍。

📖 微型故事《一张小纸条》

💫 小北考试没考好，把卷子揉成一团。同桌默默递来一张小纸条：One piece of advice: sleep early, exercise, then try again. 小北展开纸条读了一遍又一遍。一个月后，小北把新卷子和小纸条钉在一起，纸条上多了一行字：The best advice is the one you follow.——最好的建议，是听进去的那一条。小北把纸条贴在书桌前，一贴就是一学期。📝

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What was the advice on the note? (Sleep early, exercise, try again)
• What did Xiaobei write at last? (The best advice is the one you follow)
• 问问孩子：给你身边的人写一条 a piece of advice，念出来录音

🏛️ 词源故事

advice 走了一条“看”的路：来自古法语 a vis，字面是“依我看”（vis = 看，和 vision、visit 同根）。“照我的看法”说多了，就变成忠告。同根词全家都是看：visible 看得见、video 视频、vision 视力。
🎯 讲给孩子：advice 的祖先说“依我看”——每条建议，都是别人眼中的风景。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
advice（名词）建议：some advice / a piece of advice
advise（动词）建议做：The doctor advises rest.
follow one's advice听从建议：Follow the doctor's advice.
advice on...关于…的建议：advice on health

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Advice is seldom welcome.” 🤝 忠言总是不那么受欢迎。—— 16世纪英语古谚（流传至今）：良药苦口，古今中外一样；但课本里 Worried 主动写信求助——主动要来的 advice，最容易被听进去。

🎵 歌词里的 advice

🎵 Baz Luhrmann《Everybody's Free (To Wear Sunscreen)》(1999)：毕业演讲式金曲，整首歌从头到尾都是 advice——Wear sunscreen（涂防晒霜）是史上最出名的一条健康建议，和本单元主题无缝对接。
🔗 一首歌 = 一整张 advice 清单，听一遍胜读十句 should。

⚡ 冷知识

课本里琳达的 advice 讲究结构：First（第一）、Next（其次）、Finally（最后）——英语作文的三段式建议模板。写信人署名 Worried（担忧的人），回信人 Linda。有趣的是英语信件文化：给陌生人写信求助，署名常用形容词（Worried、A Student），回信人一看就懂——这也是英语课里最早学到的笔友信格式。
