Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and chant
Who is she? Who is he? She is my mother. He is my father. And they love me.
Who is she? Who is he? She is my aunt. He is my uncle. And they love me.
Who are we? We are together. We are a family.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and repeat（课本 P41）
— Is that man your father?
— No, he isn't. He's my uncle. That tall man is my father.
— Really? He is handsome.
— Is she your aunt?
— Yes, she is. She's beautiful.

Lesson 3 · A. Read and tick（失物招领小故事）
Who lost the water bottle?
— Good morning, Mrs Chen. Can you help me?
— Sure, Bill. A boy lost this water bottle…
— What does he look like?
— He has short hair and big eyes.
— OK. I see. It's Li Ming's water bottle.
— Thank you very much. — You're welcome.

Story Time（小蝌蚪找妈妈）
— Excuse me. Are you our mother?
— She's green. She has big eyes. She can swim.

📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写fam·i·ly —— 三音节，重音在前；口语常“吃掉”中间音节读成 /ˈfæmli/，但拼写一个字母都不能少！✏️ 口诀：Father And Mother, I Love You —— 每个词的首字母连起来正是 FAMILY
音·发音/ˈfæm.ə.li/ 三音节，注意 a 读 /æ/ 不读 /eɪ/；🎧 跟读：a happy family · family photo · family tree
意·释义家庭；家人 🏠（生物学里还指“科”：a family of sheep 一个绵羊家族——课本 Fun Time 板块就有！）
用·句型① We are a family. (我们是一家人。)
② This is my family photo. (这是我的全家福。)
③ There are four people in my family. (我家有四口人。)
④ I love my family. (我爱我的家。)
拓·同族词family → families（复数，y 变 i 加 es）；family tree 家谱/家庭树 · family name 姓氏；同单元亲戚词：uncle / aunt / cousin

📋 功能句总结 · 介绍家人

问数量：How many people are there in your family? — There are four.

指认成员：Who is she? — She is my mother.
Who is he? — He is my father.

⚠️ people 本身就是复数，不说 two peoples！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

三年级下册 Unit 4 是“家庭与亲戚”主题单元。建议在家拿全家福做“家庭英语指认游戏”：指着照片问 Who is he/she?，让孩子用 He is/She is my... 回答；再扩展问 Is she your aunt?，练 Yes, she is. / No, she isn't. 全家福就是最好的英语教具。

👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《A Photo of My Family》

📖 作业是带一张全家福。Amy 举起照片：“This is my family.” 她指着高个子说：“My father is tall.” 又指着长头发说：“My mother has long hair.” 最后拍拍自己的小脑袋：“And this is me!” 同桌 Tom 问：“How many people?” Amy 伸出四根手指：“Four! We are a happy family!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• How many people are there in Amy's family? (Four)
• Who is tall in the photo? (Amy's father)
• 拿出你家全家福，用 This is my... 介绍两个人

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 成员 ── father · mother · me · uncle · aunt · cousin
├── 数量 ── How many people...? — There are four.
├── 照片 ── family photo 全家福 · family album 相册
├── 引申 ── family tree 家庭树 · family name 姓氏
└── 情感 ── I love my family. / We are a family.

🏛️ 词源故事

family 来自拉丁语 familia，最初指“住在同一屋檐下的一家人（连仆人一起算）”，词根 famulus 意为“仆人”——千年前“家人”和“同住的人”几乎是一回事，后来词义才收窄成“有血缘或婚姻关系的家”。
🎯 讲给孩子：一词两读——书面语 /ˈfæməli/，口语快读 /ˈfæmli/，都对。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Blood is thicker than water.” 血浓于水——英语谚语，提醒我们亲人之间的情谊最深厚。中文里也有对应的“家和万事兴”。

🎵 歌词里的 family

🎵 Mary J. Blige《Family Affair》(2001)：Billboard 六周冠军金曲（Dr. Dre 制作），“Family affair” 直译“一家人的事”，鼓点一响就能记住 family 的拼读。
🎵 彩蛋：备选曲 Sister Sledge《We Are Family》(1979) 在 aunt 页同源互链——四姐妹用一首歌唱出“我们是一家人”！

⚡ 冷知识

英语里 family 作主语有“整体/成员”两读：指整体用 is（My family is big），强调每个成员用 are（My family are all here）——这是初中常考点。另外，家谱英文叫 family tree（家庭树），因为家族像树一样开枝散叶，试着画一画你家的 family tree 吧！
