Lesson 2 · B. Do a survey

Lesson 3 · A. Read and match（短文）
I want to do many jobs in the future.
On Mondays, I want to be a police officer. That must be a cool job.
On Tuesdays, I want to be a doctor. I can help sick people.
On Wednesdays and Thursdays, I want to be a teacher. I can teach English.
I want to be a farmer on Fridays.
On the weekend, I just want to be myself. I want to play from morning to evening. That sounds fun!

Let's Spell
ar: arm, bar · or: horn, pork · er: driver, tiger, turtle · ur: fur, dirt, bird

Let's Check · A. 听力（人物自述）
Hi, I'm Amenda. I want to be a police officer in the future. It's a cool job.
Hello, I'm Van. I like to eat. I want to be a cook in the future. Then I can eat any time.
Hello, I'm Alex. I want to be a farmer in the future. It's an interesting job.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写in + the + future 三词固定搭配，the 别丢
✏️ 口诀：在未来 in the future，中间 the 是桥
音·发音/ɪn ðə ˈfjuːtʃə(r)/ 重音落在 future
🎧 跟读：in the future · in the near future 不久的将来
意·释义将来、未来 🚀（指从现在往后的时间；future 也可单独作名词：未来）
用·句型① What do you want to be in the future? (课文句)
② I want to be a doctor in the future. (课文句)
③ In the future, cars may fly. (未来汽车也许会飞。)
④ No one knows the future. (未来无人知晓。)
拓·同族词in the near future 不久的将来 · future jobs 未来职业 · 反义 in the past

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课文问What do you want to be in the future?
课文答I want to be a police officer in the future.
畅想In the future, robots will help us.
哲理We can't change the past, but we can build the future.
📋 功能句总结 · 未来愿望句型（全单元主线）
问：What do you want to be in the future?
答：I want to be a/an + 职业.
理由：Because...
展开：I want to do many jobs in the future.（Lesson 3 短文开篇句）
⚠️ in the future 通常放句尾，也可放句首加逗号
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

in the future 是本单元的“万能钥匙”——对话、短文、歌曲、听力全靠它开头。建议家长每周问一次 What do you want to be in the future? 答案可以天天变：今天 cook、明天 police officer。孩子在反复回答里，自然背下完整问句。注意纠正丢 the 的错误：in future 这个说法英式里虽有，小学阶段统一记 in the future。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《写给十年后的我》

📬 老师让大家给未来的自己写信。小梅提笔：Hello! I'm you in the past — 不对，you are me in the future! 她认真地写：What do you want to be? I hope you are a doctor in the future, because I want to help sick people now. 她把信塞进铁盒：十年后打开！同桌问：What if you change your mind? 小梅笑：Then the future me will write back! ✉️

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who is the letter for? (The future her)
• What does she want to be? (A doctor)
• 问问孩子：What do you want to write to your future self?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 愿望 ── I want to be... in the future.
├── 提问 ── What do you want to be?
├── 时间 ── in the near future 不久的将来
├── 反义 ── in the past 在过去
└── 畅想 ── In the future, cars may fly.

🏛️ 词源故事

future 来自拉丁语 futurus，是动词 esse（是、存在）的将来分词——字面意思“将要存在的”。英语没法预知未来，只好用“将要存在的东西”命名它。中文的“未来”更妙：还没来到的地方。🎯 讲给孩子：future 在拉丁语里就是“将要来”——所以 in the future 装满了还没发生、但值得期待的事。

📜 名言 & 谚语

"The best way to predict the future is to invent it." 💡
预测未来的最好方式，就是创造它。—— 计算机科学家艾伦·凯（Alan Kay）1971年的名言。孩子们写下的每个愿望，都是正在发明中的未来。

🎵 歌词里的 in the future

🎵 Timbuk 3《The Future's So Bright, I Gotta Wear Shades》(1986)：Billboard 前二十；未来亮得必须戴墨镜——in the future 最摇滚的一次出场。

⚡ 冷知识

时光胶囊（time capsule）是写给未来的信件工程：1939年纽约世博会，西屋公司埋下著名的“西屋时光胶囊”，定于6939年开启——整整五千年后！里面放着日常用品和微缩胶片，是人类留给 in the future 最浪漫的礼物。你的愿望铁盒，就是一个迷你 time capsule。
