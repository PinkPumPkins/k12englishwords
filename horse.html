Lesson 1 · B. Let's play
— We're going to the zoo. What animals do you want to see? Why?
— I want to see tigers. They're big.
— I want to see pandas. They're cute.
— I want to see elephants. They're big and tall. I want to see their long noses.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and answer
— Hi, Linda. What are you doing?
— I'm drawing a picture of my favourite animal. It's a big, grey animal.
— Yes, my favourite animal is the elephant.
— What's your favourite animal? — Guess!
— Wow, that's a beautiful picture! Let me see. Mmm, your favourite animal is the horse?
— No. Look. My favourite animal is the bee. — It's lovely!

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（节选）
Different children love different animals.
Bob likes elephants. An elephant eats leaves and grass. It lives on the grassland or in the forest. It is big and strong. It has two long teeth called tusks and a big nose called a trunk.
Amanda loves pandas. A panda lives in a bamboo forest and eats bamboo there. It's black and white.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写hor·se 注意词尾不发音的 e！不要写成 hors
✏️ 口诀：马尾巴悄悄藏起来（e 不发音）
音·发音/hɔːs/ 长音 /ɔː/，嘴巴收圆
🎧 跟读：ride a horse · a white horse · horses
意·释义马 🐴
用·句型① Your favourite animal is the horse? (你最喜欢的动物是马吗？——课本原句)
② Horses can run fast. (马跑得快。)
③ I want to ride a horse. (我想骑马。)
④ A horse lives on the farm. (马住在农场。)
拓·同族词pony 小马 · foal 马驹 · cowboy 牛仔（骑马放牛的人）；习语 dark horse 黑马

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
能力A horse can run very fast.
动作I want to ride a horse.
外形The horse is tall and strong.
家园Horses live on the farm.
功能句总结 · can + 动词原形

表能力：A horse can run fast.（can 后面动词用原形！）

否定：It can't fly.（can't = can not）

提问：Can a horse run? — Yes, it can. / No, it can't.

⚠️ can 是情态动词：can runs ❌ → can run ✔

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

Lesson 2 对话里，画小蜜蜂的图被朋友猜成马——正好教孩子"猜测-否定-更正"三步对话：Is it...? No! It's...! 建议在家玩"你说我猜"动物游戏：一人描述（It's big. It can run fast.），一人猜（Horse?），猜错继续描述。重点纠正 horses 复数发音 /ˈhɔːsɪz/。

📖 微型故事《The Fast Horse and the Little Bee》

🐴 农场里，小马吹牛："I'm the fastest animal here!" 小蜜蜂嗡嗡飞过："I'm small, but I can fly!" 小马不服："Let's race! From the farm to the big tree!" 哨声一响，小马撒开蹄子——哒哒哒！小蜜蜂直线飞——嗡！小马绕过篱笆、跳过小河，到了树前一看：小蜜蜂已经在花丛里等他啦。"Why are you so fast?" 小马喘着气问。小蜜蜂笑："I fly straight. You run around!" 两个好朋友拉钩约定：明天再比一次。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 比赛终点在哪里？(The big tree)
• 小蜜蜂为什么赢？(It flies straight)
• 问问孩子：If you race your friend, how can you win?

🏛️ 词源故事

horse 来自古英语 hors，上千年前就这么叫。马的身高单位很特别——用"手"（hand）量！1 hand = 4 英寸（约 10 厘米），一匹马从蹄到肩大约 15 到 16 hand，这个量法从古埃及骑手一直传到今天的马场。
🎯 讲给孩子：量马不用尺，手掌摞起来量。

📜 名言 & 谚语

"You can lead a horse to water, but you can't make it drink."（牵马到河边容易，逼马喝水难。）—— 英语谚语：你可以提供机会，但不能强迫人行动。

🎵 歌词里的 horse

🎵 Lil Nas X《Old Town Road》(2019)：Billboard 史上蝉联冠军周数纪录（19 周）的现象级热单，歌词唱到 "horses in the back"——与 fast 页互链：马儿跑得快！备选：America《A Horse with No Name》(1972)。

⚡ 冷知识

马站着就能睡觉！它们腿部有"锁定结构"，四条腿不用弯曲也能撑住身体，这是野外随时逃跑的本领。另外，"黑马 dark horse" 一词 19 世纪从英国赛马场流行开来，专指爆冷夺魁的马，后来引申到选举、比赛等各个领域。
