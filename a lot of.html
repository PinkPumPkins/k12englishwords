📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写a + lot（一大块）+ of，三小块拼成“许多”。它的兄弟 lots of 更随意，意思一样。
✏️ 口诀：a lot of 万金油，可数不可数都不愁。
音·发音/ə ˈlɒt əv/ of 弱读成 /əv/
🎧 跟读：a lot of meat · a lot of fruit · a lot of water
意·释义许多（= lots of；肯定句里比 many/much 更常用）
用·句型① I often eat a lot of meat. (我经常吃很多肉。)
② We should eat a lot of fruit. (我们应该多吃水果。)
③ There is a lot of water in the bottle. (瓶里有很多水。)
④ Thanks a lot! (多谢！——a lot 单独作副词)
拓·同族词三兄弟：a lot of / lots of / many+much（疑问否定常用）；a lot 单独用是副词：play a lot

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
肉类I often eat a lot of meat.
水果We should eat a lot of fruit.
液体There is a lot of milk in the fridge.
口语Thanks a lot!
📋 功能句总结 · “许多”的四兄弟
a lot of：万金油，肯定句之王 — eat a lot of fruit ✔
lots of：a lot of 的随意版 — lots of water
many：专配可数 — many apples
much：专配不可数 — much water（常用在否定/疑问）
⚠️ 口语诀窍：肯定句说 a lot of，否定疑问用 many/much！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

a lot of 与 too much 是本单元的量词双雄：好的东西用 a lot of（eat a lot of fruit），过头的东西用 too much（eat too much candy）。在家玩餐桌分类：孩子把盘中食物分成要多吃 a lot of 和要少碰 too much 两堆，边分边说英语。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 快问快答
家长说词，孩子接 a lot of 还是 too much：fruit → a lot of；candy（劝告时）→ too much
✏️ 关卡2 · 替换闯关
用 lots of 替换：I eat a lot of meat. → I eat ______ meat. (lots of)
✅ 关卡3 · 课本句补全
We should eat ______ fruit. (a lot of)
Do you eat ______ candy? (a lot of)
🔍 关卡4 · 餐桌分类
把今晚的菜分成 a lot of（多吃）和 too much（少吃）两堆并说出英语
🎙️ 关卡5 · 口语大挑战
录一句多吃宣言
🎤 示例：We should eat a lot of fruit and vegetables!

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《许多王国点兵记》

💫 量词王国举行大阅兵！国王 a lot of 站在城楼：我什么兵都能带——苹果兵（可数）报数 a lot of apples！水滴兵（不可数）报到 a lot of water！台下 many 将军举手：可数的交给我！much 将军也举手：不可数的归我！国王大笑：别争啦，日常说话找我 a lot of 最方便，考试仔细时再用你们俩。三军齐呼：Many hands make light work! 🏰

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who can lead both countable and uncountable troops? (A lot of)
• What does Mr. Many lead? (Countable things like apples)
• 问问孩子：用 a lot of 造两个句子——一个接可数，一个接不可数

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 万金油 ── a lot of + 可数/不可数 ✔
├── 兄弟 ── lots of（口语随意版）
├── 分工 ── many + 可数 · much + 不可数
├── 课本 ── eat a lot of fruit ✔ · a lot of meat
└── 单独用 ── Thanks a lot!（副词）

🏛️ 词源故事

lot 的老家是古英语 hlot，本义是抽签用的小物件——古人抓阄分东西，每签就是一份份额，份额多了自然就是许多。同族的 lottery（彩票/抽彩）至今还保留着抓阄的原义。a lot of 字面就是“一大份额的”——从抓阄的小木片到大份的许多，走了上千年。
🎯 讲给孩子：a lot of 的祖先是抓阄的签，lottery 是它的亲兄弟。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
a lot of + 可数复数a lot of apples / books / candies
a lot of + 不可数a lot of water / meat / fruit
lots of同义随意版：lots of fun
a lot（副词）单独用：Thanks a lot. / play a lot

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Many hands make light work.” 🙌 人多力量大。—— 经典谚语：手（可数！）多了活就轻了。量词们凑在一起，连谚语都在给可数名词示范。

🎵 歌词里的 a lot of

🎵 21 Savage《a lot》(2018)：格莱美最佳说唱歌曲，hook 反复 a lot，量词感十足。
🔗 与 a lot 页互链（同曲同源）——a lot 副词版，a lot of 短语版，一首歌两个用法。

⚡ 冷知识

a lot of 和 lots of 意思完全一样，但口语里 lots of 更随意；写正式作文时，老师们更偏爱 many（可数）和 much（不可数）。课本的做法很聪明：建议多吃时用 a lot of fruit（口语亲切），劝少吃时用 too much candy（语气更重）——量词的选择本身就是态度。
