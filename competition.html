📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Feelings（情绪）

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number（小狗连环画，情绪九宫格）
My dog climbs on the table and eats Lily's lunch, so I am angry.（狗偷吃午餐→生气）
He's ill, and I'm worried.（狗病了→担心）
My dog suddenly runs over to Yaoyao, so Yaoyao is scared.（狗冲向瑶瑶→害怕）
I can't find my dog. It makes me cry. → I am very sad.（找不到狗→哭→难过）
He comes back home. I am very excited.（狗回家了→激动）
My dog has a running race. He runs very fast and wins the race. I am proud.（赢赛跑→自豪）

Lesson 1 · B. Let's talk（情绪接龙）
— I am going to sing for my class.（自豪）
— My grandma is ill.（担心）
— My cousin takes away my model plane.（生气）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（老照片里的为什么）
— You look sad in this picture. Why?
— I was sad because I was ill. I had to stay in bed.
— I was happy because Andy came to see me and helped me with my maths.
— I was worried because I wasn't good at maths. I didn't like maths tests.
— Did you do well in the maths test? — Yes. Look, I got an A. I was so proud. — Congratulations!

Lesson 2 · B. Choose and talk（because 句型操练）
— You look sad. Why?
— I am sad because I am ill. I can't play with my friends. I have to stay in bed.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Andy 的日记两则）
Wednesday：I got an A on my maths test. I felt very happy. … After school we went to a singing competition. We sang together and won first place, so I felt very proud.
Friday：My grandmother was ill. She went to hospital, and I was very worried. … I played basketball with my friend Jiayi. I hurt my leg, so I was sad.

Let's Spell · ough 的发音

Fun Time · Good to know（颜色与情绪）
To 'see red' means to get very angry.（see red＝勃然大怒）
In English, you 'feel blue' when you are sad.（feel blue＝心情低落）
但在中国，红色是喜庆的颜色：春节穿红衣、发红包。

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写com·pe·ti·tion —— compete（竞争）的名词，全单元最长的“比赛”词
✏️ 口诀：compete 加 ition，小赛变大宴！
音·发音/ˌkɒm.pəˈtɪʃ.n/ 重音在第三音节，tion 读 /ʃn/
🎧 跟读：a singing competition · take part in a competition · win a competition
意·释义竞赛、比赛 🏆（比才艺、作品的正式比赛；速度赛用 race，球赛用 match/game）
用·句型① We went to a singing competition. (我们去参加了歌唱比赛。)
② We won first place in the competition. (我们在比赛中得了第一。)
③ Did you take part in the competition? (你参加比赛了吗？)
④ The competition was fierce but friendly. (比赛很激烈但很友好。)
拓·同族词compete 竞争（v.）→ competitor 竞争者 → competition 竞赛；同义 contest

📋 功能句总结 · 三种“比赛”的分工

competition/contest（才艺、作品）：a singing/writing competition（课本两处原句）

race（速度）：a running race（Lesson 1 小狗的赛跑）

match/game（球类对抗）：a football match（Let's Check 周三）

⚠️ 搭配：take part in / enter a competition（参赛），win first place in the competition（得第一）。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

competition 是单元收尾的大词。可以陪孩子盘点：参加过哪些 competition？唱歌、写作、跳绳都算。把三个“比赛”词一次分清：跑得快是 race，打对抗是 match，比才艺是 competition。再讲冷知识：ABBA 就是从欧洲歌唱大赛的冠军走出来的——大赛的确出大明星。

📖 微型故事《走廊里的海选》

歌唱比赛报名那天，五年级走廊成了“海选现场”：有人哼歌跑调跑出了新航线，有人一开口全走廊安静。小雨和佳怡组了队，放学后天天在楼梯间练和声。正式比赛那天，两人一句 Don't worry, be happy 唱得全场跟着拍手——first place！评委说：competition 评的是名次，走廊里练的是友情，两手都要硬。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What competition did the girls enter? (A singing competition.)
• What song did they sing? (Don't Worry, Be Happy.)
• 问问孩子：If you could enter any competition, which one would you choose? Why?

🏛️ 词源故事

competition 来自拉丁语 competere，由 com-（一起）+ petere（追求、冲向）组成，字面是“一起冲向同一个目标”——竞争者得先站上同一条跑道。petere 的后代还有 appetite（胃口：向食物冲）和 petition（请愿：向权威请求）。
🎯 讲给孩子：competition 的字面是“一起追”——对手，也是同行者。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
compete竞争、参赛（v.：compete in the race）
competitor竞争者、选手（-or 表人）
contest同义：竞赛（多指才艺类：a speech contest 演讲比赛）
win first place得第一名（课本原句搭配）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“He writes — not that you won or lost — but how you played the Game.” ⚽
（伟大的记分员）记下的不是你输或赢，而是你怎么打这场比赛。—— 格兰特兰德·赖斯诗句（1908）：比赛丈量的从来不止名次。

🎵 歌词里的 competition

🎵 ABBA《The Winner Takes It All》（1980）：“赢的人拿走一切”——把竞赛的胜负感唱到了人心深处；ABBA 自己就是 1974 年欧洲歌唱大赛冠军出道的“比赛传奇”。
🔗 与 win 页互链（同曲同源）；prize 页亦同曲互链。

⚡ 冷知识

全世界最著名的“歌唱 competition”是 1956 年创办的欧洲歌唱大赛（Eurovision Song Contest）：各国一曲定胜负，冠军一夜红遍欧洲——1974 年的冠军就是 ABBA（凭《Waterloo》）。从参赛者到规则改写者，ABBA 用十年证明了大赛的含金量。
