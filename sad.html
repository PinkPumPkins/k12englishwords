📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Feelings（情绪）

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number（小狗连环画，情绪九宫格）
My dog climbs on the table and eats Lily's lunch, so I am angry.（狗偷吃午餐→生气）
He's ill, and I'm worried.（狗病了→担心）
My dog suddenly runs over to Yaoyao, so Yaoyao is scared.（狗冲向瑶瑶→害怕）
I can't find my dog. It makes me cry. → I am very sad.（找不到狗→哭→难过）
He comes back home. I am very excited.（狗回家了→激动）
My dog has a running race. He runs very fast and wins the race. I am proud.（赢赛跑→自豪）

Lesson 1 · B. Let's talk（情绪接龙）
— I am going to sing for my class.（自豪）
— My grandma is ill.（担心）
— My cousin takes away my model plane.（生气）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（老照片里的为什么）
— You look sad in this picture. Why?
— I was sad because I was ill. I had to stay in bed.
— I was happy because Andy came to see me and helped me with my maths.
— I was worried because I wasn't good at maths. I didn't like maths tests.
— Did you do well in the maths test? — Yes. Look, I got an A. I was so proud. — Congratulations!

Lesson 2 · B. Choose and talk（because 句型操练）
— You look sad. Why?
— I am sad because I am ill. I can't play with my friends. I have to stay in bed.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Andy 的日记两则）
Wednesday：I got an A on my maths test. I felt very happy. … After school we went to a singing competition. We sang together and won first place, so I felt very proud.
Friday：My grandmother was ill. She went to hospital, and I was very worried. … I played basketball with my friend Jiayi. I hurt my leg, so I was sad.

Let's Spell · ough 的发音
bought 买 · brought 带来 · thought 想 · enough 足够 · tough 坚硬 · rough 粗糙

Fun Time · Good to know（颜色与情绪）
To 'see red' means to get very angry.（see red＝勃然大怒）
In English, you 'feel blue' when you are sad.（feel blue＝心情低落）
但在中国，红色是喜庆的颜色：春节穿红衣、发红包。

Story Time · 灰姑娘的情绪过山车
Cinderella was sad（继母姐姐懒惰，她做所有家务）→ the stepsisters were always angry with her → 为舞会 excited → Cinderella cried → 见到仙女 she was scared → 舞会上 she was so happy → 12点她 was worried，跑丢一只鞋 → 王子找遍全城 find the beautiful girl → 鞋子 fitted! → They were very happy together.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写sad —— 三个字母的最短情绪词；sad→sadly→sadness 三件套
✏️ 口诀：s-a-d，sad 是 happy 的对家！
音·发音/sæd/ 一个音节，a 读 /æ/ 嘴巴张大
🎧 跟读：feel sad · a sad story · sad to say
意·释义难过的 😢（心情低落；口语还说 feel blue“发蓝”、down“低落”——课本 Fun Time 就有 feel blue）
用·句型① I am sad because I am ill. (我难过，因为我病了。)
② Don't be sad — tomorrow is another day. (别难过，明天又是新的一天。)
③ The story has a sad ending. (故事的结局很悲伤。)
④ Why do you look so sad? (你怎么看起来这么难过？)
拓·同族词sad → sadly 难过地 → sadness 悲伤；情绪同伙：down 低落 · blue 忧郁的（feel blue）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
安慰Don't be sad. I'm here with you.
原因句I was sad because my friend moved away.
描述It's a sad movie, but a good one.
对比sad 难过的（人/事）→ sadness 悲伤（名词）
📋 功能句总结 · feel sad 与 sad 家族

feel sad（感到难过）：I feel sad when it rains all day.（下了一整天雨，我感到难过。）

sad + 名词：a sad story / sad news（悲伤的故事/消息）

because 原因句：I am sad because I am ill.（课本 Lesson 2 核心句。）

⚠️ sad→sadly（副词）→sadness（名词），三件套一起记。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

sad 是孩子需要学会“说出来”的情绪。孩子说 I'm sad，家长先问 Why?，再用 Don't be sad because... 安慰回去。课本 Fun Time 提到 feel blue——英语用“蓝色”说伤心，而 the blues（蓝调）音乐正是从这种情绪里长出来的，可以放一首和孩子共听。

👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《雨天的小蓝伞》

周五放学，小雨没带伞，好朋友转学的事还没缓过来。她坐在台阶上，雨点敲着书包：“I'm sad today. My best friend moved to another city.” 妈妈撑开一把蓝色小伞走过来：“Feeling blue? Let's walk home in the rain.” 一路踩水花、数彩虹，到家时小雨笑了：“The rain is still sad, but I feel better.” 蓝色的伞下，伤心被慢慢晾干了。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why was the girl sad? (Her best friend moved to another city.)
• What colour was the umbrella? (Blue.)
• 问问孩子：What do you do when you feel sad?

🏛️ 词源故事

sad 的祖先竟和“吃饱”有关：古英语 sæd 意思是“饱了、腻了”，和 satisfy（满足）是远亲——都源自“足够”。从“吃到撑”引申为“对什么都提不起劲”，再变成“伤心”——从胃里的满，到心里的空，一词跨越千年。
🎯 讲给孩子：sad 和 satisfy 是远房亲戚，各自走了不同的路。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“The word 'happy' would lose its meaning if it were not balanced by sadness.” 🌈
如果没有悲伤来平衡，“快乐”这个词就失去了意义。—— 心理学家荣格《回忆·梦·思考》：难过不是快乐的敌人，是快乐的底色。

🎵 歌词里的 sad

🎵 Elton John《Sad Songs (Say So Much)》（1984）：传奇钢琴家的名曲，副歌唱透一个道理——伤心的时候，伤心歌最懂你（Sad songs say so much）；难过不必藏，音乐会接住你。
🔗 情绪家族页互链：与 cry 页、worried 页同属 Unit 4 情绪天团。

⚡ 冷知识

英语里伤心会“变颜色”：feel blue（发蓝）从17世纪的 blue devils（忧郁小恶魔）演变而来，后来这种情绪催生了美国最重要的音乐流派之一——the blues 蓝调。伤心到极致，居然弹出了一种世界音乐，还一路孕育了摇滚——这就是“难过”的创造力。
