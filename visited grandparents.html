📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写visit + ed —— visit 以 t 结尾，加 ed 后 -ted 读 /ɪd/，多出一个音节！
✏️ 口诀：visit 音节短，加 ed 多一拍：vi-si-ted
音·发音/ˈvɪzɪtɪd/ 三个音节，重音在第一拍
🎧 跟读：visited grandparents · visited my uncle · visited a museum
意·释义看望、拜访祖父母（过去发生）。grandparents 是 grandfather 与 grandmother 的合称
用·句型① I visited my grandparents last weekend. (上周末我去看望祖父母了。)
② I cleaned the windows for them. (我帮他们擦了窗户。——课本同篇！)
③ Grandma cooked delicious food for me.
④ Did you visit your grandparents?
拓·同族词visit → visited → visiting；visitor 访客
家族词：grandfather 爷爷/外公 · grandmother 奶奶/外婆

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句On Sunday, I visited my grandparents.
孝心句I cleaned the windows for them.
描述Grandma cooked noodles for me. Yummy!
询问Did you visit your grandparents last weekend?
功能句总结 · 家族称呼

grandparents 合称：grandfather 爷爷/外公 + grandmother 奶奶/外婆
英语不分内外：英语里爷爷奶奶外公外婆都说 grandpa/grandma，靠语境区分
告别用语：See you next time, Grandma!（下次见，奶奶！）

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

看望祖父母是练习过去式最好的真实场景：回家路上和孩子复盘 We visited grandparents. We ate noodles. Grandma was happy. 再让孩子给老人打个视频电话，说一句 Hello Grandma, I visited you last weekend! 孝心和英语一起升温。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《Grandma's Magic Kitchen》

周末，Ben 跟爸妈去看望祖父母。刚进门，Grandma 就变魔术似的端出热腾腾的面条。Ben 边吃边问：Grandma, how did you know I was hungry? Grandma 眨眨眼：A grandma's kitchen has magic. 饭后 Ben 帮着擦桌子、扫地。回家路上他说：I visited my grandparents. I ate magic noodles! 心里暖暖的。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Grandma cook? (Noodles)
• What did Ben do after lunch? (He cleaned the table and swept the floor)
• 问问孩子：What can you do for your grandparents next time?

🏛️ 词源故事

visit 来自拉丁语 visitare，本义“去看望”，词根 videre 意思是“看见”——看望看望，先“看”到才叫“望”。英语里 visit a museum（参观）和 visit grandparents（探望）用同一个词。grand- 来自拉丁语，表示“大一辈”。🎯 讲给孩子：visit 的核心是带着眼睛和心去看，光打电话可不算 visited 哦。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“家有一老，如有一宝。” 🏮
英译：A family with an elder is like having a treasure in the house.——中文谚语英译版。visited grandparents 不只是过去式练习，更是一次“寻宝”。

⚡ 冷知识

美国真的有“全国祖父母节”（National Grandparents Day）：1978年由卡特总统签署设立，定在九月劳动节后的第一个星期日。发起人最初的心愿，就是让隔代亲多一次团圆——和我们的重阳节心意相通。
