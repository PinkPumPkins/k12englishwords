Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and chant
Who is she? Who is he? She is my mother. He is my father. And they love me.
Who is she? Who is he? She is my aunt. He is my uncle. And they love me.
Who are we? We are together. We are a family.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and repeat（课本 P41）
— Is that man your father?
— No, he isn't. He's my uncle. That tall man is my father.
— Really? He is handsome.
— Is she your aunt?
— Yes, she is. She's beautiful.

Lesson 3 · A. Read and tick（失物招领小故事）
Who lost the water bottle?
— Good morning, Mrs Chen. Can you help me?
— Sure, Bill. A boy lost this water bottle…
— What does he look like?
— He has short hair and big eyes.
— OK. I see. It's Li Ming's water bottle.
— Thank you very much. — You're welcome.

Story Time（小蝌蚪找妈妈）
— Excuse me. Are you our mother?
— She's green. She has big eyes. She can swim.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写much —— 四个字母；别和 march（行军/三月）、match（比赛）弄混，它们是邻居 ✏️ 口诀：m-u-c-h，“马吃草”吃太多（much = 太多）
音·发音/mʌtʃ/ 单音节，ch 读 /tʃ/；🎧 跟读：Thank you very much. · too much sugar · How much is it?
意·释义adv. 非常（very much）；det. 许多（+不可数名词：much water, much time）；How much? 还能问价格
用·句型① Thank you very much. (非常感谢。)
② I like it very much. (我非常喜欢它。)
③ Too much sugar is bad. (太多糖不好。)
④ How much is the toy? (这个玩具多少钱？)
拓·同族词much ↔ many：much + 不可数（much water），many + 可数（many apples）；too much 太多（略含“过度”）；much + 比较级 = ……得多（much better）

📋 功能句总结 · Thank you 答谢链

谢：Thank you. → Thank you very much.（语气更强）

回：You're welcome. / That's OK. / My pleasure.

⚠️ much + 不可数名词：much water ✔ / much books ✘——可数名词要用 many！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

“Thank you very much. — You're welcome.” 是课本 Lesson 3 的礼貌收尾句。建议在家设“感谢回声”规则：家人帮忙后必须说 Thank you very much.，对方答 You're welcome.；买东西时让孩子用英语问 How much is it?——much 的两大用法（非常/多少）一天打通。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《Too Much Candy》

📖 东东迷上了糖果：“One, two, three... I want more!” 妈妈按住糖罐：“Too much candy is bad for your teeth.” 东东噘起了嘴。牙医阿姨检查后说：“You have a little hole now.” 东东红了脸：“I'm sorry. Thank you very much for helping me.” 妈妈眨眨眼：“So, how much candy now?” 东东伸出两根手指：“Just two a week!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Dongdong eat too much of? (Candy)
• What happened to his tooth? (A little hole)
• 问问孩子：much 后面跟可数还是不可数名词？much water 对不对？（对）

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 非常 ── Thank you very much. · I like it very much.
├── 许多 ── much + 不可数：much water · much time
├── 对比 ── many + 可数：many books · many apples
├── 问价 ── How much is it? — It's five yuan.
└── 过度 ── too much sugar 太多糖（要少吃哦）

🏛️ 词源故事

much 来自古英语 micel（大的、多的），它与苏格兰语 mickle（同样表示“大”）是亲兄弟；而 many（许多）另有血统——所以 much / many 意思像，出身却不同。
🎯 讲给孩子：much 的老家意思是“大”——所以它天生和“量”打交道。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
how much①多少钱 ②多少（+不可数）：How much water?
too much太多（含“过度”）：too much TV
much better好得多（much + 比较级，加强语气）

📜 名言 & 谚语

莎士比亚《皆大欢喜》里罗瑟琳问：“Can one desire too much of a good thing?”（好东西会嫌太多吗？）——后来演变成英语谚语 “You can have too much of a good thing.”（好东西也不能贪多），跟 much 的用法天生一对。

🎵 歌词里的 much

🎵 Spice Girls《Too Much》(1997)：辣妹组合连续第六支英国冠军单曲，也是1997年 UK 圣诞冠军单曲——“too much of something” 的副歌把 much 的“太多”感唱得明明白白（美国 Hot 100 最高第9名）。
🎵 互链：与 welcome 页互链——课本答谢对 Thank you very much. — You're welcome. 正是一问一答。

⚡ 冷知识

much 和 many 的分工是考试常客：much + 不可数（much water / much time），many + 可数（many books / many dogs）。还有更强大的用法：much 放在比较级前表示“……得多”——much better（好得多）、much bigger（大得多），一个 much 让句子瞬间升级！
