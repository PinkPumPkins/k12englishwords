📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答

— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写send 四字母短词；过去式 sent（d 变 t），现在分词 sending
✏️ 口诀：send 变 sent，d 变 t 不商量！
音·发音/send/ 短元音 e
🎧 跟读：send a letter · send me a message
意·释义发送、寄 📤（把东西或消息送到别人那里）
用·句型① I want to send a postcard to my grandma. (我想给奶奶寄张明信片。)
② Please send me a message. (请给我发条消息。)
③ Dad sends an email every morning. (爸爸每天早上发一封邮件。)
④ Don't forget to send the letter. (别忘了寄信。)
拓·同族词send a letter 寄信 · send an email 发邮件 · send a short message 发短信；过去式 sent

功能句总结 · send + 双宾语
send sb sth = send sth to sb
Send me a message. = Send a message to me.
给我发条消息。
邮局场景：I want to send a postcard to my grandma. 我想给奶奶寄张明信片。
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

send 是“寄、发”的万能动词：寄信用 send a letter，发邮件用 send an email，发消息用 send a message。和孩子一起给远方亲人寄一张明信片，写上 I want to send this postcard to…，一次真实任务记住一个词。

📖 微型故事《爷爷的第一封电子邮件》

💻 爷爷学会用电脑啦！明明帮他给老战友发第一封邮件：“Send it, please!” 点击发送，屏幕跳出 “Message sent!” 爷爷盯着两个字笑了：“我寄了一辈子信，这封信一秒钟就到了。” 当晚老战友回了邮件，爷爷又惊又喜。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Grandpa send? (一封电子邮件)
• How fast did it arrive? (一秒钟)
• 问问孩子：你想给谁 send 一条消息？

🗺️ 思维导图

send 发送 ├── 家族 ── send a letter / an email / a message ├── 时态 ── send → sent（d变t） ├── 句型 ── send sb sth = send sth to sb ├── 词源 ── 本义“让…上路” → 把东西送出去 └── 互链 ── post office 页 · mail a present 页

🏛️ 词源故事

send 来自古英语 sendan，本义“使…前往、让…动身”——它的日耳曼词根就是“去、走”。所以 send 的本质不是“纸和笔”，而是“让东西替你上路”：从驿马快递到电子邮件，变的只是“路”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

诗人约翰·多恩的名句（海明威《丧钟为谁而鸣》书名出处）：“Never send to know for whom the bell tolls; it tolls for thee.”（不要去打听丧钟为谁而鸣，它也为你而鸣。）send 在这里的古雅用法是“派人去打听”。

🎵 歌词里的 send

🎵 The Marvelettes《Please Mr. Postman》(1961)：“Please Mr. Postman, look and see, is there a letter in your bag for me?”——等信的人求邮差“送”来消息。与 post office 页、mail a present 页同曲互链。

⚡ 冷知识

第一封电子邮件是1971年工程师 Ray Tomlinson 发出的，他还顺手选了 @ 符号隔开“人名”和“电脑名”——今天全球邮箱里的 @ 就这样定了下来。他事后坦言：自己已不记得第一封邮件的内容了。
