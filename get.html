📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写get 三字母超短词；过去式 got
✏️ 口诀：get 一家子全家桶：起床上车再到达！
音·发音/ɡet/ 短促有力
🎧 跟读：get some money · get a ball · get up
意·释义得到、拿到 🎁（本课用法：get some money from the bank 取钱）
用·句型① Get some money from the bank. (从银行取些钱。)
② I got a new toy yesterday. (我昨天得到一个新玩具。)
③ Can you get me a cup of water? (能给我拿杯水吗？)
④ She gets good grades. (她成绩很好。)
拓·同族词get 超能动词：get up 起床 · get on/off 上/下车 · get home 到家；本单元 get to 到达（互链）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本Get some money from the bank.
获得I got a present on my birthday.
动作Get up early and get some exercise.
理解Got it!（明白啦！）
功能句总结 · get 的高频口语
Got it! 明白啦！（课堂上老师讲完，学生最常用）
Get up! 起床！ / Get on the bus. 上公交车
get + 名词：get a ball / get some money 拿到…
get 是英语口语里的“万能动词”，先记住“得到”这个核心义。
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

get 的义项多，先只抓“得到”：拿到东西说 I got it!。再把家里的 get 短语当口令游戏：Get up!（跳起来）Get the ball!（捡球）Get on the sofa!（坐沙发）。孩子动作做对了，短语就都记住了。

📖 微型故事《Get Back！逃跑的小皮球》

⚽ 明明的皮球滚进了街心花园。妈妈喊：“Get it back!” 明明一路追，跨过小河、绕过滑梯，一把抱住皮球，学着电视里唱歌：“Get back to where you once belonged!” 妈妈笑弯了腰：“That's a Beatles song!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Mum shout? (Get it back!)
• What song did Mingming sing? (Get Back)
• 问问孩子：你的玩具“逃跑”过吗？用 get it back 说一说。

🗺️ 思维导图

get 得到 ├── 核心 ── 得到、拿到：get a present ├── 家族 ── get up · get on · get home ├── 时态 ── get → got ├── 口语 ── Got it! 明白啦 └── 互链 ── get to 页 · money 页（get some money）

🏛️ 词源故事

get 来自古老的日耳曼语 geta（获得）。一千年来它开枝散叶：get 到达（get to）、get 变得（get tired）、get 理解（Got it!）——今天它是英语口语里义项最多的动词之一。英国人也爱说 have got（有）：I've got a ball = I have a ball。

📜 名言 & 谚语

电影《肖申克的救赎》经典台词：“Get busy living, or get busy dying.”（要么忙着活，要么忙着死。）两个 get，把人生态度说得干干净净——这也是无数人的座右铭。

🎵 歌词里的 get

🎵 The Beatles《Get Back》(1969)：Billboard 冠军，副歌 “Get back to where you once belonged”；最难忘的是1969年1月30日披头士在伦敦街头屋顶的传奇“最后一场演唱会”，唱的就有它。备选曲：Daft Punk《Get Lucky》。与 get to 页（Oleta Adams《Get Here》）互链。

⚡ 冷知识

美国1993年起的经典广告语 “Got Milk?”（有牛奶吗？）只有两个词，却火遍全球、被无数品牌模仿。get 的过去分词 got 站在句首，成了广告史上最成功的开场白之一。
