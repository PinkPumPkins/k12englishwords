Lesson 1 · A. 订计划

— From January sixteenth to twenty-eighth, I'm going to skating classes. I'm going to play in the snow there with my cousin.

— Spring Festival is coming in February.

Lesson 1 · B. 大家的寒假计划

— I'm going to learn Chinese painting.

（计划短语：learn to make dumplings · visit my grandparents · do some chores · go to a maths club · meet my pen pal）

Lesson 2 · A. 放学最后一天
— Lily, today is the last day of school. Where are you going and what are you going to do there?
— I'm going to Harbin with my parents. We're going to see ice lanterns and play in the snow.
— I don't like cold weather. I'm afraid of getting a cold.
（短语：bring back Harbin sausages 带回哈尔滨红肠）

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（寒假心愿）
I am going to read some books about Canada. I hear there is a lot of snow in Canada, so I want to learn to ski. I think it will be hard, but I want to have a try!
I am going to prepare for a Peking Opera competition. I hope I can win the first prize this year.
I am looking forward to visiting my uncle. A wonderful winter vacation is waiting for me!

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写bring —— b·r·i·ng 四个字母；过去式不是 branged，而是 brought（和 buy→bought 长得像）！
✏️ 口诀：带来 bring，带回 brought，ng 变 ught。
音·发音/brɪŋ/ 一个音节
🎧 跟读：bring some snacks · bring it here · bring back gifts
意·释义带来；拿来（朝说话人方向拿过来；反义 take 带走）
用·句型① I'm going to bring back Harbin sausages. (我要带回哈尔滨红肠。——课本短语 bring back！)
② Please bring your homework here tomorrow. (请明天把作业带来。)
③ Can you bring me some water? (能给我拿点水吗？)
④ What should I bring to the party? (聚会我该带点什么？)
拓·同族词bring back 带回 · bring up 抚养；提出 · bring together 使相聚；四兄弟：bring 带来 / take 带走 / fetch 去取回 / carry 随身扛

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
请求Can you bring me some water?
计划I'm going to bring back some gifts.
聚会What should I bring to the party?
对比Bring it here, please.（带过来） / Take it there.（带过去）
📋 功能句总结 · bring 与 take 的方向感

bring＝朝这边来：Bring your book to me.（把书带到我这儿。）

take＝往那边去：Take your book to school.（把书带去学校。）

礼貌请求：Can you bring me ...? / Please bring ...

⚠️ 方向是关键：来我这里用 bring，去别处用 take

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

寒假走亲访友正是练 bring 的时机：出门前问 What are we going to bring?（我们要带点什么？）；回家后说 I brought some gifts for grandma.（过去式 brought 别说错）。用真实的一来一回，孩子就把方向感记住了。

📖 微型故事《空手而归的教训》

去外婆家前，妈妈问东东：“What are we going to bring?” 东东摆摆手：“Nothing! We can buy things there.” 到了外婆家，外婆端出热腾腾的饺子，还给他们带了自家晒的红薯干。东东有点脸红。回家路上他说：“Next time, I'm going to bring something. Grandpa always brings us gifts. I want to bring, too.” 妈妈笑了：带来的是东西，暖的是心。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Grandma give them? (Dumplings and dried sweet potatoes 红薯干)
• What did Dongdong decide? (Next time he will bring something too.)
• 问问孩子：When you visit someone, what will you bring?

🏛️ 词源故事

bring 是英语里最古老的词之一，来自古英语 bringan，和德语 bringen 是亲戚——一千多年前的日耳曼人就在说它。它的过去式 brought 也走了一条不规则的老路（类似 buy→bought、think→thought）。
🎯 讲给孩子：bring 不规则，是因为它资格太老——越老的词，越爱“不走寻常路”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“April showers bring May flowers.” 🌧️→🌸
四月雨带来五月花。—— 英语谚语：bring 最诗意的用法——眼下的努力，是 bring 给未来的礼物。

🎵 歌词里的 bring

🎵 Evanescence《Bring Me to Life》（2003）：格莱美获奖摇滚金曲，“Wake me up inside” 的对唱经典——歌名直译“把我带回生活”，唤醒感十足（摇滚风格已标注）。bring 在歌名里打头阵：唤醒生命的，正是“带来”的力量。

⚡ 冷知识

英语考试最爱考的四兄弟：bring（带来）、take（带走）、fetch（去取回来）、carry（随身扛）。记忆诀窍全在方向：朝你走来用 bring，离你而去用 take，跑个来回是 fetch，扛在肩上是 carry。哈尔滨红肠、北京烤鸭——放假回家的行李箱里，装的全是 bring。
