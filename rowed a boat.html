📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写row + ed —— row 以 w 结尾，直接加 ed：rowed
✏️ 口诀：row 双桨加 ed，rowed a boat 出发喽
音·发音/rəʊd/ 与 road（马路）完全同音！
🎧 跟读：rowed a boat · row, row, row your boat
意·释义划船（过去发生）。row 划（桨）；boat 小船
用·句型① We rowed a boat in the park. (我们在公园划船了。——课本原句！)
② I rowed a boat on the lake. (我在湖上划船了。)
③ Row, row, row your boat! (划呀划呀划小船——儿歌！)
④ Did you row a boat last weekend?
拓·同族词row → rowed → rowing；同音词：rowed = road
相关词：oars 船桨 · lake 湖 · river 河

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句We rowed a boat in the park.
场景I rowed a boat on the lake with my family.
儿歌Row, row, row your boat, gently down the stream.
询问Did you row a boat on Sunday?
功能句总结 · 同音词 rowed / road

rowed：/rəʊd/ 划船（row 的过去式）🚣
road：/rəʊd/ 马路
例：We rowed a boat near the road.（我们在马路附近划了船。）
⚠️ 听到 /rəʊd/ 要靠上下文判断是哪个词！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

row 和 road 同音是绝佳听力梗：家长说 /rəʊd/ a boat，让孩子判断是划船还是马路。周末去公园划船时实时喊 Row! Row! Row!，回家总结 We rowed a boat! 儿歌《Row Row Row Your Boat》是全家最容易唱的过去式复习曲。

👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The Wrong Way Boat》

爸爸带 Sam 去公园划船。Sam 抢着划桨，小船却原地转圈圈！Dad, why won't it go? 爸爸笑：Row together, not against each other. 两人喊着 one-two、one-two，船终于乖乖向前。上岸时 Sam 举着桨宣布：I rowed a boat! And I made it go straight! 湖面上一圈圈波纹，像在鼓掌。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why did the boat go in circles at first? (Sam rowed against Dad)
• What did Dad teach Sam? (Row together)
• 问问孩子：If you rowed a boat, who would you take with you?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 原形 ── row a boat → rowed a boat
├── 同音词 ── rowed = road /rəʊd/
├── 配件 ── oar 桨 · lake 湖 · life jacket 救生衣
├── 课本句 ── We rowed a boat in the park.
└── 儿歌 ── Row Row Row Your Boat

🏛️ 词源故事

row 来自古英语 rōwan，和拉丁语 rēmus（桨）是远亲——“划”这个词在印欧语里东西方都有亲戚。boat 来自古英语 bāt。rowed 与 road 同音纯属巧合，但英语学习者很喜欢这个梗：先走 road，再 row a boat。🎯 讲给孩子：一条桨一只船，row 是人类最古老的“发动机”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“A smooth sea never made a skilled sailor.” ⛵
平静的大海练不出好水手。——英语谚语。Sam 的船转圈圈的时候，正是他学得最快的时候。

🎵 歌词里的 rowed a boat

🎵 传统童谣《Row Row Row Your Boat》：全球传唱度最高的划船歌，Row, row, row your boat, gently down the stream... life is but a dream。

🎵 与 row a boat 页互链（同源曲目）；一人一句的合唱版最适合家庭卡拉OK。

⚡ 冷知识

牛津剑桥的“赛艇对抗赛”从1829年比到今天，快两百年，是历史最悠久的校际体育赛事之一——两校八人艇队每年在泰晤士河上一决胜负，全英国围观。划船（rowing）也是现代奥运会最古老的项目之一。
