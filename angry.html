📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Feelings（情绪）

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number（小狗连环画，情绪九宫格）
My dog climbs on the table and eats Lily's lunch, so I am angry.（狗偷吃午餐→生气）
He's ill, and I'm worried.（狗病了→担心）
My dog suddenly runs over to Yaoyao, so Yaoyao is scared.（狗冲向瑶瑶→害怕）
I can't find my dog. It makes me cry. → I am very sad.（找不到狗→哭→难过）
He comes back home. I am very excited.（狗回家了→激动）
My dog has a running race. He runs very fast and wins the race. I am proud.（赢赛跑→自豪）

Lesson 1 · B. Let's talk（情绪接龙）
— I am going to sing for my class.（自豪）
— My grandma is ill.（担心）
— My cousin takes away my model plane.（生气）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（老照片里的为什么）
— You look sad in this picture. Why?
— I was sad because I was ill. I had to stay in bed.
— I was happy because Andy came to see me and helped me with my maths.
— I was worried because I wasn't good at maths. I didn't like maths tests.
— Did you do well in the maths test? — Yes. Look, I got an A. I was so proud. — Congratulations!

Lesson 2 · B. Choose and talk（because 句型操练）
— You look sad. Why?
— I am sad because I am ill. I can't play with my friends. I have to stay in bed.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Andy 的日记两则）
Wednesday：I got an A on my maths test. I felt very happy. … After school we went to a singing competition. We sang together and won first place, so I felt very proud.
Friday：My grandmother was ill. She went to hospital, and I was very worried. … I played basketball with my friend Jiayi. I hurt my leg, so I was sad.

Let's Spell · ough 的发音
bought 买 · brought 带来 · thought 想 · enough 足够 · tough 坚硬 · rough 粗糙

Fun Time · Good to know（颜色与情绪）
To 'see red' means to get very angry.（see red＝勃然大怒）
In English, you 'feel blue' when you are sad.（feel blue＝心情低落）
但在中国，红色是喜庆的颜色：春节穿红衣、发红包。

Let's Check · 一周情绪日记
Monday：Bill lost his kite, so he felt sad. → Tuesday：Andy's dad went to hospital. We were all very worried. → Wednesday：Our team won, so we were very excited. → Thursday：Binbin made his model plane himself, so he was proud. → Friday：My brother took away my new book. I was very angry. → Saturday：I was scared because a big black dog ran up to me. → Sunday：I was happy because my aunt came back to China.

Story Time · 灰姑娘的情绪过山车
Cinderella was sad（继母姐姐懒惰，她做所有家务）→ the stepsisters were always angry with her → 为舞会 excited → Cinderella cried → 见到仙女 she was scared → 舞会上 she was so happy → 12点她 was worried，跑丢一只鞋 → 王子找遍全城 find the beautiful girl → 鞋子 fitted! → They were very happy together.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写an·gry —— anger（怒气）去 er 加 y；注意 g 后有个 r
✏️ 口诀：angry 里藏 anger，去掉 er 就生气！
音·发音/ˈæŋ.ɡri/ 两个音节，n+g 连读 /ŋɡ/
🎧 跟读：be angry with · get angry · an angry face
意·释义生气的 😠（最常用的“生气”词；get angry 生气了，be angry with sb 生某人的气）
用·句型① Don't be angry with me. (别生我的气。)
② My teacher was very angry because I didn't finish my homework. (老师很生气，因为我没完成作业。)
③ He got angry about the broken window. (他因打碎的窗户而生气。)
④ What are you angry about? (你在气什么？)
拓·同族词anger 怒气（n.）→ angry 生气的 → angrily 生气地；网络新词 hangry = hungry + angry（饿到发火）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
道歉安抚I'm sorry. Please don't be angry.
原因句He was angry because his brother took his book.
询问Why are you so angry?
对比angry with + 人；angry about + 事。
📋 功能句总结 · angry 的介词分工

be angry with + 人：Mum is angry with me.（妈妈生我的气。）

be angry about/at + 事：He is angry about the noise.（他为噪音生气。）

⚠️ 一句话记：with 对人，about 对事——课本里“狗偷吃午餐所以生气”“老师因作业生气”都是这两个介词的活教材。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

生气是最需要“翻译”的情绪。孩子生气时先带他说英文：I'm angry because...——把火气变成句子，火就灭了一半。结合课本 Fun Time：英语里 see red 表示“勃然大怒”，但在中国红色是喜庆色——正好跟孩子聊聊中西方颜色情绪的差异。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《被摔碎的飞机模型》

课间，小宇刚拼好的飞机模型被同桌当弹弓玩，“啪”地摔成了三截。小宇脸涨得通红：“I'm so angry! My model plane!” 他攥紧拳头想吼回去，又想起妈妈的话：先说清楚，再谈办法。“I'm angry because you broke it. Please be careful next time.” 同桌红着脸递来胶水：“Sorry. Let me fix it with you.” 半小时后，飞机重新上天——生气的最高段位，是让事情变好。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why was Xiaoyu angry? (His model plane was broken.)
• What did the classmate do? (He said sorry and helped fix it.)
• 问问孩子：When you are angry, what can you do to cool down?

🏛️ 词源故事

angry 的老家在北欧：来自古诺尔斯语 angr，本义是“苦恼、愁烦”。维京人把它带进英语后，“心里的堵”慢慢变成了“头上的火”。它的近亲 hangry（饿怒）2018年被收入韦氏词典——饿到发火这件事，人类早有共识。
🎯 讲给孩子：生气是维京人送来的词，但灭火的法子要自己练。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Anybody can become angry — that is easy. But to be angry with the right person, to the right degree, at the right time, for the right purpose, and in the right way — that is not easy.” ⚖️
人人都会生气，这很容易；但对的人、对的度、对的时刻、对的缘由、对的方式——那可不简单。—— 亚里士多德《尼各马可伦理学》

⚡ 冷知识

课本 Fun Time 里的 see red（勃然大怒）有个流行解释：斗牛场的牛冲的不是红布的颜色，而是晃动的动作——牛其实是色盲！不过“红＝怒”的印象早就写进了英语。另外，“愤怒的小鸟”（Angry Birds）让 angry 成了全球小学生都会拼的单词——看来“生气”也要讲专业。
