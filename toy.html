📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写toy 3个字母超短词；复数 toys（元音+y 直接加 s，不用变 i）
✏️ 口诀：boy → boys，toy → toys，元音后 y 好说话！
音·发音/tɔɪ/ 单音节，双元音 /ɔɪ/ 读准 oy
🎧 跟读：a toy shop · buy a toy · my toys
意·释义玩具 🧸（toy shop 玩具店）
用·句型① He sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. (他看到餐馆对面的玩具店。)
② Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. (Lucky想要玩具，就买了个球。)
③ I play with my toys after school. (放学后我玩玩具。)
④ This toy shop is next to the bank. (玩具店在银行旁边。)
拓·同族词toy shop 玩具店 · toy car 玩具车；复数 toys（boy→boys 同规则）；互链 across from 页（toy shop across from…）

📖 微型故事《餐馆对面的玩具店》

🧸 课本里的 Lucky 路过餐馆，抬头看见一家 toy shop across from the restaurant。橱窗里的红色皮球正冲他眨眼。“Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball.” 抱着新球，Lucky 忘了邮局的钟声——玩具的快乐，连任务都能让它等等。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Lucky see across from the restaurant? (玩具店)
• What did he buy? (一个球)
• 问问孩子：如果玩具店就在你家对面，你想要什么玩具？

🏛️ 词源故事

toy 的身世词源学界至今没有定论：一般认为它来自中古英语 toy（嬉戏、小玩意儿），13-14世纪时 toy 指的是“玩乐、玩意儿”，后来才专指儿童玩具。一个“来路不明”的小词，却成了全世界孩子的快乐密码。

📜 名言 & 谚语

《玩具总动员》主题曲名句：“You've got a friend in me.”（你有个朋友在我身边。）Randy Newman 写的这首歌说透玩具的意义——最好的玩具不是最贵的，而是陪你的那个。

🎵 歌词里的 toy

🎵 电影《玩具总动员》主题曲《You've Got a Friend in Me》(1995)：Randy Newman 创作并演唱，奥斯卡最佳原创歌曲提名，电影与歌一起成了两代人的童年。与 nice 页同曲互链；本单元和 across from 页（toy shop 所在句）互链。

⚡ 冷知识

乐高积木的名字 LEGO 来自丹麦语 “leg godt”，意思是“玩得开心”。乐高公司1932年由木匠 Ole Kirk Kristiansen 创办——先做木玩具，1949年才有了塑料积木。世界冠军积木的初心，就是一句“玩得好”。
