📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 My Family

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and chant
Who is she? Who is he? She is my mother. He is my father. And they love me.
Who is she? Who is he? She is my aunt. He is my uncle. And they love me.
Who are we? We are together. We are a family.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and repeat（课本 P41）
— Is that man your father?
— No, he isn't. He's my uncle. That tall man is my father.
— Really? He is handsome.
— Is she your aunt?
— Yes, she is. She's beautiful.

Lesson 3 · A. Read and tick（失物招领小故事）
Who lost the water bottle?
— Good morning, Mrs Chen. Can you help me?
— Sure, Bill. A boy lost this water bottle…
— What does he look like?
— He has short hair and big eyes.
— OK. I see. It's Li Ming's water bottle.
— Thank you very much. — You're welcome.

Story Time（小蝌蚪找妈妈）
— Excuse me. Are you our mother?
— She's green. She has big eyes. She can swim.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写wel·come —— well（好）+ come（来）的“联姻词”，但只写一个 l！✏️ 口诀：well 来 welcome，丢一个 l 才地道
音·发音/ˈwel.kəm/ 重音在前；🎧 跟读：You're welcome. · Welcome to my home! · a welcome guest
意·释义int. 不客气（You're welcome.）；v./n. 欢迎（Welcome to China 欢迎来中国）；adj. 受欢迎的（a welcome guest 受欢迎的客人）
用·句型① — Thank you very much. — You're welcome. (—非常感谢。—不客气。)
② Welcome to my home! (欢迎来我家！)
③ You are always welcome. (你随时受欢迎。)
④ A warm welcome! (热烈欢迎！)
拓·同族词You're welcome = You are welcome 的缩写；welcome mat 门口“欢迎垫”；welcome party 欢迎会；同义答语：No problem. / My pleasure. / Anytime.

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子

待客Welcome to my home! Please sit down.

形容词Help is always welcome. (帮助永远受欢迎。)
📋 功能句总结 · You're welcome 答谢链

问谢：Thank you very much.

答（标准）：You're welcome.

答（轻松）：No problem. / My pleasure. / Anytime.

⚠️ Welcome to + 地点：Welcome to Beijing!——to 后面直接加地点，别加 the！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

“You're welcome.” 是礼貌教育的最后一环：谢谢→不客气，链条才完整。建议在家玩“做客游戏”：孩子当主人开门说 Welcome to my home!，家长扮客人感谢帮忙，孩子答 You're welcome.；再看看自家门口地垫上有没有 WELCOME 字样，让礼貌英语长在生活里。

👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《Welcome Home, Grandpa》

📖 爷爷出差回来了！朵朵举着画冲到门口：“Welcome home, Grandpa!” 爷爷放下行李：“Oh, what a beautiful picture!” 原来朵朵画了一家人手拉手。爷爷问：“Is that me?” 朵朵点点头：“Yes! We are a family. You are always welcome.” 爷爷搂住她：“This is the best welcome ever.”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who came back? (Grandpa)
• What was in the picture? (The family holding hands)
• 问问孩子：客人来你家，你会用英语说哪句欢迎语？

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 答谢 ── Thank you → You're welcome. 不客气
├── 欢迎 ── Welcome to my home / school / China!
├── 形容词 ── a welcome guest 受欢迎的客人
├── 家族 ── welcome mat 欢迎垫 · welcome party 欢迎会
└── 拆词 ── wel(乐意) + come(来)：来得正是时候

🏛️ 词源故事

welcome 是英语里少见的“自带剧情”的词：古英语 wilcuma，由 wil（愿意、乐意，will 的老祖先）+ cuma（来的人）组成，本义是“来的正是时候、让人高兴的客人”；后来受 “well come”（来得好）的影响，拼写变成 welcome——字面就是“你来得真好！”
🎯 讲给孩子：说 You're welcome 时，等于在说“你谢得正好，我心甘情愿”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

西班牙语有句待客名言 “Mi casa es su casa.”（我家就是你家）；英语也有对应的温暖谚语 “East or west, home is best.”（东好西好，还是家最好）——一迎一归，welcome 和家永远是好朋友。

🎵 歌词里的 welcome

🎵 Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young《Our House》(1970)：传世经典，“Our house is a very very very fine house” 唱出了家的温暖——主人最常说的那句 “Welcome!” 就藏在这栋房子里。
🎵 互链：与 show around 页互链（Our House 同源，见 living room 页）；与 much 页互链（课本答谢对：Thank you very much. — You're welcome.）。

⚡ 冷知识

英美人家门口常铺一块写着 WELCOME 的地垫（welcome mat），客人一进门就踩在“欢迎”上——这是最日常的 welcome。回答谢谢也不止 You're welcome 一种：No problem（没问题）、My pleasure（我的荣幸）、Anytime（随时效劳）都很地道，换着用显得特别有礼貌！
