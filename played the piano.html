📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写play the piano —— 乐器前必须加 the！play + ed → played
✏️ 口诀：乐器“戴帽”（the），played the piano 才地道
音·发音/ˌpiˈænəʊ/ 三音节，重音在“呀”上：pi-A-no
🎧 跟读：played the piano · play the violin · play the guitar
意·释义弹钢琴（过去发生）。piano 钢琴；弹奏乐器用 play
用·句型① I played the piano at the school show. (我在学校汇演上弹了钢琴。)
② On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club. (课本同款句式！)
③ She played the piano for three years.
④ Did you play the piano last weekend?
拓·同族词play the + 乐器：the piano / the violin / the guitar
对比：play football / play games（球类游戏不加 the）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
乐器句I played the piano for my family.

现在对照I play the piano every day.
询问Did you play the piano at the party?
功能句总结 · the 的戴帽规则

戴帽：play the piano 🎹 / play the violin / play the drums
不戴帽：play football ⚽ / play chess ♟ / play computer games 🎮

口诀：乐器戴帽，球类棋类“裸奔”

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

如果孩子在学琴，练琴打卡改成英语版：I played the piano for 30 minutes. 家长追问 What did you play? 孩子答曲名。每天一句，the 的戴帽规则和过去式同步内化。注意孩子容易把 the 丢掉，说成 play piano。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 戴帽仪式
说短语时孩子做“戴帽子”动作：play the piano(戴!) · play chess(不戴) · play the guitar(戴!)
✏️ 关卡2 · 过去式填空
① She ___ (play) the piano yesterday. → played
② I can ___ the piano. → play
✅ 关卡3 · 小小演奏家
用家里的“乐器”（碗、笔盒）开一场演奏会，结束宣布 I played the piano on my bowl! 全家鼓掌
🔍 关卡4 · 听音猜乐器
家长说 violin / piano / guitar，孩子选对应乐器图或模仿演奏动作，得一颗星
🎙️ 关卡5 · 口语大挑战
录音打卡

📖 微型故事《The Kitchen Piano》

Nina 还没有自己的钢琴，但她有“锅碗瓢盆乐团”。周末家庭会上，Nina 用勺子敲出一首曲子，郑重宣布：I played the piano——kitchen piano! 全家笑翻，爸爸却认真鼓掌。生日那天，Nina 收到一张卡片：兑奖券一张——钢琴课一节。她抱着爸爸喊：Best prize ever! 从此，真钢琴上的每个音，她都敲得像那天一样开心。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What was Nina's “piano”? (Pots and bowls with a spoon)
• What was her birthday prize? (A piano lesson coupon)
• 问问孩子：What “instrument” can you make at home?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 原形 ── play the piano → played the piano
├── the 规则 ── 乐器加the · 球类不加
├── 乐器家族 ── violin 小提琴 · guitar 吉他 · drums 鼓
├── 课本同款 ── played the violin in a music club
└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── Piano Man 口琴+钢琴双绝

🏛️ 词源故事

piano 是意大利语“轻柔”的缩写——全名 pianoforte，“轻柔-响亮”：这件乐器的革命在于按键轻重能控制声音大小，之前的羽管键琴做不到。forte（响亮）至今还是音乐术语，弹得响就叫 forte。🎯 讲给孩子：piano 的名字就是它的说明书——“轻”和“响”都听你的。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Life is like a piano; what you get out of it depends on how you play it.” 🎹
生活像钢琴：你弹出什么，取决于你怎么弹。——英语流行格言。played the piano 的每次练习，都是在给生活调音。

🎵 歌词里的 played the piano

🎵 Billy Joel《Piano Man》(1973)：传世叙事经典，口琴与钢琴双绝，Sing us a song, you're the piano man——酒吧钢琴师的一夜群像。

🎵 与 play the piano 页互链（同源曲目）；本页与 played computer games / played chess 组成 play 过去式三兄弟。

⚡ 冷知识

现代钢琴约有88个键，但它“出生”在1700年前后——意大利乐器制造师克里斯托福里（Bartolomeo Cristofori）发明了能控制轻重的击弦机。从此音乐家可以在一件乐器上从耳语弹到呐喊——pianoforte 名不虚传。
