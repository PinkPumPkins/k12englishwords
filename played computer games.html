📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写play + ed —— play 以 ay 结尾，直接加 ed：played
✏️ 口诀：play 收尾加 ed，played games 笑嘻嘻
音·发音/pleɪd/ 一个音节，干净利落
🎧 跟读：played computer games · played games · play → played
意·释义玩电脑游戏（过去发生）。computer games = 电子游戏
用·句型① I played computer games for an hour. (我玩了一个小时电脑游戏。)
② He played computer games after homework. (写完作业后他玩了电脑游戏。)
③ Don't play computer games too long! (别玩太久！)
④ Did you play computer games last weekend?
拓·同族词play 三大件：games 游戏（无 the）· football 球类（无 the）· the piano 乐器（有 the！）
同单元互链：played the piano / played chess

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
时间管理I played computer games for half an hour.
顺序I played games after I finished my homework.
家庭规则Mum said, No more computer games today!
询问Did you play computer games on Sunday?
功能句总结 · play 的三个搭配

play + 游戏/球类：play computer games · play football（不加 the）
play the + 乐器：play the piano · play the violin（要加 the！）

口诀：球类游戏“裸奔”，乐器“戴帽”（the）🎩

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

英语规则可以借游戏讲：play computer games 和 play football 都不加 the，play the piano 要加 the——“乐器戴 the 帽”。同时约定游戏时间规则，用英语计时：Play for 30 minutes, then stop. 时间到说 Time's up! 让规则和英语一起生效。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · the 的裁判
家长说短语，孩子当裁判喊“要 the / 不要 the”：play piano(要!) · play football(不要) · play chess(不要)
✏️ 关卡2 · 过去式填空
① Yesterday he ___ (play) computer games. → played
② I often ___ chess with Dad. → play
✅ 关卡3 · 时间谈判
用英语约定游戏时间：Can I play computer games for ___ minutes? 谈成“交易”才开机
🔍 关卡4 · 排序成句
games / I / played / computer / yesterday → I played computer games yesterday.
🎙️ 关卡5 · 口语大挑战
录音打卡
🎤 示例：Last weekend I played computer games, but I also climbed a hill!

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The 30-Minute Deal》

Kevin 想玩电脑游戏，妈妈伸出三根手指：30 minutes, then stop. Deal? Kevin 击掌：Deal! 他玩得正酣，计时器响了，手痒啊！可他想起击掌的约定，关机、深呼吸，跑去找爸爸下棋。晚饭时妈妈悄悄对爸爸说：He kept his deal today. Kevin 听见了，心里比赢游戏还爽。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• How long could Kevin play? (30 minutes)
• What did Kevin do after the game time? (He played chess with Dad)
• 问问孩子：What is your 30-minute deal?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 原形 ── play computer games → played computer games
├── play 家族 ── games 无the · 球类 无the · 乐器 有the
├── 时间管理 ── for 30 minutes · after homework
├── 同单元 ── played the piano · played chess
└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── Pac-Man Fever 游戏热

🏛️ 词源故事

computer 来自拉丁语 computare（计算）——它的本职是“会计算的机器”。game 来自古英语 gamen（游戏、乐趣），play 来自古英语 plegan，本义“嬉戏、活动身体”。🎯 讲给孩子：computer games 连起来就是“用计算机器玩耍”——上世纪70年代起，它才真的变成家家户户的娱乐。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy.” ⚖️
只学习不玩耍，聪明孩子也变傻。——英语谚语。玩了 computer games，也别忘了 climbed a hill 和 jumped rope——play 要雨露均沾。

🎵 歌词里的 played computer games

🎵 Buckner & Garcia《Pac-Man Fever》(1982)：Billboard 前十，史上第一首“为电子游戏而唱”的热门歌曲，整首都在唱吃豆人的魔性循环。

🎵 与 play computer games 页互链（同源曲目）；1982年的“游戏热”，今天叫“电竞”。

⚡ 冷知识

吃豆人 Pac-Man 的设计师岩谷彻说，灵感来自一块被切掉一角的披萨——那个缺口成了吃豆人的嘴。1980年游戏上市后风靡全球，1982年甚至有了自己的冠军单曲 Pac-Man Fever。一块披萨，喂饱了整个游戏史。
