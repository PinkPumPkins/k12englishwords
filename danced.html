单词 danced
词性 v. 跳舞（dance的过去式）
音标 /dɑːnst/
难度 ⭐⭐⭐ (五年级)
原形 dance
拼写 e 结尾只加 d（不是 danceed）
📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写dance 以 e 结尾 → 过去式只加 d：danced
✏️ 口诀：e 结尾只添 d，danced 舞步美
音·发音/dɑːnst/ 一个音节，别读成“蛋斯特”两个音节
🎧 跟读：danced · dance · dancing
意·释义跳舞（过去发生）。dance 动词、名词都能当：have a dance 跳一支舞
用·句型① We danced at the party. (我们在派对上跳舞了。)
② We danced and watched cartoons all morning. (我们跳了一上午舞、看卡通。——课本原句！)
③ She danced with her friends.
④ Did you dance at Ann's birthday party?
拓·同族词dance → danced → dancing；dancer 舞者
生日会两大件：dance 跳舞 · sing 唱歌（sing → sang）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句We danced and watched cartoons all morning.
派对I danced with my friends at the party.
现在对照I often dance to music.
询问Did you dance at the party?
功能句总结 · 生日派对用语

邀请：May I have this dance?（能请你跳支舞吗？）
夸赞：You dance so well!（你舞跳得真好！）
生日歌：Happy birthday to you! 🎂
感谢：Thank you for the wonderful party!

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

danced 是“e 结尾只加 d”的典型样本，让孩子和 live→lived、like→liked 放一起记。在家放一首迪斯科，全家跳三分钟，跳完立刻说 We danced!，用动作锁住过去式。提醒孩子千万别写成 danceed。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 听音起舞
家长说 dance，孩子跳舞；说 danced，孩子摆结束姿势喊 Finished!
✏️ 关卡2 · 拼写门诊
danced / danceed / daced 哪个对？→ danced（e 结尾只加 d）
✅ 关卡3 · 过去式家族
dance → ____ · like → ____ · live → ____ → danced / liked / lived
🔍 关卡4 · 生活找一找
放一首歌跳30秒，跳完说 I danced! 再补一句 I listened to music too! 得一颗星
🎙️ 关卡5 · 口语大挑战
复述课本句并录音打卡

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The Robot Dance》

生日会上音乐响起，Max 不敢跳：I can't dance. 妹妹拉起他的手：Just move like a robot! Max 僵硬地摆动手臂——全场大笑，大家学起机器舞。那一晚所有人都 danced。回家后 Max 对妈妈说：Mum, I danced like a robot! 妈妈笑弯了腰。原来舞步没有对错，开心就好。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who taught Max the robot dance? (His little sister)
• How did Max dance? (Like a robot)
• 问问孩子：What dance would you do at a party?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 原形 ── dance → danced（e结尾只加d）
├── 词族 ── dancer 舞者 · dancing 动名词
├── 场景 ── party 派对 · birthday 生日会 · disco 迪斯科

└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── Last Dance 迪斯科女王的谢幕曲

🏛️ 词源故事

dance 来自古法语 dancier（跳舞），再往前追就查不清了——它的“出身”是英语常用词里的悬案之一，学者猜可能与动作、节拍有关，但没有定论。英语里 dance 既能跳舞也能指舞会：went to a dance 就是去参加舞会。🎯 讲给孩子：连词源学家都说不清 dance 从哪来，但全世界都会跳——快乐不需要户口。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
dancern. 舞者（dance + r）
dancingn. 跳舞（动名词，与 dancing 页同族）
dance to music跟着音乐跳舞
go to a dance去参加舞会

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Dance like nobody's watching.” 💃
跳舞吧，像没人看一样。——英语世界流传极广的格言。danced 的快乐不在于跳得好不好，而在于跳没跳。

🎵 歌词里的 danced

🎵 Donna Summer《Last Dance》(1978)：迪斯科女王传世曲，奥斯卡最佳原创歌曲——“最后一支舞，请留给我”。

🎵 与 dance 页互链（同源曲目）；danced 正是 Last Dance 唱完后的那个瞬间。

⚡ 冷知识

《Last Dance》真的拿过奥斯卡：1979年凭电影《Thank God It's Friday》获最佳原创歌曲奖。迪斯科女王 Donna Summer 从教堂唱诗班女孩一路跳成传奇——她年轻时选角落选、被告知“不够格”，后来全世界都在跳她的舞。
