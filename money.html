📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写money 5个字母，o 读 /ʌ/，别写成 many（许多）！
✏️ 口诀：money 和 many 长得像，o 和 a 分清爽！
音·发音/ˈmʌni/ 两音节，重音在前
🎧 跟读：get some money · spend money · pocket money
意·释义钱 💰（不可数名词：说 much money，不说 many moneys）
用·句型① Get some money from the bank. (从银行取些钱。)
② I save my pocket money. (我攒零花钱。)
③ Money doesn't grow on trees. (钱不是树上长出来的。)
④ How much money do you need? (你需要多少钱？)
拓·同族词pocket money 零花钱 · piggy bank 存钱罐；同义表达：cash 现金；本单元互链：bank 页

功能句总结 · money 不可数
❌ many moneys / a money → ✅ much money / a lot of money
How much money…?（问多少钱用 how much）
花钱三连：spend money 花钱 · save money 存钱 · get money 拿钱
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

money 不可数是考点：问多少钱用 How much money…?。给孩子开一个“零花钱实验”：每周 pocket money 分三罐——save（存）、spend（花）、share（分享），边贴英文标签边念，财商和英语一起长。

📖 微型故事《存钱罐里的三个愿望》

🐷 小猪罐满了，朵朵倒出硬币数了数。妈妈给了三个罐子：save、spend、share。朵朵把钱分好：“Some money to save, some to spend, and some to share with my friend!” 她用 share 罐给同桌买了一块橡皮——钱原来会让人开心两次：一次得到，一次给予。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• How many jars did Mum give? (三个)
• What are their names? (save / spend / share)
• 问问孩子：你的钱会怎么分？

🗺️ 思维导图

money 钱 ├── 语法 ── 不可数：much money ✔ / many money ❌ ├── 家族 ── pocket money · cash 现金 ├── 动词 ── spend 花 / save 存 / get 拿 ├── 词源 ── 拉丁语 moneta，来自朱诺神庙铸币厂 └── 互链 ── bank 页 · get 页

🏛️ 词源故事

money 来自拉丁语 moneta，源头是罗马的朱诺·莫内塔神庙——“莫内塔”意为“警示者朱诺”。罗马人在这座神庙里开铸币厂，神庙的名字渐渐就成了“钱”：法语 monnaie、英语 money 都从这里来。所以每个硬币上，其实都住着一位女神。

📜 名言 & 谚语

英语谚语：“Money doesn't grow on trees.”（钱不是从树上长出来的。）长辈用这句话告诉孩子：钱要靠劳动换，花之前想想它从哪儿来。

🎵 歌词里的 money

🎵 Pink Floyd《Money》(1973)：传奇专辑《月之暗面》名曲，开头就是收银机“叮叮当当”的音效，整首歌居然是少见的七拍子——钱的声音本身成了节奏。与 bank 页（21 Savage《Bank Account》）互链。

⚡ 冷知识

纸币其实不是纸：人民币的主要原料是棉短绒，美元约四分之三是棉、四分之一是麻——所以钞票又韧又防水，洗钞票时它常常“毫发无损”。
