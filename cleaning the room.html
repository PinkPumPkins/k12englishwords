📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Family Activities（P48-57）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and chant
— What are you doing?
— I'm watching TV.
— What is she doing?
— She's feeding the fish. / She's cooking dinner. / She's listening to music.
— What is he doing?
— He's cleaning the room.
— What are they doing?
— They're walking the dog.
— What are we doing?
— We're watering the plants.
（本课短语：cooking dinner 做饭 · walking the dog 遛狗 · watering the plants 浇花 · cleaning the room 打扫房间 · listening to music 听音乐 · watching TV 看电视 · feeding the fish 喂鱼）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and repeat（电话对话）
— Hello! This is Lily.
— Hi, Lily.
— What are you doing? Can you play with me?
— Sorry, not now. I'm watching TV. It's basketball, my favourite sport.
— What's your sister doing? Can she play with me?
— She's cleaning her room now, but we can all play in one hour.
— OK. Great!

Lesson 3 · A. Read and find（公园短文）
It's sunny today. Everyone is happy.
The children are playing in the park.
Wang Han is walking his dog. Zhao Qi is feeding the fish.
Li Le is flying a kite. Zhou Ming is playing football with his brother.
（run 的 -ing 形式 running 属于本单元进行时词族：双写 n 再加 ing）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写clean·ing the room —— clean 直接加 ing；room 只有一个 o。✏️ 口诀：clean 加 ing，房间亮晶晶；两个 o 一个 m，bathroom 好兄弟
音·发音/ˈkliːn.ɪŋ ðə ruːm/ ee 读长音 /iː/。🎧 跟读：cleaning the room · clean my desk · a clean window
意·释义正在打扫房间 🧹（clean 形容词'干净的'、动词'打扫'一词两用；room 房间）
用·句型① What is he doing? — He's cleaning the room. (他在打扫房间。)
② She's cleaning her room now. (她现在在打扫她的房间。)
③ Your room is so clean! (你的房间真干净！)
④ Let's clean the room together. (我们一起打扫房间吧。)
拓·同族词clean 打扫/干净 → cleaner 清洁工/更干净 · cleaning lady 保洁阿姨；同族家务：sweep the floor 扫地 · do the housework 做家务

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
进行时问答What is she doing? — She's cleaning her room.
约定等待She's cleaning her room now, but we can all play in one hour.
夸赞整洁Wow! Your room is clean and tidy.
分工合作I clean the desk, and you sweep the floor.
📋 功能句总结 · clean 与 tidy

clean＝干净（形容词）：The room is clean.　clean＝打扫（动词）：Clean the table. 擦干净桌子

clean and tidy：干净又整齐——夸房间的万能句。

相关家务短语：clean the room 打扫房间 · sweep the floor 扫地 · clean the window 擦窗户

⚠️ 拼写辨析：clean（干净）≠ clear（清澈/清楚），别混！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

打扫房间是进行时最佳实景课：孩子擦桌子就说 I'm cleaning my desk.，家长问 What are you doing? 让孩子用 -ing 句回答，干活学英语两不误。顺带引出 Lesson 2 的时间约定 She's cleaning her room now, but we can all play in one hour.——教孩子'忙完再玩'的表达。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The Clean Room Race》

📖 周日早上，妈妈宣布：'Room cleaning race! The cleanest room wins a sticker!' 兄妹俩冲回房间。Max 一边擦桌子一边喊：'I'm cleaning the room!' Lily 拿着小扫把：'I'm sweeping the floor!' 十分钟后，妈妈戴白手套摸了摸书架：'Clean and tidy! Both win!' 兄妹击掌：'Cleaning together is fun!'——原来比赛最好的奖品是一起干活。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What are the children doing? (They're cleaning their rooms.)
• Who won the race? (Both — the rooms were clean and tidy)
• 问问孩子：打扫时你想当'擦桌员'还是'扫地员'？用英语报个岗

🏛️ 词源故事

clean 来自古英语 clæne，本义'干净的、清澈的'；room 来自古英语 rūm，本义是'空间'——房间就是家里划出的一块'空间'。
🎯 讲给孩子：clean 作形容词是'干净的'，加 -er 变 cleaner 既可以指'更干净'，也可以指'清洁工'——把东西擦干净的人。

📜 名言 & 谚语

'Cleanliness is next to godliness.' 爱清洁近乎虔诚——经典英语谚语（常被归于18世纪牧师约翰·韦斯利）：把身边收拾干净，也是一种了不起的修养。

⚡ 冷知识

'春季大扫除'不只在过年——英语世界有 spring cleaning：冬天屋里烧煤又闷又黑，天气一暖就彻底开窗大扫除。还有个冷知识：第一台真空吸尘器发明于1901年，机器大到要停在街上，用长管子从窗户伸进屋里吸灰尘——那时的'打扫'才是大工程！
