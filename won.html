📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写win → won —— 不规则变化！不是 wined，更不是 winned
✏️ 口诀：i 变 o，win 变 won（和 one 同音）
音·发音/wʌn/ 一个音节，与 one /wʌn/ 完全同音
🎧 跟读：won the game · won the match · win → won → winning
意·释义获胜、赢（过去发生）。win + 比赛/奖品；击败某人用 beat
用·句型① My favourite team won the match. (我最喜欢的队赢了比赛。——课本原句！)
② I won the chess game! (我赢了棋局！)
③ She won first prize. (她得了一等奖。)
④ Did your team win or lose? — We won!
拓·同族词win → won → winning（双写n）
辨析：win + 比赛/奖 · beat + 对手：I beat Tom. = I won the game.

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
日常I won the game! Hooray!
辨析I won the game. I beat my brother.
询问Did you win the badminton match?
功能句总结 · win 与 beat

win + 赛事/奖项：won the match · won the game · won first prize 🏆
beat + 对手：beat my friend · beat Class 2

口诀：win 赢的是“事”，beat 赢的是“人”——别对对手说 I won you！（×）

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

won 是不规则过去式，也是孩子最容易写成 winned 的词。家庭比赛后立刻复述：You won! I lost! 胜负句每天都能用。同音梗记牢：won = one，“第一名”正好 won——谐音记忆一次到位。同时示范胜不骄：won 之后说 Good game, let's play again!

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 同音抢答
家长说 /wʌn/，孩子判断是 one(数字) 还是 won(赢了)——用手指比1或举双手欢呼
✏️ 关卡2 · 不规则门诊
win → ____ (won!) · run → ____ (ran!) · winned 为什么错？——win 不规则！
✅ 关卡3 · win/beat 分诊
I ___ the game / I ___ Tom → won / beat
🔍 关卡4 · 家庭赛场
任何比赛后用英语复盘：Who won? We won! / They beat us. Good game! 得一颗星

📖 微型故事《The Silver Medal Winner》

运动会跑步决赛，Ming 冲线时慢了半步——第二名。他低着头不想说话。爸爸举起手机里的照片：看，你冲线的样子像不像飞？Ming 笑了。第二天的棋类友谊赛，Ming 一路过关斩将，最后一局喊出 I won! 领奖时他说：Today I won, yesterday I lost. But I practice every day. 掌声响起来——输赢都是练习，奖牌只是打卡。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What place did Ming get in the race? (Second)
• What did Ming win the next day? (The chess game)
• 问问孩子：What will you do after you lose a game?

🏛️ 词源故事

win 来自古英语 winnan，本义是“战斗、苦干”——最早的“赢”不是靠运气，是打出来的。won 的拼写 i 变 o，是不规则变化的经典款，同族还有 sing→sang、swim→swam——元音一动，时间就回到过去。🎯 讲给孩子：won 里藏着一千年前“汗水”的味道。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Winners never quit and quitters never win.” 🏆
赢家永不放弃，放弃者永不赢。——常归于美式橄榄球名帅文斯·隆巴迪的名言。won 之前，谁都在坚持练习。

🎵 歌词里的 won

🎵 ABBA《The Winner Takes It All》(1980)：ABBA 传世金曲，The winner takes it all, the loser has to fall——胜负与人生的瑞典式咏叹。

🎵 与 win 页互链（同源曲目）；与 competition / prize 页同曲家族。won the match 的心情，ABBA 唱全了。

⚡ 冷知识

二战时，英国首相丘吉尔用“V”手势（掌心向外）鼓舞民心，V 就是 Victory（胜利）。这个手势从此风靡全球，今天运动员获胜时还会比V——一个手势，就是全人类都懂的 We won!
