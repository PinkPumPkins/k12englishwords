Get ready · 热身
— Do you often go to the zoo? What is your favourite animal?
— I like butterflies. They are insects. They can fly.

Lesson 1 · 课文短文（节选）
Lily and her classmates are going to see the animals in the zoo.
First, they are going to see the sharks. Sharks are fish. They can swim under the water. They are very big and like to eat small animals in the sea.
Then they want to see the snakes and the butterflies. Snakes are reptiles. They are very long and cold. Butterflies are beautiful animals. They are insects. They have six legs, and they can fly.
Next, they want to see some mammals, such as monkeys, pandas and tigers. Mammals give milk to their babies.
At last, they are going to see the penguins. Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They live on the ice. Look, how cute they are!

Lesson 1 · 分类知识（节选）
Reptiles have cold blood. They have scales. Most reptiles have four legs. All insects have six legs. Mother insects lay eggs. Most insects can fly.

Lesson 2 · 问答对话（节选）
— What kind of animals are monkeys?
— They are mammals.
— What can monkeys do?
— They can climb trees very well.
— What do monkeys like to eat?
— They like to eat peaches and bananas.

Lesson 3 · 短文 The Elephant（节选）
Elephants are mammals. They can be four metres tall. They don't eat meat. They only eat fruit, leaves and grass. They need a lot of food, so they spend most of their time eating. The elephant has two long, white teeth called tusks. It is the only animal with a trunk — a special long nose. The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut. The elephant is also the mammal with the biggest ears. They look like two big fans. Elephants live in Africa and Asia. They are very strong, very smart and of course, very, very big.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写kind，四个字母好记；ind组合读/aɪnd/。✏️口诀：善良的人kind，问种类也用kind
音·发音/kaɪnd/ 单音节，同find、mind押韵。
🎧 跟读：what kind of animals · a kind girl
意·释义n. 种类（what kind of...哪一种）；adj. 善良的（a kind heart一颗善心）
用·句型① What kind of animals are monkeys?（猴子是什么类别的动物？——课本原句）
② They are mammals.（它们是哺乳动物。——课本原句）
③ What kind of animals do you like?（你喜欢哪种动物？）
④ She is very kind.（她很善良。）
拓·同族词kinds复数；a kind of一种 · all kinds of各种各样的 · kind-hearted好心的；近义词：sort / type

功能句总结 · What kind of...? 万能问法

问动物：What kind of animals are monkeys?

问食物：What kind of food do you like?

问书：What kind of books do you read?

⚠️ 主语单数时动词用is：What kind of animal is a dolphin?

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

kind一词两义是本页核心。饭桌上玩“kind问答”：What kind of fruit do you like?孩子答完，反问他What kind of animals are cats?练课本句型；孩子帮忙做事时说You are so kind!——两个意思都“活”在生活里。

📖 微型故事《The Kind Zookeeper》

🦓 动物园来了位新饲养员Anna。动物们私下打听：“What kind of keeper is she?”（她是个怎样的饲养员？）小猴先试：“She gave me peaches — so kind!”小企鹅举手：“She made our ice bigger — kind!”大象最后慢悠悠地说：“She knows what kind of animals we are, and she loves us all the same.”于是大家把答案写在门口：Two kinds of kind here — she knows every kind of animal, and she is kind to every one.

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• “What kind of keeper is she?”是什么意思？(她是怎样的饲养员)
• 为什么说Anna身上有两种kind？(懂各种动物+心地善良)
• 问问孩子：What kind of friend do you like?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 义1种类 ── what kind of哪一种 · a kind of一种 · all kinds of各种 ├── 义2善良 ── a kind heart善心 · be kind to对……友善 ├── 课本句 ── What kind of animals are monkeys? — They are mammals. ├── 近义词 ── sort / type（种类） └── 反义词 ── unkind不友善的

🏛️ 词源故事

kind的两个意思同根——都来自古英语gecynd（天性、出身）。“种类”指“同一天性的一群”；“善良”则从“亲人般的天性”发展而来——对家人友善，本就是人的天性。kindred（亲属）也是这个家族的。
🎯 讲给孩子：kind从“出生天性”长出了两根枝丫。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Kindness is the language which the deaf can hear and the blind can see.”（善良是聋人能听见、盲人能看见的语言。）——广为流传的马克·吐温名言。kind是它的名词兄弟。

🎵 歌词里的 kind

🎵 Grand Funk Railroad《Some Kind of Wonderful》(1974)：摇滚经典，歌名some kind of wonderful（某种奇妙）里，kind的“种类”义在唱歌。
🎵 与mammal页、reptile页、insect页互链：What kind of animals are...?的答案就在这三页！

⚡ 冷知识

科学家已命名的鸟类约1万种、鱼类3万多种，还在不断发现新的kind；What kind of...?是英语里最高频的问句之一；注意kind问的是“类”，不是“数量”——问数量要用how many。
