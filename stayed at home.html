词性 v.phr. 待在家里（stay的过去式）
音标 /steɪd ət ˈhəʊm/
难度 ⭐⭐⭐ (五年级)
原形 stay at home
口语 stayed home 也可（省 at）
📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

✅ 原文经51jiaoxi英语朗读宝跟读页、好多云课文翻译页、zxxk课件与教案摘要多源交叉核对（智慧山源页访问故障未采信）。本词为本单元 Get ready 词表词，Lesson 1 歌谣问答出现 I stayed at home；Lesson 3 短文出现现在时对照 I usually stay at home。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写stay + ed —— stay 以 ay 结尾，直接加 ed：stayed
✏️ 口诀：stay 加 ed，stayed home 慢悠悠
音·发音/steɪd/ 一个音节，词尾 /d/ 轻收
🎧 跟读：stayed at home · stayed home（口语可省 at）
意·释义待在家里（过去发生）。stay 停留、保持
用·句型① — What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home. (歌谣原句！)
② I stayed at home because it rained. (因为下雨我待在家里。)
③ I usually stay at home on Saturday morning. (我通常周六上午待在家。——课本现在时原句！)
④ We stayed at home and watched TV.
拓·同族词stay → stayed → staying
家族：stay at home 待家 · stay in bed 卧床（Lesson 2 同款）· stay healthy 保持健康

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
歌谣句— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
课本句I usually stay at home on Saturday morning.
原因I stayed at home because it was rainy.
时态对照Last weekend I stayed at home; this weekend let's climb a hill!
功能句总结 · because 因果句

because 因果句：I stayed at home because it rained.（因为下雨，我待在家。）
对比句：I stayed at home, but my friend climbed a hill.
⚠️ because 接“原因”，英语里 because 和 so 不连用

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

stayed at home 不是“无聊”的代名词——陪孩子回忆一次宅家周末，用英语列清单：I stayed at home. I read books. I helped Mum cook. 孩子会发现“待在家”也有满满的过去式素材。下雨天正好用上 Lesson 2 的 It was rainy. I played in the rain.

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The Best Rainy Day》

周六下大雨，野餐计划泡汤，May 蔫在沙发上：Boring! 妈妈变出一箱旧照片：要不我们把“野餐”搬进客厅？毯子铺地上，三明治摆盘中，全家围着照片讲爸爸妈妈小时候的故事，笑倒一片。晚上 May 在日记里写：I stayed at home. But it was the best rainy day! 原来快乐不需要出门。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why did May stay at home? (It rained)
• What did the family do on the blanket? (A picnic and photo stories)
• 问问孩子：What can you do on a rainy day at home?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 原形 ── stay at home → stayed at home
├── 家族 ── stay in bed 卧床 · stay healthy 保持健康
├── 因果 ── because it rained
├── 时态对照 ── usually stay(现在) / stayed(过去)
└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── Sweet Home Alabama 家的方向

🏛️ 词源故事

stay 经古法语进入英语，本源可追到拉丁语 stare（站立）——“站着不动”就是停留。home 来自古英语 hām（家、村庄），英语地名后缀 -ham（如 Nottingham 诺丁汉）都是它的后代：一个 hām 就是一个“家”。🎯 讲给孩子：stay = 站着不动，home = 老窝，stayed at home 就是“在老窝站了一整天”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Home is where the heart is.” ❤️
心之所安，即是家。——英语谚语。stayed at home 的周末，也可以是心里最满的周末。

🎵 歌词里的 stayed at home

🎵 Lynyrd Skynyrd《Sweet Home Alabama》(1974)：传世摇滚，Sweet home Alabama! 一响起全场大合唱——“家”是摇滚乐永恒的副歌。

🎵 与 home 页互链（同源曲目）；stayed at home 与 home 是“动词短语 + 名词”的一家人。

⚡ 冷知识

英语造了个新词 staycation：stay + vacation，“宅家度假”——不旅行、在家里或家附近过的假期。这个词在2000年代油价上涨时爆红，如今已被词典正式收录。宅家不算浪费时间，它已经是“官方认证”的度假方式。
