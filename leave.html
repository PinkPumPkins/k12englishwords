📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写leave → left（过去式）；left 同时是“左边”！一词两义
✏️ 口诀：leave 走了变 left，left 还能指左边！
音·发音/liːv/ 长元音，别读成 live /lɪv/（住、生活）
🎧 跟读：leave home · Lucky leaves at 3:30
意·释义离开 🚪（leave home 离家；也指“留下、留给”：leave a message 留言）
用·句型① Lucky leaves at 3:30. (Lucky三点半出发。)
② We leave school at 4:30. (我们四点半放学离校。)
③ Please leave a message. (请留言。)
④ Don't leave your homework at home! (别把作业忘在家里！)
拓·同族词leave for + 目的地 出发去…；leave behind 留下/忘带；三单 leaves（课本原句！）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本Lucky leaves at 3:30.
出行The train leaves at 9:00.
留言Please leave a message after the beep.
叮嘱Don't leave your umbrella on the bus.
功能句总结 · leave 的两张面孔
① 离开：Lucky leaves at 3:30. / We leave for the park at 2:00.
② 留下：leave a message 留言 / leave the door open 让门开着
一个词两个方向：“走”的人和“留下”的东西，leave 都管！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

leave 和 left 的“一词两义”要专门讲：He left.（他走了）Turn left.（向左转）——left 既是 leave 的过去式又是“左边”。唱歌学最有效：Leave the Door Open 这首歌唱两遍，leave + open 的搭配就刻在耳朵里了。

📖 微型故事《不许开门的演唱会》

🚪 爸爸是 Silk Sonic 的歌迷，周末大扫除时循环播放 Leave the Door Open，边拖地边唱：“Leave the door open…” 明明提醒：“Dad, 安全第一，出门要 leave the door CLOSED!” 爸爸愣了两秒，全家笑成一团——歌可以唱，门可不能真开着。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What song does Dad like? (Leave the Door Open)
• Why can't the door be open? (安全)
• 问问孩子：leave the door open 和 close the door，你选哪个？为什么？

🗺️ 思维导图

leave 离开 ├── 离开 ── leave home · leave for the park ├── 留下 ── leave a message · leave the door open ├── 时态 ── leave → left（=左边！） ├── 词形 ── 三单 leaves（课本句 Lucky leaves at 3:30） └── 互链 ── before 页 · turn left 页

🏛️ 词源故事

leave 来自古英语 læfan，本义“让…留在原地、遗留”。后来词义转向“离开”——其实两义相通：离开一个地方，就是把它留在身后。leave behind（留下、忘带）、leave a message（留个话）都保留着最老的“留”字义。

📜 名言 & 谚语

户外运动爱好者的金句（作者已不可考）：“Take nothing but pictures, leave nothing but footprints.”（除了照片什么都不带走，除了脚印什么都不留下。）leave 在这里是最温柔的意思——只留 footprint。

🎵 歌词里的 leave

🎵 Silk Sonic《Leave the Door Open》(2021)：Bruno Mars 与 Anderson .Paak 组合的出道单曲，Billboard 冠军复古灵魂乐。与 door 页（Bob Dylan《Knockin' on Heaven's Door》）错开配歌；本单元和 before 页互链。

⚡ 冷知识

left 的“一词两义”常闹笑话：英语初学者看到 “He left.” 会翻译成“他在左边”。记住：left = leave 的过去式（走了）或方位词（左边），看句子里的搭档是谁——有 turn 就是“左”，有 home 就是“离开”。
