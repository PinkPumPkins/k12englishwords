📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写between = be（在）+ tweo（二）→“夹在两个中间”，看见 tween 就想起 two
✏️ 口诀：be + two 站中间，左手右手各一边！
音·发音/bɪˈtwiːn/ 三音节，重音在后：be-TWEEN
🎧 跟读：between the bank and the park · between us
意·释义在…（两者）之间 🌉（The bank is between the supermarket and the park.）
用·句型① The bank is between the supermarket and the park. (银行在超市和公园之间。)
② Sit between Mum and Dad. (坐在爸爸妈妈中间。)
③ There is a small shop between the two big buildings. (两栋大楼之间有家小店。)
④ It's a secret between you and me. (这是你我的秘密。)
拓·同族词between…and… 在…和…之间；in the middle of 在…中央；亲戚词：twins 双胞胎（两个！）、twenty 二十

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
方位The shop is between the bank and the school.
家庭I sit between my mum and my dad.
时间Come between 3:00 and 4:00.
情感It's a secret between you and me.
功能句总结 · between 和 and 是好搭档
方位：The bank is between the supermarket and the park.
银行在超市和公园之间。
时间：Open between 9:00 and 17:00. 营业时间9点到17点。
⚠️ between 后面 always 跟两个对象，用 and 连接！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

between 需要两个“参照物”。让孩子拿两个抱枕把自己夹在中间说 I'm between the two pillows，再换成超市和银行场景，把课本句型 The bank is between the supermarket and Sunday Park 玩出来。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《夹在中间的小面包》

🍞 熊爸爸买了一长条面包，熊哥哥和熊妹妹同时伸手。熊爸爸把面包切成三块：“Look! You are between the two halves of this bread — so this middle piece is yours!” 妹妹咬一口中间那块：“I like being between!” 全家都笑了。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• How many pieces did Dad cut? (三块)
• Which piece is the girl's? (中间那块)
• 问问孩子：一家三口散步时，你走在谁和谁之间？

🗺️ 思维导图

between 之间 ├── 方位 ── between the bank and the park ├── 时间 ── between 9:00 and 5:00 ├── 秘密 ── between you and me 咱俩的秘密 ├── 词根 ── be(在) + two(二) → 夹在两者中间 └── 亲戚 ── twins 双胞胎 · twenty 二十

🏛️ 词源故事

between 来自古英语 betwēonum：be（在）+ twēon（两者），和 two、twin（双胞胎）、twenty 是亲戚——它们都藏着古老的“二”。“夹在二中间”，就是 between 的全部秘密。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
betweenbe(在) + twēon(两者，古英语) → 在两者之间
twins双胞胎：between 的“二”家亲戚
twentytwenty(二十) = 两倍的十，同一个“二”词根

📜 名言 & 谚语

英语习语 “between a rock and a hard place”（进退两难，字面“卡在岩石和硬地之间”）——夹在中间不好受，英语用它形容两难处境。

🎵 歌词里的 between

🎵 Jimmy Eat World《The Middle》(2002)：Billboard 前五的治愈摇滚，副歌 “It just takes some time… everything will be alright” 唱给每一个“夹在中间”的孩子。与 next to 页、across from 页（本单元方位词三兄弟）互链。

⚡ 冷知识

两者之间用 between，三者及以上传统上用 among。但权威英语词典都注明：只要强调“两两之间”（比如四个人手拉手围成圈），三者以上也可以用 between——这条老规则正在变宽松。
