📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写stomach（胃）+ ache（疼）= stomachache。⚠️ stomach 里的 ch 发 /k/ 不发 /tʃ/！
✏️ 口诀：胃加疼就是胃疼，ch 咬牙读“科”。
音·发音/ˈstʌ.mə.keɪk/ 三个音节，重音最前
🎧 跟读：I've got a stomachache. · have a stomachache · often have stomachaches
意·释义胃疼、肚子疼（口语也常说 tummy ache）
用·句型① I've got a stomachache. (我胃疼。)
② I often have stomachaches. (我经常胃疼。)
③ What's wrong? — I have a stomachache. (怎么了？我胃疼。)
④ You should see a doctor. (你应该去看医生。)
拓·同族词ache 家族：headache 头疼 · toothache 牙疼 · backache 背疼

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
问诊— What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache.
频率I often have stomachaches.
建议You should see a doctor and drink some warm water.
家族stomachache / headache / toothache
📋 功能句总结 · 看病问答（本单元功能句核心）
问：What's wrong?（你怎么了？）
答：I've got a stomachache. / I have a headache.
追问：What should I do?
处方：You should see a doctor. / drink some warm water.
⚠️ I've got = I have got = I have，三种说法同一个意思！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

ache 家族是本单元拼写主线，建议打印一张 ache 火车图：车头 ache，三节车厢 stomach / head / tooth。孩子演小医生问 What's wrong?，家长演病人捂不同部位答 I've got a...，一轮游戏把三个 ache 词全过一遍。重点盯 stomach 的 ch 读 /k/。

👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《肚子里的抗议信》

💫 弗兰克的肚子住着一群小精灵，天天替他消化糖果。某天糖果超载，精灵们写了一封抗议信贴到肚皮上：Dear Frank, you ate too much candy! We are on strike!（罢工）弗兰克捂着肚子去找医生：I've got a stomachache! 医生笑着念出回信：You should drink some warm water, and you shouldn't eat too much candy. 精灵们这才复工。从此弗兰克的肚子安静又舒服。💌

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why did the tummy elves go on strike? (Frank ate too much candy)
• What was the doctor's advice? (Drink warm water; don't eat too much candy)
• 问问孩子：演一演 If your tummy writes you a letter, what does it say?

🏛️ 词源故事

stomach 的旅程很长：希腊语 stomachos 本指食道入口、喉咙口，经拉丁语 stomachus 进入英语，才慢慢专指“胃”。ache 来自古英语 acan，是“持续地疼”。两个词一拼，stomachache 就是“胃在持续地疼”。
🎯 讲给孩子：stomach 这个词先指喉咙，后指胃——中途还搬过一次家。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
stomach胃：The stomach helps digest food.
ache疼痛（持续）：My head aches.
have a stomachache胃疼（英式常说 have）
I've got a stomachache课本句式：I've got = I have

📜 名言 & 谚语

“An apple a day keeps the doctor away.” 🍎 一天一苹果，医生远离我。—— 英语世界最著名的健康谚语：与其疼了喊 stomachache，不如天天水果加洗手，把医生“挡”在门外。

🎵 歌词里的 stomachache

🎵 Billy Ray Cyrus《Achy Breaky Heart》(1992)：Billboard 前五的舞曲热潮，achy 就是 ache 的形容词形态——ache 家族在歌名里开派对。
🔗 与 headache 页互链（同曲）；与 toothache 页互链（同曲）——ache 三兄弟共用一首歌。

⚡ 冷知识

小朋友说肚子疼，英语口语常说 tummy ache（tummy 是幼儿语的肚子）；医生写病历则用正式说法 abdominal pain（腹痛）。同一个疼，三种礼服：tummy ache 童语、stomachache 课本语、abdominal pain 医生语——从幼儿园听到医院全通关。
