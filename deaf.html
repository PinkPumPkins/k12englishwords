📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写deaf —— d + eaf；ea 在这里读 /e/，不是 /iː/！
✏️ 口诀：deaf 短短五个字母，ea 读短音。
音·发音/def/ 单音节，结尾 f 轻擦；别读成 /diːf/
🎧 跟读：the deaf 聋人 · a deaf child · deaf and blind
意·释义聋的 👂（听不见或听力严重受损；the deaf = 聋人总称）
用·句型① Helen could not hear. She was deaf. (海伦听不见，她失聪了。)
② She is famous for helping the deaf and blind. (她以帮助聋人和盲人闻名。)
③ Sign language helps deaf people talk. (手语帮助聋人交流。)
④ Be patient with deaf people. (对听障人士要有耐心。)
拓·同族词deaf 聋的 → deafness 耳聋（n.）；the deaf 聋人总称；手语 sign language

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.
关心Sign language helps deaf people communicate.
尊重表达She is hard of hearing.（她听力不好——委婉说法。）
总称句We should help the deaf, the blind and the old.（the+形容词=一类人）
📋 功能句总结 · the + 形容词 = 一类人

the deaf：聋人（总称）；the blind 盲人；the old 老人；the rich 富人。

课本句：She is famous for helping the deaf and blind.（她以帮助聋人和盲人闻名。）

⚠️ the deaf 是复数概念：The deaf need our help.

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

这页要教的不只是单词，还有尊重。三件事：1) 教孩子“和听障朋友说话要面对对方、放慢速度”；2) 一起学两个手语动作（“谢谢”：拇指弯曲点两下）；3) 讲海伦·凯勒：听不见看不见，却学会读书写字、大学毕业、写了很多书。孩子的同理心，就从“换个方式沟通”开始。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《不一样的耳朵》

社区来了一位新邻居，爷爷耳朵听不太清。乐乐说话他总没反应，乐乐以为他生气了。妈妈说：“He is deaf. 他只是需要你面对他、说慢一点。” 乐乐试了：放慢、加手势——爷爷笑了，回了乐乐一个大拇指和一句手语“谢谢”。乐乐回家学了一晚上手语。原来耳朵可以不一样，心对齐就行。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why didn't the neighbour answer? (Because he was deaf.)
• How did Lele talk with him? (Slowly, with gestures, face to face.)
• 问问孩子：如果遇到听障朋友，你想先学哪句手语？

🏛️ 词源故事

deaf 是个日耳曼老词：古英语 dēaf，和德语 taub 是远亲，几千年来几乎没离开过“听不见”这个意思。the deaf（聋人总称）这种“形容词前加 the 指一类人”的用法，英语里延续了上千年。
🎯 讲给孩子：有些词古老，是因为人类一直需要它。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“The best and most beautiful things in the world cannot be seen or even touched. They must be felt with the heart.” 💗
世上最美好的东西，眼睛看不见，手摸不着，要用心去感受。—— 课本名人海伦·凯勒：又聋又盲的她，写下了这句话。

🎵 歌词里的 deaf

🎵 Simon & Garfunkel《The Sound of Silence》（1964）：传奇民谣经典——“Hello darkness, my old friend”，一首关于“无声”的歌，让更多人想起聋人朋友的世界。
🔗 与 blind 页互链：the deaf and blind 正是课本里海伦·凯勒帮助的人；与 quiet 页同曲（安静与无声，一体两面）。

⚡ 冷知识

手语不是世界通用的——中国手语和美国手语差别很大，就像中文和英文。失聪的贝多芬在听不见的情况下写出了《第九交响曲》，首演时他听不见掌声，直到乐手把他转过身。声音会缺席，但音乐和心意不会。
