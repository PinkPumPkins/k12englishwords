Get ready · 热身
— Do you often go to the zoo? What is your favourite animal?
— I like butterflies. They are insects. They can fly.

Lesson 1 · 课文短文（节选）
Lily and her classmates are going to see the animals in the zoo.
First, they are going to see the sharks. Sharks are fish. They can swim under the water. They are very big and like to eat small animals in the sea.
Then they want to see the snakes and the butterflies. Snakes are reptiles. They are very long and cold. Butterflies are beautiful animals. They are insects. They have six legs, and they can fly.
Next, they want to see some mammals, such as monkeys, pandas and tigers. Mammals give milk to their babies.
At last, they are going to see the penguins. Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They live on the ice. Look, how cute they are!

Lesson 1 · 分类知识（节选）
Reptiles have cold blood. They have scales. Most reptiles have four legs. All insects have six legs. Mother insects lay eggs. Most insects can fly.

Lesson 2 · 问答对话（节选）
— What kind of animals are monkeys?
— They are mammals.
— What can monkeys do?
— They can climb trees very well.
— What do monkeys like to eat?
— They like to eat peaches and bananas.

Lesson 3 · 短文 The Elephant（节选）
Elephants are mammals. They can be four metres tall. They don't eat meat. They only eat fruit, leaves and grass. They need a lot of food, so they spend most of their time eating. The elephant has two long, white teeth called tusks. It is the only animal with a trunk — a special long nose. The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut. The elephant is also the mammal with the biggest ears. They look like two big fans. Elephants live in Africa and Asia. They are very strong, very smart and of course, very, very big.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写fan，三个字母超好写。✏️口诀：扇子fan一扇，“粉丝”fan跟着跑
音·发音/fæn/ 单音节。
🎧 跟读：two big fans · a football fan
意·释义n. 扇子（电扇）；迷、粉丝（I am a fan of...我是……的粉丝）
用·句型① They look like two big fans.（它们看起来像两把大扇子。——课本原句，指大象耳朵）
② I am a fan of pandas.（我是熊猫迷。）
③ My dad is a football fan.（我爸是球迷。）
④ Turn on the fan, please.（请打开风扇。）
拓·同族词fans复数；fan club粉丝俱乐部 · fan letter追星信；同单元：elephant的big ears就像fans

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句Its ears look like two big fans.
粉丝I am a fan of monkeys.
电器Turn on the fan, please.
球迷My mum is a basketball fan.
功能句总结 · look like看起来像

比喻句：They look like two big fans.（耳朵像扇子）

仿写：The moon looks like a banana. / His eyes look like stars.

粉丝句：I am a fan of...（of + 名词）

⚠️ look like + 名词；look + 形容词（You look happy!）——别混！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

fan的两义一次讲透：大象耳朵像扇子（实物义），孩子是自己的“粉丝”（抽象义）。让孩子说“I am a fan of...”（动物、球队、书都行），再把家里的电风扇指给他看——一词两义从此打通。

📖 微型故事《The Elephant Fan Club》

🐘 夏天太热，小动物们排队找大象Andy。小猴突然喊：“Your ears look like two big fans!”Andy扇了扇耳朵，凉风扑面：“Welcome to the Elephant Fan Club — I'm the fan, and you're my fans!”大家哈哈大笑：“You are the fan, we are the fans!”小企鹅掏出小本本：“So fan means both a tool and a person who loves someone?”Andy点点头：“In English, cool wind and true love go together!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 大象的耳朵像什么？(Two big fans)
• 这里的fan和fans分别指什么？(扇子/粉丝)
• 问问孩子：What are you a fan of?

🏛️ 词源故事

fan（扇子）来自拉丁语vannus（扬谷的簸箕）。fan（粉丝）一般认为是fanatic（狂热者）的缩写——19世纪美国棒球场里，为心爱球队“疯狂”的观众自称the fans，从此流行开来。
🎯 讲给孩子：从“扇风”到“狂热”，fan走了一条“越吹越热”的路。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“We are the champions, my friends.”（我们是冠军，我的朋友们。）——Queen《We Are the Champions》副歌开嗓第一句。台下挥舞的双臂，正是fans的海洋。

🎵 歌词里的 fan

🎵 Queen《We Will Rock You》(1977)：跺脚+拍手的节奏就是为现场fans设计的，全场跺脚拍手=粉丝的海洋。
🎵 与elephant页互链：大象的大耳朵就是两把天然的big fans！

⚡ 冷知识

大象扇耳朵不只是为了凉快——耳朵上布满血管，扇动能给血液“物理降温”；人体最大的散热器官是皮肤；“粉丝”这个词，正是从英文fan音译到中文的。
