📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写too much 由两个小词组成：too（过于）+ much（多）。写的时候别把 too 写成 to！
✏️ 口诀：两个 o 的 too 才“过于”，一个 o 的 to 是“到”。
音·发音/tuː mʌtʃ/ 注意 much 的 /ʌ/ 短促开口
🎧 跟读：too much candy · too much TV · too many cakes
意·释义太多、过度（后面接不可数名词：candy、water、TV time）
⚠️ too many + 可数名词复数：too many cakes
用·句型① Don't eat too much candy. (别吃太多糖果。)
② I watch too much TV. (我看电视太多。)
③ Eating too much meat is bad for you. (吃太多肉对你不好。)
④ Thanks a lot, but that's too much. (多谢，但太多了。)
拓·同族词反义伙伴：just enough 刚刚好；近义：a lot of 许多（中性语气）；too much 自带“过了头”的批评语气，劝告时最常用

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
劝告You shouldn't drink too much juice.
提问Do you eat too much candy?
描述I watched too much TV last night.
对比too much water（不可数）/ too many sweets（可数）
📋 功能句总结 · too much vs too many
看后面接的名词：
too much + 不可数名词：too much candy · too much water · too much homework
too many + 可数名词复数：too many cakes · too many books · too many apples
⚠️ 口语里 too much 还能表示“太夸张了”：That's too much!
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

本单元的劝告句型 You shouldn't eat too much... 在家就能演：拿糖果罐和水果盘，孩子演小护士用英语劝家人——别吃太多糖 (You shouldn't eat too much candy)、多吃水果 (You should eat more fruit)。重点盯 too 的拼写和 much/many 的分工。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 听音分类
家长念词，孩子用举手势判断 too much（不可数）/ too many（可数）：candy → too much；cakes → too many；water → too much
✏️ 关卡2 · 填空闯关
Don't eat too ______ candy. (much)
Don't eat too ______ cakes. (many)
✅ 关卡3 · 课本句补全
You shouldn't eat too ______ meat. You should eat ______ vegetables and fruit. (much; more)
🔍 关卡4 · 生活找一找
找出家里一样要适量的东西（糖、盐、饮料），说一句 Don't eat/drink too much ______! 说对得一颗星
🎙️ 关卡5 · 口语大挑战
用 You shouldn't... too much. 给自己定一条健康规矩并录语音打卡
🎤 示例：I shouldn't play too much on the computer.

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《太多先生的烦恼》

💫 小镇上住着一位太多先生（Mr. Too Much）：喝水要喝 too much cola，睡前要看 too much TV。一天他捂着肚子去看医生：I've got a stomachache! 医生笑了：You shouldn't eat too much candy, and you shouldn't drink too much cola. 太多先生回家写下新规矩：A little is fine, too much is trouble!（一点点刚好，太多就麻烦）🍎

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Mr. Too Much drink too much of? (Cola)
• What was wrong with him? (He had a stomachache)
• 问问孩子：What do you eat too much of? 试着用英语说一句劝告

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 搭配 ── too much + 不可数：candy / water / TV
├── 区分 ── too many + 可数复数：cakes / books
├── 句型 ── You shouldn't eat too much...
├── 近义 ── a lot of（中性）· plenty of（充足）
└── 口语 ── That's too much!（太夸张了）

🏛️ 词源故事

too 来自古英语 tō，本义是“到那个程度”，后来分化出两个意思：一个是“也”（Me too!），一个是“过于”（too much）。much 则来自中世纪英语 muche（多、大量）。too much 字面就是“多到过头了”——英语用一个短词组，把适量“的反面”说得明明白白。
🎯 讲给孩子：too 是一对双胞胎——“也”和“过于”，too much 里住的是“过于”这个。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
too much + 不可数名词too much candy / water / juice（糖、水、果汁没法一个个数）
too many + 可数名词复数too many cakes / eggs / books（蛋糕、蛋能数）
much 单独用书面味重：Much of it is true.（其中许多是真的）
a lot of可数不可数都接：a lot of candy / a lot of candies

📜 名言 & 谚语

“You can have too much of a good thing.” 🍬 好东西过了头也会变成坏事。—— 英语流传已久的老话，和课本的 You shouldn't eat too much candy 是同一个道理。

🎵 歌词里的 too much

🎵 Spice Girls《Too Much》(1998)：辣妹组合的圣诞冠军单曲，Billboard 冠军；too 的“过于”味在歌名里拉满。
🔗 与 much 页互链（同曲《Too Much》——much 讲“多”，too much 讲“太多”）。

⚡ 冷知识

世界卫生组织建议每天添加糖最好控制在约25克（大约6茶匙）以内——一听可乐的含糖量就可能超标。课本 Fun Time 也是这个口径：We need only a little fat and sugar, such as oil, candy, ice cream and cake. We shouldn't eat too much. “一点点没问题，太多会出事”，正是 too much 的使用说明书。
