📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写next = near（近）的古最高级，本义“最近的”→ next to 即“紧挨着最近的一个”
✏️ 口诀：next to 排队站，肩并肩，不隔街！
音·发音/nekst/ 词尾 -xt 要连读，to 弱读 /tə/
🎧 跟读：next to the bank · next to me
意·释义紧邻、在…旁边 🏦（中间不隔东西：The bank is next to a restaurant.）
用·句型① The bank is next to the restaurant. (银行紧挨着餐馆。)
② Sit next to me. (坐我旁边。)
③ The post office is next to Joy's home. (邮局紧邻Joy家。)
④ Who is the girl next to the window? (窗户旁边的女孩是谁？)
拓·同族词next week 下周 · next door 隔壁 · next stop 下一站；近义词：beside / by

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
方位The post office is next to the school.
教室Please sit next to your partner.
出行Get off at the next stop.
生活My best friend lives next door.
功能句总结 · 说位置
A 在 B 旁边：A is next to B.
The bus stop is next to the bank. 公交站在银行旁边。
三兄弟对比：
next to 紧挨着（不隔东西）· across from 隔街相对 · between 在两者之间
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

next to 最容易和 across from 混。在家用积木或玩偶摆位置：紧贴摆放说 next to，隔开一拳摆 across from，两块积木夹住一个摆 between。摆一摆，比读十遍记得牢。

📖 微型故事《谁坐在我旁边》

🚌 开学第一天，小美上校车，第一排空着两个位子。她刚坐下，一个男孩问她：“Is this seat next to yours?” 小美点头：“Yes! Sit next to me!” 一路聊天成了好朋友。后来他们发现，两人家也只隔一堵墙——真是 next door 的 next to！

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who sat next to Xiaomei? (一个男孩)
• What did she say? (Sit next to me!)
• 问问孩子：你的同桌 next to 你吗？

🗺️ 思维导图

🏛️ 词源故事

next 来自古英语 nēahst，是 near（近）古老的最高级——“最近的那个”。所以 next to 不是随便的“旁边”，而是“紧贴着的旁边”：离你最近、中间不隔任何东西。next week（下一周）也是“离现在最近的那一周”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

英语里常用 “next to nothing” 表示“几乎没有”：The ticket cost next to nothing.（这张票几乎没花钱。）下次省了钱，就可以用上这个地道说法。

🎵 歌词里的 next to

🎵 Emeli Sandé《Next to Me》(2012)：英国灵魂女声金曲（知名度中上，已标注），副歌一遍遍唱 “You will find him next to me”，温暖守候直击人心。与 across from 页（The Beatles《Across the Universe》）互链。

⚡ 冷知识

在英语国家填表格时常见 next of kin（直系亲属、最近亲属）——next 在这里就是“最近”的本义。坐飞机时 “next to the window”（靠窗）也是空乘常说的句子。
