📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写watch + ed —— watch 以 ch 结尾，过去式直接加 ed（ed 读 /t/）
✏️ 口诀：watch 看完加 ed，watched TV 坐沙发
音·发音/wɒtʃt/ 一个音节干脆利落，词尾 tsh 轻碰即止
🎧 跟读：watched TV · watched a film · watched a match
意·释义看电视（过去发生）。watch 指专注地看（电视、比赛、电影），see 泛泛地看
用·句型① I watched TV last night. (我昨晚看电视了。)
② We watched a football match together. (我们一起看了一场足球赛。)
③ — Did you watch TV? — Yes, I did.
④ My grandpa watches TV every day. (爷爷每天都看电视。)
拓·同族词watch → watched → watching；watchman 守夜人
辨析：watch TV 看电视 / see a film 看电影 / read a book 看书——英语的“看”分场合！

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
过去式I watched TV with my dad last night.
频率I watch TV for half an hour every day.
课本同源We watched a football match on Sunday.
询问Did you watch TV last weekend?
功能句总结 · watch / see / look / read 的“看”

watch：专注看动态 → watch TV / watch a match 👀
see：看见、看电影 → see a film
look at：盯着看 → look at the blackboard
read：看（书报）→ read books 📖

⚠️ 英语的“看”要选对动词，不能全用 look！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

孩子容易把“看电视”说成 look TV 或 see TV。跟孩子定一个家庭规则：完成作业才能 watch TV，用英语说 It's TV time! / No TV today. 再把过去式嵌进日常对话：What did you watch? — I watched a cartoon.

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The TV-Free Saturday》

周六早上，Ann 打开电视，遥控器却没电了！Oh no! 没有电视怎么看动画片？爸爸提议：我们来演“电视节目”吧！Ann 当主持人，爸爸播天气预报，妈妈当歌唱家，小狗汪汪当嘉宾，全家笑成一团。晚上来电了，Ann 却说：Let's play “TV” again!——原来最好看的节目在家里。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why couldn't Ann watch TV? (The remote had no battery)
• What did the family do instead? (They acted out “TV shows”)
• 问问孩子：If there were no TV, what would you do?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 原形 ── watch TV → watched TV
├── 看什么 ── cartoon 动画 · match 比赛 · film 电影
├── 辨析 ── watch TV ✔ · see a film ✔（别混）
├── 句型 ── What did you watch? → I watched a cartoon.
└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── Video Killed the Radio Star

🏛️ 词源故事

watch 来自古英语 wæccan，本义是“醒着守夜”——守夜人 watchman 就是这么来的，所以 watch 自带“专注盯着”的味道。TV 是 television 的缩写：tele- 是希腊语“远”，vision 是拉丁语“看”，合起来“从远处看”；望远镜 telescope 用的是同一个“千里眼”前缀。🎯 讲给孩子：watched TV = 过去“守”在电视前。

📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
watchmann. 守夜人/门卫（watch + man）
wristwatchn. 手表（wrist 手腕 + watch）
televisionn. 电视（tele 远 + vision 看）
viewern. 观众（view + er）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Television is chewing gum for the eyes.” 📺
电视是眼睛的口香糖。——常归于美国喜剧演员 Fred Allen 的著名调侃。提醒我们：watched TV 之外，还要出门 climbed a hill！

🎵 歌词里的 watched TV

🎵 The Buggles《Video Killed the Radio Star》(1979)：1981年8月1日 MTV 开台播放的第一支MV，唱的是“视频时代来了，电台明星谢幕了”——电视接管娱乐的预言歌。

🎵 与 make a video call 页互链（同源曲目）；watched TV 的 watch，正是当年全家守在屏幕前的动作。

⚡ 冷知识

遥控器改变了一切：最早的电视机要走到机器前拧旋钮换台，有了遥控器，人才“钉”在沙发上。英语还造了个词 couch potato（沙发土豆），专指整天躺着看电视的人——你的周末是 watched TV 型，还是 climbed a hill 型？
