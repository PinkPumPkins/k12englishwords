Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and chant
— What are you doing?
— I'm watching TV.
— What is she doing?
— She's feeding the fish. / She's cooking dinner. / She's listening to music.
— What is he doing?
— He's cleaning the room.
— What are they doing?
— They're walking the dog.
— What are we doing?
— We're watering the plants.
（本课短语：cooking dinner 做饭 · walking the dog 遛狗 · watering the plants 浇花 · cleaning the room 打扫房间 · listening to music 听音乐 · watching TV 看电视 · feeding the fish 喂鱼）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and repeat（电话对话）
— Hello! This is Lily.
— Hi, Lily.
— What are you doing? Can you play with me?
— Sorry, not now. I'm watching TV. It's basketball, my favourite sport.
— What's your sister doing? Can she play with me?
— She's cleaning her room now, but we can all play in one hour.
— OK. Great!

Lesson 3 · A. Read and find（公园短文）
It's sunny today. Everyone is happy.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写every·one —— every（每一）＋ one（一个），合成一个词指'每个人'。✏️ 口诀：every 加 one，人人算一个，动词加 s 别忘了
音·发音/ˈev.ri.wʌn/ 三个音节，重音在首。🎧 跟读：everyone is here · Everyone is happy. · everyone in my family
意·释义人人；每个人 🌍（＝everybody；作主语时谓语用第三人称单数：Everyone is here。合写 everyone 只指人；分开写 every one 还能指物）
用·句型① Everyone is happy. (大家都很高兴。)
② Everyone in my family likes music. (我家每个人都喜欢音乐。)
③ Good morning, everyone! (大家早上好！)
④ Is everyone here today? (今天大家都到了吗？)
拓·同族词复合不定代词家族：someone/somebody 某人 · anyone/anybody 任何人 · nobody 没有人 · everybody＝everyone

📋 功能句总结 · everyone 的三单铁律

Everyone＋is/likes/has：Everyone is happy. ✓（不是 are！）

打招呼：Good morning, everyone! 课堂上天天用

指人：everyone 只指人；every one（分开写）可指物：every one of the books

⚠️ everyone 直接接形容词/动词，不加 of：Everyone is happy ✓

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

'everyone' 是班级高频词：上课问好 Good morning, everyone!、点名 Is everyone here? 都能练。在家玩'家庭播报'：指着全家人说 Everyone is happy today!，让孩子体会 everyone 当单数用、谓语加 s 的规则——这是考试高频点，也是最容易说成 Everyone are 的错误。

📖 微型故事《The Sun Says Good Morning》

📖 晴天的早晨，阳光跳进教室。老师微笑：'Good morning, everyone!' 全班齐声：'Good morning!' 老师说：'It's sunny today. Everyone is happy!' 只有窗户边的 Tom 低着头——他的新铅笔不见了。大家悄悄帮忙找，Lily 在讲台下摸到一支：'Is this yours?' Tom 跳起来：'Yes! Thank you, everyone!' 阳光下，现在，everyone is really happy.

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What was the weather like? (Sunny)
• What did Tom lose? (His new pencil)
• 问问孩子：全班帮忙时该说哪句？学一学 Thank you, everyone!

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 构成 ── every＋one ＝ 人人
├── 同义 ── everybody（everyone 只指人）
├── 语法 ── 谓语三单：Everyone is/likes/has
├── 家族 ── someone · anyone · nobody · everything
└── 课本句 ── It's sunny today. Everyone is happy.

🏛️ 词源故事

everyone 是两个老词的'牵手'：every 来自古英语 ǣfre ǣlc（永远·每个），one 来自古英语 ān。复合不定代词家族（someone / anybody / nobody / everybody）都是英语用'任意词＋one/body'像搭积木一样批量造出来的。
🎯 讲给孩子：everyone 合写只指人，every one 分开写连物也能指——一个空格，两种本领。

📜 名言 & 谚语

'Many hands make light work.' 人多好办事——经典英语谚语（14世纪就有了）：everyone 都出一份力，重活也会变轻，教室大扫除时喊这句最应景。

🎵 歌词里的 everyone

🎵 USA for Africa《We Are the World》（1985）：45位巨星为慈善合唱的史诗，'We are the world, we are the children'——人人参与，正是 everyone 的主题曲。
🎵 互链：与本单元 children 页同一句歌词遥相呼应，两页连唱更有气势！（歌曲名单沿用）

⚡ 冷知识

everyone 作主语是单数（Everyone is here），但英语口语里常用 they 来回指：Everyone has their own seat.——早在莎士比亚时代作家们就这么用了，如今已是规范用法。另外，'Good morning, everyone!' 是英语课堂出现频率最高的话之一，全世界的小学每天早上都要说上亿遍。
