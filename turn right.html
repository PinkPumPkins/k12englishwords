📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写turn(转) + right(右) → 向右转；right 和 write（写）同音不同形
✏️ 口诀：right 右边，也是“正确”——右手是正确的手！
音·发音/raɪt/ 长双元音，注意与 write 同音
🎧 跟读：turn right at the first crossroads
意·释义向右转 ↱（口令词）
用·句型① Turn right at the first crossroads. (在第一个路口右转。)
② Go straight. Then turn right. (直走，然后右转。)
③ It's on your right. (它在你右手边。)
④ You can't turn right at this crossroads. (这个路口不能右转。)
拓·同族词right 三兄弟：右边 / 正确 / 权利（rights）；right hand 右手 · right-hand man 得力助手

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
指路Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads.
位置It's on your right.
口令Turn right! Now you're facing the bank!
安全Look right, look left, then cross.
功能句总结 · on your right
指到为止：…turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.
就在你右手边。
左右对调：It's on your left. 就在你左手边。
⚠️ 指路时说 on your right，说的是“对方”的右边！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

right 的三个意思（右/正确/权利）一次讲清：举起右手说 This is my right hand，再竖大拇指说 You are right!。指路游戏里强调 It's on your right——用对方的左右来指路，是方位感的进阶训练。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《右转右转，方向对了》

🚗 爸爸开车去新的图书馆，明明当导航：“Go straight… turn right at the first crossroads!” 爸爸照做，图书馆果然在右手边：“It's on your right!” 明明得意地说：“Right turn, right place — two rights make it right!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who gave directions? (明明)
• Where was the library? (On their right)
• 问问孩子：右转后看到了什么？用 on your right 说一句。

🗺️ 思维导图

turn right 向右转 ├── 指路 ── turn right at the first crossroads ├── 家族 ── turn left 向左转 · go straight 直行 ├── 三义 ── 右边 / 正确 (You are right!) / 权利 ├── 同音 ── write 写（同音异形） └── 互链 ── turn left 页 · go straight 页 · south 页同曲

🏛️ 词源故事

right 来自古英语 riht，本义“笔直的、正确的”——多数人惯用右手，“正确之手”就是右手，于是 right 又有了“右边”的意思。left 是“弱手”，right 是“正确之手”，左右两个词背后，藏着古人对世界的直白观察。

📜 名言 & 谚语

英语谚语：“Two wrongs don't make a right.”（两个错凑不成一个对。）这里的 right 是“正确”——但它同时是“右边”，和本页的 turn right 正好组成一词双关。

🎵 歌词里的 turn right

🎵 Village People《Go West》(1979)：迪斯科经典，副歌 “Go West” 齐声高唱，方向感十足；1993年 Pet Shop Boys 翻唱再爆红。与 south 页（同曲互链）、本单元 turn left 页互链。

⚡ 冷知识

为什么握手用右手？一般认为：右手是多数人的持武器之手，伸右手等于向对方展示“我没有武器”——这个习惯流传了几千年，如今成了全球通用的问候动作。
