Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Nancy 成长自述）
My name is Nancy. I was born on April 5th, 2004. I learned to speak at six months old. I learned to climb when I was eight months old. I climbed out of my bed and hurt my head. I learned to walk when I was one year old. One day I walked out of my home and into the street! I learned to eat with chopsticks when I was three. I got food all over my face! I learned to swim when I was four, but I only went swimming in the bath. When I was five I learned to wash clothes, but I got myself all wet! In grade four I learned to cook – I cooked eggs, but they tasted very bad!! Now I'm in grade five.

Story Time · The Ugly Duckling（丑小鸭）
Once there was a mother duck. She was sitting on seven little eggs. One by one, six little yellow ducklings came out of the eggs. ... Two days later, out came a duckling. But this duckling wasn't yellow. It was grey, and it had a long, thin neck. ... Summer came. The ducklings all grew bigger, and the ugly duckling was the biggest. ... Finally, spring came. 'Look at the swan,' she said, 'It's beautiful!' ... He was a swan, a white swan with a long neck. He wasn't ugly. He was beautiful!

📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写out + of —— 固定搭档：从里面出来用 out of，后面必须跟名词
✏️ 口诀：出来找 out，后面 of 带“老窝”
音·发音/aʊt əv/ of 弱读 /əv/；out 的双元音 /aʊ/ 嘴巴先开后合
🎧 跟读：climb out of bed · walk out of the room
意·释义① 从…出来：climb out of bed 爬出被窝；② 用完了：We're out of milk. 牛奶没了
用·句型① I climbed out of my bed and hurt my head. (我爬下床摔了头。——Nancy 名句)
② I walked out of my home and into the street! (我走出家门上了街！)
③ We are out of milk. (我们的牛奶喝完了。)
④ The ducklings came out of the eggs. (小鸭子们破壳而出。)
拓·同族词反义搭档：out of ↔ into（一出一进，课本同句出现）；out 家族：outside 外面 · find out 查明

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
出来The cat jumped out of the box.
用完We are out of orange juice.
破壳Six ducklings came out of the eggs.
出门He walked out of the room quietly.
功能句总结 · out of 与 into 的进出舞

出：climb out of bed（爬出床）
进：walk into the street（走进街道）

Nancy 名句：I walked out of my home and into the street! —— 一句话一出一进，方向感全靠这两个短语。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

out of 与 into 是天然的成对词，建议用“钻被窝”游戏一次教双词：钻进去说 climb into bed，钻出来说 climb out of bed。另外 We are out of milk（牛奶没了）是家庭高频句，冰箱空了随时练。注意：“从…出来”必须说 out of，后面要跟名词。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The Great Bed Escape》

八个月的 Nancy 还不会走，却已经是个“越狱专家”。那天午睡，她先翻过围栏——climb out of bed! 再光着脚丫一路扶墙，一直挪到客厅。等妈妈找到她时，小家伙正扶着茶几笑得得意，额头上还有刚才磕的小包。妈妈心疼又好笑：You climbed out of your bed and hurt your head! 从此围栏又加高了一格。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Nancy climb out of? (Her bed)
• What happened to her head? (She hurt it)
• 问问孩子：Have you ever 'escaped' from bed?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 义① ── 从…出来：out of bed / out of the eggs
├── 义② ── 没有：We are out of milk.
├── 反义 ── into（同框：walked out of home into the street）
├── 课本 ── Nancy 爬出床摔了头 · 丑小鸭破壳
└── 语法 ── 后面必须跟 of + 名词

🏛️ 词源故事

out 是英语最古老的词之一，古英语 ūt；of 同样古老，表“从…分离”。out of 合起来就是“从内向外”。丑小鸭故事里 out came a duckling 是倒装句——out 跳到句首，“扑棱一下钻出来了”，画面感拉满。英语还有 be out of one's mind（发疯）——脑子“跑出去”了。🎯 讲给孩子：out of 是方向词里的“出口标志”，看见它就有东西正往外跑。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Get out of your comfort zone.” 🚀
走出舒适区。——现代励志格言。Nancy 八个月爬出床、一岁走出家门，正是人生最早两次“走出舒适区”。

🎵 歌词里的 out of

🎵 Hall & Oates《Out of Touch》(1984)：Billboard 冠军，80年代二重唱之王的代表作，开场合成器一响就是满满80年代感——out of touch（失去联系）与 out of（出来）共用同一个 out of 家族。

🎵 记忆点：副歌 You're out of touch 拉长音唱出 out of 的连读 /aʊt‿əv/。

⚡ 冷知识

英语词典里 out 参与的短语超过两百个，是名副其实的“造组词之王”：out of bed、run out、break out、find out……而“破壳而出”在英语世界有个专门说法 hatch out。丑小鸭故事里六个鸭蛋 one by one 破壳，用的正是 came out of the eggs——一个 out of，装下了所有“新生命的出口”。
