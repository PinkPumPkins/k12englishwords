📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写tooth（牙）+ ache（疼）= toothache。注意 tooth 的复数是 teeth（oo 变 ee）！
✏️ 口诀：牙疼 toothache，两颗牙变 teeth。
音·发音/ˈtuːθ.eɪk/ th 咬舌尖送气
🎧 跟读：I have a toothache. · brush your teeth · go to the dentist
意·释义牙疼；病根常是 candy 和不刷牙
用·句型① I have a toothache. (我牙疼。)
② You should brush your teeth every day. (你应该每天刷牙。)
③ You should go to the dentist. (你应该去看牙医。)
④ He has a bad toothache. (他牙疼得厉害。)
拓·同族词牙齿家族：tooth→teeth · dentist 牙医 · brush one's teeth 刷牙

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
问诊— What's wrong? — I have a toothache.
处方You should brush your teeth every day.
就医You should go to the dentist.
复数one tooth → two teeth（oo 变 ee）
📋 功能句总结 · 牙疼问诊全流程（Let's Check 3 原句）
医生问：What's your name? / What's wrong?
病人答：I have a toothache.
追查病因：Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much.
医生处方：You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.
⚠️ brush your teeth 用复数 teeth——一次刷一整排！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

toothache 把 ache 家族和刷牙教育一次串起来。在家玩牙医诊所游戏：孩子当牙医问 What's wrong? / Do you often eat candy?，家长演爱吃糖的病人；最后互相监督刷牙，边刷边说 brush my teeth, two minutes, twice a day。重点练 tooth→teeth 的不规则复数。

💫 张翔的嘴里有一座牙齿王国，十位小白卫士日夜站岗。可糖果部队天天来敲门，卫士们喊：You shouldn't eat too much candy! 某天最勇敢的小卫士中弹倒下——Toothache! 牙疼警报响彻王国。国王紧急派兵：牙膏刷刷军团出动！一天两次，每次两分钟，把糖果残渣全部清走。警报解除，小白卫士们闪闪发光：Thank you, brush brush army! 🦷

🏛️ 词源故事

tooth 是英语最古老的核心词之一，古英语写作 tōth，和拉丁语 dens（牙，dentist 的来源）不同族——牙医学界用拉丁名，日常生活用本族名。tooth 的复数 teeth 是英语古老的元音变化：oo 变 ee，和 foot→feet 是一对孪生兄弟。ache 家族里，toothache 中世纪就已出现，是牙和疼最古老的联手。
🎯 讲给孩子：tooth 变 teeth 就像换牙——换的不是一个字母，是一对！

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
tooth → teeth不规则复数：oo 变 ee（和 foot→feet 一样）
dentist牙医（来自拉丁语 dens 牙）
brush one's teeth刷牙：brush your teeth twice a day
go to the dentist看牙医（课本 Let's Check 原句）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“牙疼不是病，疼起来真要命。” 😬 中国民间俗语：牙一疼，脸都垮。课本的解法很科学：少吃糖、天天刷牙、疼了就去看牙医（go to the dentist）。

🎵 歌词里的 toothache

🎵 Billy Ray Cyrus《Achy Breaky Heart》(1992)：ache 家族三兄弟共用一首主题曲，achy（酸痛的）就是 ache 的形容词形态。
🔗 与 stomachache 页互链（同曲）；与 headache 页互链（同曲）——ache 三兄弟在这个单元集合完毕！

⚡ 冷知识

牙医建议每天刷牙两次、每次两分钟，最好早晚各一次——课本 Let's Check 的处方是 brush your teeth every day，Lesson 3 更细：He should brush his teeth every morning and evening. 每年3月20日是世界口腔健康日（World Oral Health Day），全世界一起检查：今天的两分钟，刷了吗？
