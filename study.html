📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写stud·y —— s-t-u-d-y；study insects 研究昆虫、study hard 努力学习
✏️ 口诀：学习研究都用它，y 变 i 才过去。
音·发音/ˈstʌ.di/ 两个音节，重音在前；u 读 /ʌ/ 别读成 /juː/
🎧 跟读：study hard · study insects · study at university
意·释义学习；研究 📖（study = 用功学 + 深入研究；名词还有“书房”的意思）
用·句型① He studied insects. (他研究昆虫。)
② I study English every day. (我每天学英语。)
③ She is studying for the test. (她在为考试复习。)
④ Dad is reading in his study. (爸爸在书房看书。)
拓·同族词study 学习/研究 → student 学生 → studio 工作室（同源）；study（书房）= 读书的房间

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句He was a scientist and studied insects.
日常I study English for half an hour every day.
复习She is studying for the maths test.
对比study（系统学习）vs learn（学会）：We learn to swim, but study English.
📋 功能句总结 · study 三件套

学什么直接接：study English / study insects（研究昆虫）。

为考试：study for the test（复习备考）。

study vs learn：study 是“下功夫学”的过程，learn 是“学会了”的结果——We study English to learn to speak it.

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

study 在课本里有两种用法：法布尔“研究”昆虫（深入研究），海伦·凯勒“苦学”读写（用功学）。陪学时点透：study 不只是背单词，还包括观察、思考、动手。家里固定一个“study time”（哪怕 20 分钟），让孩子说 I'm going to study now.——仪式感比时长更重要。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站

📖 微型故事《台灯上的小纸条》

期末前，小雨把 study 写在纸条上贴到台灯上。妈妈问：“What does it mean today?” 小雨说：“Fabre studied insects. He watched, asked and wrote. So today I study like a scientist!” 她把数学错题当“昆虫”观察，找出三只“同款错误”。考完那天，她把纸条翻过来写上：Studied. Done.——study 过去了，本事留下了。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Xiaoyu stick on the lamp? (A note that says 'study'.)
• How did she study like a scientist? (She watched her mistakes like insects.)
• 问问孩子：你的“错题昆虫”长什么样？怎么抓住它？

🏛️ 词源故事

study 来自拉丁语 studere，本义“热心投入、急切向前”——studium 是“热情”。也就是说，“学习”这个词的老祖宗，说的不是“用功”，而是“心里热”。
🎯 讲给孩子：study 的原动力是“热”，不是“压”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Studies serve for delight, for ornament, and for ability.” 📚
读书足以怡情，足以傅彩，足以长才。—— 培根《论读书》（王佐良译）：学习不为别人，是给自己长本事。

🎵 歌词里的 study

🎵 Chuck Berry《School Days》（1957）：摇滚先驱经典——“Ring ring goes the bell”上课铃一响就开唱，从早读唱到放学，study 的主场就是校园。
🔗 与 school 页互链（同曲）：School Days 里，study 是每天的主线任务。

⚡ 冷知识

法布尔研究昆虫可以趴在地上看一整天，《昆虫记》写了十卷、花了约 30 年。中文“学”字也有讲究：甲骨文的“学”像双手摆弄算筹（小棍儿）——古人学算术，也是“动手研究”。拼写小陷阱：study 的过去式是 studied 不是 studyed，y 要变 i！
