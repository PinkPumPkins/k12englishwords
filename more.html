📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

✅ 原文经教习网英语朗读宝全课时跟读页、电子课本与同步练习资源多源交叉核对，智慧山确认单元目录。more 是本单元的加法词：Lesson 1 的 You should eat more vegetables and fruit.、Lesson 3 信里 Try to walk more——医生开的每一张处方，最后都落在 more 上：多蔬菜、多走路。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写m-o-r-e 四个字母，是 much/many 的比较级——更多。
✏️ 口诀：much 变 more，many 也变 more，most 是它俩的最多。
音·发音/mɔː/ 英音长音；美音 /mɔːr/ 卷舌
🎧 跟读：eat more fruit · walk more · more and more
意·释义更多的（形容词/代词）；更（副词，构成长比较级：more careful）
用·句型① You should eat more vegetables and fruit. (你应该多吃蔬菜水果。)
② Try to walk more. (试着多走走路。)
③ I need more water. (我需要更多水。)
④ She is more careful than me. (她比我更细心。——more 构成比较级)
拓·同族词反义：less 更少（little 的比较级）；递进：more and more 越来越

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
处方You should eat more vegetables and fruit.
运动Try to walk more every day.
比较级more careful / more beautiful
递进more and more 越来越多
📋 功能句总结 · more 的两副面孔
more + 名词（更多的）：eat more fruit · drink more water
more + 长形容词（构成比较级）：more careful · more beautiful
more 单独用：Try to walk more.
⚠️ 反义词：less 更少——Less is more!（少即是多，经典名言）
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

more 是健康处方的万能开头：eat more fruit、drink more water、walk more。在家让孩子开一张 more 处方给全家，再用 less 反向开一张少碰清单（less candy、less TV）。一句话同时练 more/less 反义对，正好接住名言 Less is more。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 多少对决
家长说好习惯（eat fruit），孩子加 more；说坏习惯（eat candy），孩子加 less → 快对得一颗星
✏️ 关卡2 · 比较级开关
用 more 组比较级：careful → ______ (more careful)
直接加 er 的：taller（不用 more！）
✅ 关卡3 · 课本句补全
You should eat ______ vegetables and fruit. (more)
Try to walk ______. (more)
🔍 关卡4 · 处方开单
给家人开一张 more 处方：Drink more water! Eat more fruit!
🎙️ 关卡5 · 口语大挑战
录一张 more+less 双向处方
🎤 示例：Eat more fruit, less candy. Walk more, watch less TV!

📖 微型故事《再多一点点》

💫 小魔杖多多只会一个咒语：More! 它对苹果念咒——苹果变多；对汤念咒——汤更多。可它对小胖的糖罐念咒，糖罐炸了！多多慌了，去找魔法奶奶。奶奶教它新咒语：该多的加 more（more fruit, more water, more sleep），该少的说 less（less candy, less TV）。多多学会了聪明咒语，全王国再也没炸过糖罐。🪄

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What was the wand's first spell? (More!)
• Why did the sugar jar explode? (Too much candy is bad!)
• 问问孩子：给一天里的事分类——哪些该 more，哪些该 less？用英语各说两句

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 身份 ── much/many 的比较级
├── 三级 ── much → more → most
├── 处方 ── eat more fruit · walk more
├── 比较级 ── more + 长形容词
└── 反义 ── less 更少（Less is more!）

🏛️ 词源故事

more 来自古英语 māra，它本来就是 much（古英语 micel）的比较级——不是借用，是亲生。同样，most 是最高级。这对“亲生母子”（much→more→most）和 little→less→least、good→better→best 一样，都是英语最古老的比较级家族。
🎯 讲给孩子：more 不是新词，是 much 长大后的名字。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
much → more → most不可数量词的三级：多-更多-最多
many → more → most可数量词的三级（共用 more/most）
more + 长形容词构成比较级：more careful
less反义词：little 的比较级——Less is more!

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Less is more.” ✨ 少即是多。—— 现代建筑大师密斯·凡德罗的名言，建筑界最有名的反话：该多的多（more fruit, more sleep），该少的少（less candy, less TV），这才是真正的 more。

🎵 歌词里的 more

🎵 Extreme《More Than Words》(1991)：Billboard 亚军的传世木吉他情歌，More than words 唱的就是——爱不需要更多语言，more 在标题里闪闪发光。
🔗 与本单元 You should eat more... 相互印证：more 后面可以接一切值得加量的好东西。

⚡ 冷知识

more 是英语比较级家族的劳动模范：它既能自己当比较级（much→more），还能帮长形容词组成比较级（more careful）。但小心别帮倒忙：短词直接加 er（taller），不说 more tall。课本里两处 more 都是接名词：eat more vegetables and fruit、walk more——这是小学生最先用熟的一种。
