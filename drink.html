单词 drink
词性 v./n. 喝；饮料
音标 /drɪŋk/
难度 ⭐⭐ (五年级)
三态 drink – drank – drunk
📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

✅ 原文经教习网英语朗读宝全课时跟读页、电子课本与同步练习资源多源交叉核对，智慧山确认单元目录。drink 在本单元是白开水代言人：Get ready 的 drink milk every day / drink too much juice、Lesson 2 的 You should drink some water、Fun Time 歌词 drink some warm water——课本把 drink 交给水和牛奶，把 too much 留给了果汁。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写d-r-i-n-k 五个字母。过去式 drank（i 变 a），过去分词 drunk（i 变 u）——元音接力跑！
✏️ 口诀：i-a-u 排排站，drink-drank-drunk 三个变。
音·发音/drɪŋk/ 一个音节，/ŋk/ 鼻音收尾
🎧 跟读：drink water · drink some warm water · a favourite drink
意·释义喝（动词）；饮料（名词）——What's your favourite drink?
用·句型① You should drink some water. (你应该喝点水。)
② I drink milk every day. (我每天喝牛奶。)
③ What's your favourite drink? (你最喜欢的饮料是什么？)
④ We shouldn't drink too much juice. (我们不该喝太多果汁。)
拓·同族词家族词：drank 过去式 · drunk 过去分词 · drinkable 可饮用的 · soft drink 软饮料

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
处方You should drink some warm water.
习惯I drink milk every day.
问答— What's your favourite drink? — Juice!
三态drink – drank – drunk
📋 功能句总结 · 喝什么最健康（课本原句盘点）
该喝的：drink lots of water · drink milk every day · drink some warm water
不该多喝的：We shouldn't drink too much juice. It's not good for our teeth.
⚠️ 问饮料：What's your favourite drink? — drink 在这里变成了名词！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

餐桌就是 drink 的课堂：递水杯说 Drink some water.，早餐时说 Drink milk every day.，再看果汁说 We shouldn't drink too much juice. 顺手练过去式：昨天喝了什么？I drank... 三态接力 drink-drank-drunk 一周就能滚瓜烂熟。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《水滴小侦探》

💫 小水滴侦探专查喝什么案。他先到强强家：桌上有可乐、果汁、白开水。侦探掏出放大镜：Juice is sweet but too much is bad for your teeth! 再到妞妞家：她正喝牛奶。侦探盖章：Milk every day, perfect! 最后到力力家——力力打完球满头汗，侦探举起白开水：You should drink lots of water! 一天下来，侦探在本子上写下结论：最好的饮料，永远是最简单的那杯。💧

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did the detective say about juice? (Too much is bad for teeth)
• What did Li Li drink after the game? (Water)
• 问问孩子：What do you drink every day? 用英语列一杯健康清单

🏛️ 词源故事

drink 来自古英语 drincan，和德语 trinken 是亲兄弟，都是日耳曼家族的孩子。它的三态变化（drink-drank-drunk）是英语最古老的元音变化遗产——几千年前人们就靠换元音表示时间变化，像唱歌一样好听。
🎯 讲给孩子：drink 的 i 像杯里的水，drank 换成 a，drunk 换成 u——一杯水变三次戏法。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
drink – drank – drunk三态：喝-喝过（去）-喝过（完成）
drank cold drinks过去式短语（课本过去式单元见过）
soft drink软饮料（汽水果汁类）
drinkable可饮用的：Is the water drinkable?

📜 名言 & 谚语

“You can lead a horse to water, but you can't make it drink.” 🐴 牵马到河边，强按马不喝水。—— 英语千年老谚语：劝告能送到耳边，行动还得自己来，正合本单元给建议的主题。

🎵 歌词里的 drink

🎵 Harry Nilsson《Coconut》(1971)：奇趣经典，整首几乎一个和弦，put the lime in the coconut and drink 'em both up 的洗脑循环里藏着本词。
🔗 与 drank cold drinks 页互链（同曲）——drink-drank 过去式对照着记。

⚡ 冷知识

《中国居民膳食指南》建议成年人每天喝大约1500-1700毫升水，约7-8杯；课本给孩子的版本更好记：You should drink lots of water and some milk every day. 少喝甜饮料的科学与课本完全一致——Lesson 2 说得直白：We shouldn't drink too much juice. It's not good for our teeth.
