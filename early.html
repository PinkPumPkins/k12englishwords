📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

✅ 原文经教习网英语朗读宝全课时跟读页、电子课本与同步练习资源多源交叉核对，智慧山确认单元目录。early 是本单元的健康处方词：Get ready 和 Lesson 1 各有一次 go to bed early，Lesson 3 信里 Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30——早睡是琳达给出的最后一条建议。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写e-a-r-l-y 四个字母，y 结尾。比较级 earlier（更早），最高级 earliest（最早）。
✏️ 口诀：early 早早起，比较级 earlier 不是 more early。
音·发音/ˈɜː.li/ 两个音节，重音在前：EAR-ly
🎧 跟读：go to bed early · get up early · early morning
意·释义早的（形容词）/ 早地（副词）；early bird 早起的人
用·句型① You should go to bed early. (你应该早点睡。)
② I get up early every day. (我每天早起。)
③ The early bird catches the worm. (早起的鸟儿有虫吃。)
④ She is an early riser. (她是个早起的人。)
拓·同族词反义：late 晚；家族：earlier/earliest · early bird · early morning 清晨

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
作息I get up early and go to bed early.
谚语The early bird catches the worm.
描述We have breakfast in the early morning.
对比early（早）/ late（晚）
📋 功能句总结 · 早晚作息问答
问：When do you go to bed?（你什么时候睡？）
答：I go to bed early, before 9:30.
问：Do you get up early?
答：Yes, I get up early every day.
⚠️ early 既是形容词也是副词：get up early（副词）/ an early breakfast（形容词）
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

把 early 变成家庭的晨型仪式：孩子每天早起5分钟，用英语说 I get up early! 再对比睡懒觉的周末 I get up late. 重点盯比较级 earlier 的拼写（不是 more early）。顺带把富兰克林那句 Early to bed and early to rise... 贴在床头。

📖 微型故事《清晨的金币》

💫 小米总睡懒觉，妈妈讲了个故事：清晨的天空会掉金币，只有早起的孩子才接得到。第二天小米6点半爬起来，院子里的露珠亮晶晶——She gets up early! 小麻雀叼着一粒金谷子落在她手心。小米笑了：The early bird catches the worm! 从那天起，小米天天 early，接住的不只是金币，还有一整个清爽的早晨。🌅

• What did Mum say falls from the sky? (Gold coins)
• What did the bird give Xiaomi? (A golden grain)

🏛️ 词源故事

early 来自古英语 ǣrlīc，就是 ǣr（早、在之前）+ -līc（-ly 后缀），字面是“比时间先到”，一千多年意思几乎没变。它和古诗里还见得到的 ere（在…之前）是亲戚。
🎯 讲给孩子：early 就是“跑到时间前面”，所以早起的鸟儿有虫吃。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
early → earlier → earliest比较级/最高级：不是 more early！
late → later → latest反义阶梯：晚的/更晚/最晚
early bird早起的人；早起的鸟儿有虫吃
early morning清晨（大清早）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“The early bird catches the worm.” 🐦 早起的鸟儿有虫吃。—— 英语世界最有名的谚语之一，和课本的 go to bed early 是天生一对：早睡才能早起。

🎵 歌词里的 early

🎵 Elvis Presley《Early Morning Rain》（原唱 Gordon Lightfoot，1966）：民谣经典，猫王翻唱版流传最广；知名度中上已如实标注，ear- 的拖长音适合跟读。
🔗 与本单元 go to bed early 相互印证：清晨的雨属于早起的人。

⚡ 冷知识

德国人也有早起金句：Morgenstund hat Gold im Mund——“清晨的嘴里含着黄金”，英语常译作 The morning hour has gold in its mouth. 全世界都在夸早起。课本里琳达给 Worried 的三步处方（每天锻炼、少玩电脑、9点半前上床）里，早睡正是最后压轴的一条。
