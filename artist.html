📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写art·ist —— art（艺术）+ ist（做…的人）；别拼成 atrist！
✏️ 口诀：art 里长出 artist，一门手艺一个人。
音·发音/ˈɑː.tɪst/ 两个音节，重音在前
🎧 跟读：a famous artist · Xu Beihong was an artist.
意·释义画家；艺术家 🎨（靠艺术创作吃饭的人；泛指艺术家，专指“画家”也说 painter）
用·句型① Xu Beihong was an artist. (徐悲鸿是一位画家。)
② The artist is painting a horse. (画家正在画马。)
③ My dream is to be an artist. (我的梦想是当艺术家。)
④ Van Gogh was a great artist. (梵高是伟大的画家。)
拓·同族词art 艺术 → artist 艺术家 → artwork 艺术品；painter 画家（更偏职业）；scientist 里的 -ist 和这里是同一个后缀！

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
名人介绍Van Gogh was a great artist. He painted the Starry Night.
课堂用语Draw like an artist! Use your imagination.
梦想She wants to be an artist when she grows up.
对比artist 泛指艺术家；painter 特指画家：Qi Baishi was a great painter.
📋 功能句总结 · What does an artist do?

问职业：— What does an artist do?（艺术家做什么？）— An artist paints pictures.（艺术家画画。）

-ist 后缀家族：art 艺术 → artist 艺术家；science 科学 → scientist 科学家；piano 钢琴 → pianist 钢琴家。

⚠️ artist 以元音开头，用 an：an artist。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

本词连着课本里的徐悲鸿。在家聊三件事：1) 拿出孩子的画，说 “You are a little artist!”；2) 讲徐悲鸿画马：他画的马奔起来像有风——好作品来自成千上万次练习；3) 区分 artist（艺术家）和 paint（画画的动作）。有条件带孩子看一次画展，指着展签说 This artist is famous for...

📖 微型故事《马的一百张脸》

美术课上，乐乐画马总画不像，想撕掉画纸。老师说：“Xu Beihong was a great artist. He painted horses all his life!” 乐乐不信：“一百匹？”他真的开始每天画一匹：躺着的、跑着的、回头的……画到第三十七匹，同桌惊呼：“This horse is running!” 乐乐笑了。原来 artist 的秘密不在画笔，在坚持。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What was Xiaolei bad at first? (Painting horses.)
• What did the teacher say about Xu Beihong? (He painted horses all his life.)
• 问问孩子：你愿意为一件喜欢的事练一百次吗？

🗺️ 思维导图

artist 艺术家/画家
├── 家族 ── art 艺术 · artwork 艺术品 · artistic 有艺术感的
├── 同后缀 ── scientist 科学家 · pianist 钢琴家 · dentist 牙医
├── 课本名人 ── Xu Beihong 徐悲鸿（善画马）
├── 外国名人 ── Van Gogh 梵高 · da Vinci 达·芬奇
└── 理想句 ── I want to be an artist.

🏛️ 词源故事

artist 的根是拉丁语 ars，意思是“技艺、手艺”。在古人眼里，木匠打家具和画家画画是同一件事——都是“把东西做得又美又好”。所以 artist 既是画家，也是一切“有手艺”的人。
🎯 讲给孩子：艺术家不是“会画画的人”，是“把手艺练到美的人”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“人不可有傲气，但不可无傲骨。” 🖌️
—— 课本画家徐悲鸿的名言：可以自信，但不能自负。他一生画马无数，也把这句刻进了自己的画风。

🎵 歌词里的 artist

🎵 The Rolling Stones《Paint It Black》（1966）：传奇滚石名曲，西塔琴前奏一响全世界都认得——“I see a red door and I want it painted black”，艺术家对色彩的执念唱成了摇滚史诗。画家的冠名曲难得，这首“刷门之歌”是最有画味的选择。
🔗 与 paint 页互链：两页同曲——artist 是人，paint 是动作。

⚡ 冷知识

梵高生前只卖出一幅油画（《红色葡萄园》），如今他的《加歇医生的肖像》拍出过 8250 万美元天价。课本里的徐悲鸿同样“苦练出身”：他画的奔马连鬃毛都带书法笔意，被戏称“一匹马里有字”。艺术的起点都一样：一笔一笔练。
