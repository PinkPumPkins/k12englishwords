— I like doing jigsaw puzzles.

Lesson 3 · A. Read and write（家庭爱好书）
This is my family's hobby book. I like collecting stamps. I have over 200 stamps. I also like skateboarding. I think it is very cool. My mother likes drawing. She is really good at it. My father likes fishing. He often goes fishing but never catches any fish. My cat likes eating fish. She often goes with my father, but she never gets any fish!

Let's Check · A/B（节选）
My friends and I have different hobbies. ... We have different hobbies. I like skateboarding. I go to a skateboarding club every week. Mark likes making models. He often makes model planes with his father. Mary likes reading books. She often reads children's stories.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写d-i-f-f-e-r-e-n-t，9个字母，双 f 别写漏
✏️ 口诀：不同的 different，两个 f 站岗！
音·发音/ˈdɪ.frənt/ 三个音节常快读成两个：DIF-rent
🎧 跟读：different hobbies · be different from · We have different hobbies.
意·释义不同的 🌈（各不相同的、有差别的）
用·句型① We have different hobbies. (我们有不同的爱好。——Let's Check 原句)
② My friends and I have different hobbies. (我和朋友们有不同的爱好。——Let's Check 原句)
③ My hobby is different from yours. (我的爱好和你的不同。)
④ Different people like different things. (不同的人喜欢不同的东西。)
拓·同族词differ 不同 → different 不同的 → differently 不同地（副词）→ difference 差别（名词）
反义词：same 相同的 → the same as 和……一样

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
描述We have different hobbies.
对比My bag is different from yours.
包容Different people, different hobbies.
反义We are in the same class.
📋 功能句总结 · be different from
'与……不同'的固定搭配：
A is different from B. A 与 B 不同。
My hobby is different from yours. 我的爱好和你的不同。

反义搭配：the same as 和……一样
My hobby is the same as yours. 我的爱好和你的一样。
⚠️ 介词只能用 from，不能换成 with！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

different 在 Let's Check 里出现两次，是单元高频形容词。建议玩'找不同'游戏：两个人各说自己的爱好，再总结 We have different hobbies! 顺带教反义词 same——different/same 成对记，比单独记牢一倍；be different from 的 from 是易错点。

📖 微型故事《不同的爱好，一样的朋友》

💫 转学生 Max 总担心自己和大家不一样：I like making models, but they like skateboarding... 运动会上他发现：Everyone is different! 朵朵爱 dancing，小安爱 doing jigsaw puzzles，而他爱 making models。班会课上大家一起喊：Different hobbies, same friendship! Max 笑了——原来不同，才是班级最有趣的地方。🌈

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What does Max like doing? (Making models)
• What did the class say? (Different hobbies, same friendship!)
• 问问孩子：你和好朋友有什么 different 的爱好？一样的地方呢？

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 词形 ── differ → different → differently
├── 反义 ── same 相同的 ↔ different 不同的
├── 句型 ── A is different from B.
├── 课本 ── We have different hobbies.
└── 俚语 ── Different strokes for different folks.

🏛️ 词源故事

different 来自拉丁语 differre：dis-（分开）+ ferre（携带、拿）——'各自把东西带向不同方向'，就是'不同'。同根词还有 difference（差别）和 indifferent（原义'没有分别'→ 引申为'漠不关心'）。它的亲戚众多：transfer（转移）、refer（提及）都是 ferre 家族——都是'带'出来的词。
🎯 讲给孩子：dis + ferre = 分开带走 = 不同。

⚡ 冷知识

世界上没有两片完全相同的雪花，也没有两个完全相同的指纹——连双胞胎的指纹都不一样。科学家正是靠指纹'各不相同'的特点来识别身份的。课本里 Sandy 说 We have different hobbies——如果全班爱好都一样，那多无聊呀！
