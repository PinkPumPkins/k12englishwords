Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写listen → listened —— 直接加 ed（t 结尾不再翻倍）
✏️ 口诀：listen 加 ed，to 字别忘记
音·发音/ˈlɪsnd/ 两音节，t 几乎被吞掉：lis-ənd
🎧 跟读：listened to music · listen to me · Listen!
意·释义听音乐（过去发生）。listen 专注地听（不及物动词，接内容要带 to）；hear 是“听见”（结果）
用·句型① On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football. (课文歌曲原句！)
② I listened to music before bed. (我睡前听了音乐。)
③ Listen to me, please! (请听我说！)
④ Did you listen to music last night?
拓·同族词listen → listened → listening（与 listening to music 页互链）
辨析：listen to 听（动作）/ hear 听见（结果）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
歌曲句I listened to music and played football.
睡前I listened to music before I went to sleep.
现在对照I often listen to music on the bus.
课堂用语Listen to the teacher carefully.
功能句总结 · listen vs hear

listen to：主动去听 → I listened to music. 🎧
hear：听见（结果）→ I heard a bird.
课堂高频：Listen carefully! / Listen and repeat!（听并跟读！）

⚠️ listen 后面必须带 to 才能接内容！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

listen to 的 to 是孩子最容易丢的小词。把睡前仪式改成英语版：Put on your headphones. Listen to music. Sleep well. 第二天问 What did you listen to?，让过去式在真实的听歌日常里循环。

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 听音辨义
家长说 listen / hear / look，孩子做动作：listen = 捂耳倾听，hear = 点头“听到了”，look = 手搭凉棚
✏️ 关卡2 · to字陷阱
补全：I listened ___ music. → to！（丢了 to 就不成句）
✅ 关卡3 · 过去式填空
① Last night I ___ (listen) to music. → listened
② ___ to me, please! → Listen
🔍 关卡4 · 歌单分享
孩子挑一首歌，用英语介绍：I listened to ___. I like it because... 得一颗星
🎙️ 关卡5 · 口语大挑战
唱课文歌曲第一句并录音

👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The Song That Stuck》

Tina 在公交车上听到一首歌，旋律一路跟着她回家。I need to find this song! 她求妈妈帮忙找了一路——超市、面包店、公园，最后发现是妹妹在房间里放的 CD！三人戴上耳机轮流听，笑作一团。那晚 Tina 睡前说：I listened to music today. The same song, five times! 妈妈说：好歌值得单曲循环。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Where did Tina first hear the song? (On the bus)
• Who had the CD? (Her little sister)
• 问问孩子：What song do you want to listen to again and again?

🏛️ 词源故事

listen 来自古英语 hlysnan，词根可追溯到印欧语的“听”，和德语 lauschen 是亲戚。music 来自希腊语 mousike，本义“缪斯女神的艺术”——希腊神话里九位缪斯掌管艺术与科学，music 就是“缪斯的礼物”。🎯 讲给孩子：listen 用耳朵，music 是女神送的——听音乐，就是接收来自奥林匹斯山的电波。

⚡ 冷知识

在留声机发明（1877年，爱迪生）之前，音乐只能现场听：想听音乐就得请乐师，或者自己弹自己唱。所以古人 listened to music 的机会比我们珍贵得多——一场音乐会，能让人记一辈子。
