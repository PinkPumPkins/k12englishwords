📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写head（头）+ ache（疼）= headache，ache 家族里最好拼的一个！
✏️ 口诀：头加疼，头疼 headache，一眼就会拼。
音·发音/ˈhed.eɪk/ 两个音节
🎧 跟读：I've got a headache. · have a headache · a bad headache
意·释义头疼（也引申为“令人头痛的事”：a big headache 大麻烦）
用·句型① I've got a headache. (我头疼。)
② You should have a rest. (你应该休息一下。)
③ I have a bad headache. (我头疼得厉害。)
④ Too much TV gives me a headache. (电视看多了我头疼。)
拓·同族词习语：a headache = 麻烦事（This math problem is a headache.）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
问诊— What's wrong? — I've got a headache.
处方You should have a rest and drink some warm water.
程度I have a bad headache.
引申Too much homework is a headache.
📋 功能句总结 · 头疼处方笺（Fun Time 儿歌原句）
报症状：I've got a headache, a headache, a headache.
求助：What should I do?
处方一：You should have a rest and drink some warm water.
处方二：You shouldn't watch TV. No TV for you!
⚠️ 课本 Fun Time 的儿歌把这套问答编成了歌，唱两遍就背会！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

Fun Time 的头疼儿歌是本单元最佳亲子素材：一人唱 I've got a headache, what should I do? 另一人唱 You should have a rest and drink some warm water. 全家轮流当病人和护士。headache 拼写简单，重点练 I've got a... 的流利度。

📖 微型故事《头顶的小雷雨》

💫 玛丽暑假天天打游戏，某天头顶下起小雷雨：轰隆隆——I've got a headache! 她去看护士阿姨。护士递上三件宝：一杯温水、一个枕头、一块写着 NO 的牌子。玛丽照做：drink some warm water, have a rest, no TV! 半小时后雷雨停了，头顶晴空万里。护士阿姨说：Screens are fun, but your head needs rest too. 🌦️

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why did Mary have a headache? (She played computer games too much)
• What were the nurse's three gifts? (Warm water, rest, no TV)
• 问问孩子：When your head rains, what should you do? 用英语开出你的处方

🏛️ 词源故事

headache 是 ache 家族中血统最正的老大：这个词在古英语里就有（heafod ece），上千年历史。有意思的是，英语里 headache 早就不只管头疼——一件棘手的事、一个难缠的问题，都可以叫 a headache（令人头痛的事）。所以 Too much homework is a headache. 既幽默又地道。
🎯 讲给孩子：headache 是双面词——头会疼，难事也叫它。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“An ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure.” ⚖️ 一盎司预防胜过一磅治疗。—— 常被归于本杰明·富兰克林《穷理查年鉴》的名句：与其头疼了找药，不如早点睡觉少看电视。

🎵 歌词里的 headache

🎵 Billy Ray Cyrus《Achy Breaky Heart》(1992)：ache 家族主题曲，achy 正是 ache 的形容词形态，Billboard 前五的排舞热潮。
🔗 与 stomachache 页互链（同曲）；与 toothache 页互链（同曲）——ache 三兄弟共用一首歌。

⚡ 冷知识

课本 Fun Time 的儿歌把 headache 的整套处方唱成了一首歌：I've got a headache. What should I do? You should have a rest and drink some warm water. You shouldn't watch TV. No TV for you! 儿歌里的 No TV for you!（不许看电视啦）是最活泼的英文禁令句式，口气坚定又好记。
