Get ready · 热身
— Do you often go to the zoo? What is your favourite animal?
— I like butterflies. They are insects. They can fly.

Lesson 1 · 课文短文（节选）
Lily and her classmates are going to see the animals in the zoo.
First, they are going to see the sharks. Sharks are fish. They can swim under the water. They are very big and like to eat small animals in the sea.
Then they want to see the snakes and the butterflies. Snakes are reptiles. They are very long and cold. Butterflies are beautiful animals. They are insects. They have six legs, and they can fly.
Next, they want to see some mammals, such as monkeys, pandas and tigers. Mammals give milk to their babies.
At last, they are going to see the penguins. Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They live on the ice. Look, how cute they are!

Lesson 1 · 分类知识（节选）
Reptiles have cold blood. They have scales. Most reptiles have four legs. All insects have six legs. Mother insects lay eggs. Most insects can fly.

Lesson 2 · 问答对话（节选）
— What kind of animals are monkeys?
— They are mammals.
— What can monkeys do?
— They can climb trees very well.
— What do monkeys like to eat?
— They like to eat peaches and bananas.

Lesson 3 · 短文 The Elephant（节选）
Elephants are mammals. They can be four metres tall. They don't eat meat. They only eat fruit, leaves and grass. They need a lot of food, so they spend most of their time eating. The elephant has two long, white teeth called tusks. It is the only animal with a trunk — a special long nose. The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut. The elephant is also the mammal with the biggest ears. They look like two big fans. Elephants live in Africa and Asia. They are very strong, very smart and of course, very, very big.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写Af·ri·ca，三音节；洲名首字母大写A。✏️口诀：非洲Africa，象群住这里
音·发音/ˈæf.rɪ.kə/ 重音在最前，词尾/kə/弱读。
🎧 跟读：Elephants live in Africa and Asia
意·释义n. 非洲🌍（世界第二大洲；草原、大象、狮子的家园）
用·句型① Elephants live in Africa and Asia.（大象住在非洲和亚洲。——课本原句）
② Africa is a big continent.（非洲是一块很大的洲。）
③ African elephants have bigger ears.（非洲象耳朵更大。）
④ There are many animals in Africa.（非洲有很多动物。）
拓·同族词African adj./n. 非洲的/非洲人（Africa+n）；African elephant非洲象 · South Africa南非（国家名）；同单元：Asia亚洲

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句Elephants live in Africa and Asia.
举例African elephants have very big ears.
地理Africa is the second largest continent.
文化African music is full of rhythm.
功能句总结 · live in + 地点

课本句型：Elephants live in Africa and Asia.

仿写：Penguins live on the ice. / Monkeys live in the forest.

大写规则：Africa ✔ · africa ✘（洲名首字母大写）

⚠️ live in + 大地点（国家/大洲），live on + 表面（ice）——介词看“里面还是表面”！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

挂一张世界地图，找出Africa和Asia，让孩子把本单元动物“送回家”：大象住Africa and Asia、企鹅住南极、老虎住Asia。一句课本句“Elephants live in Africa and Asia”能把地理和英语一起学了。

📖 微型故事《Two Homes for Elephants》

🌍 非洲象Kibo和亚洲象Mei在动物园相遇。Kibo说：“My home is Africa — big grassland, big ears!”Mei说：“My home is Asia — forest, and my ears are smaller.”他们比了比耳朵，笑作一团。饲养员说：“You are cousins! Elephants live in Africa and Asia — two homes, one family.”从那天起，Kibo用大耳朵给Mei扇风，Mei用小耳朵听Kibo讲草原的故事。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Kibo的家在哪里？(Africa)
• 两头象哪里不一样？(耳朵大小)
• 问问孩子：Where do elephants live?（课本句抢答）

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 大洲 ── Africa非洲（世界第二大洲） ├── 动物 ── elephant大象 · lion狮子 · giraffe长颈鹿 ├── 对比 ── African elephant耳朵大 / Asian elephant耳朵小 ├── 洲名家族 ── Asia亚洲 · Europe欧洲 · Antarctica南极洲 └── 课本句 ── Elephants live in Africa and Asia.

🏛️ 词源故事

Africa的来源有几种说法：一说起自罗马人对北非部落的称呼Afri，加上表示“土地”的后缀-ca；一说来自腓尼基语afar（尘土）。罗马人把这片土地叫Africa terra（非洲之地），名字沿用两千多年。
🎯 讲给孩子：一个大洲的名字，从“部落”和“尘土”里长出来。
