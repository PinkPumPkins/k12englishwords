📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写go(走) + straight(直的) → 一直走；straight 记住 ai 在中间、gh 不发音
✏️ 口诀：straight 里 gh 闭嘴不说话！
音·发音/streɪt/ 重读，gh 完全不发音
🎧 跟读：Go straight and turn left.
意·释义直行 ⬆️（指路口令：一直往前走，不拐弯）
用·句型① Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. (直走在第一个路口左转。)
② Go straight, and you can see the bank. (一直走就能看到银行。)
③ Go straight ahead for two minutes. (直走两分钟。)
④ Please go straight home after school. (放学后请直接回家。)
拓·同族词straight 本义“直的”：a straight line 直线；straight away 立刻；反义：turn 拐弯

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
指路Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads.
描述The street is very straight.

引申Do your homework straight away.
功能句总结 · 指路第一步
Go straight. = 一直走
升级版：Go straight ahead（往前直走）/ Go straight on（英式：继续直行）
完整示范：— How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads.
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

go straight 是指路三件套的第一句。家里玩“机器人指令”：家长是机器人，孩子发指令 Go straight / Turn left / Stop!，然后交换。孩子当发令员时，必须说完整的 Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads 才算通关。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《直线小蚂蚁》

🐜 小蚂蚁要回家，路线笔直：“Go straight, and you'll see your home!” 它一直走一直走，中间不吃不玩。旁边的蝴蝶问：“Why don't you turn?” 蚂蚁说：“My home is on a straight line!” 到家一看——原来今天的家就安在花盆的直路上。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• How does the ant go home? (Go straight)
• Who asked it a question? (蝴蝶)
• 问问孩子：从教室到你座位，是直走还是拐弯？

🗺️ 思维导图

go straight 直行 ├── 指路 ── Go straight and turn left. ├── 升级 ── go straight ahead / go straight on ├── 词形 ── straight 直的 / straight away 立刻 ├── 词源 ── 与 stretch(拉伸) 同源 → 拉直 └── 互链 ── turn left 页 · turn right 页

🏛️ 词源故事

straight 和 stretch（拉伸）是一家人：中古英语里“被拉直的东西”就是 straight。所以 go straight 的画面感是——像一根拉直的绳子一样走。别把它写成 strait（海峡/困境），那是一对著名形近词。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
go straightgo(走) + straight(拉直的) → 直行
straight away立刻、马上（英式常用）
strait海峡；形近词陷阱：strait ≠ straight

📜 名言 & 谚语

几何学名言：“The shortest distance between two points is a straight line.”（两点之间，直线最短。）它原是数学公理，如今也常用来比喻办事直接了当。

🎵 歌词里的 go straight

🎵 The Byrds《Turn! Turn! Turn!》(1965)：Billboard 冠军，歌词改编自《圣经·传道书》第三章——“To everything, turn, turn, turn”，万物皆有时。曲名里全是 turn，正好和 go straight 组成“转弯 vs 直行”对照组。与 turn left 页、turn right 页互链。

⚡ 冷知识

地图上飞机航线是弯的，不是直线？因为地球是球体：球面上两点间最短的是“大圆航线”，在平面地图上看起来就成了弧线。所以飞行员说 go straight 时，走的其实是“球面直线”。
