📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写sci·en·tist —— science（科学）去 ce 加 tist；中间是 en 不是 an
✏️ 口诀：science 摘尾换 tist，科学家就诞生。
音·发音/ˈsaɪən.tɪst/ 三个音节，重音在前；sci 读 /saɪ/
🎧 跟读：a famous scientist · study insects like a scientist.
意·释义科学家 🔬（研究自然、做实验找答案的人；science 科学，scientific 科学的）
用·句型① He was a French scientist. (他是一位法国科学家。)
② The scientist studies insects. (这位科学家研究昆虫。)
③ Newton was a famous scientist. (牛顿是著名的科学家。)
④ Scientists ask questions and find answers. (科学家提出问题并找到答案。)
拓·同族词science 科学 → scientist 科学家 → scientific 科学的；同后缀：artist 艺术家 · dentist 牙医 · pianist 钢琴家

📋 功能句总结 · -ist：专业的人

名词 + ist = 职业：science 科学 → scientist 科学家；art 艺术 → artist 艺术家；dent 牙 → dentist 牙医。

课本问答句：— Who's this man? What did he do?（这人是谁？他做什么的？）— He was a French scientist.（他是法国科学家。）

⚠️ What did he do? 问的是“生前职业”，用过去式！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

科学家是本单元出镜率最高的职业：法布尔研究昆虫、牛顿发现苹果落地的秘密。在家做个“五分钟小实验”（比如把盐化进水里），让孩子说 “I'm a little scientist!”。重点讲法布尔精神：蹲下来看蚂蚁搬家半小时不动，就是科学家的功夫。别急着打断孩子的“为什么”，爱问为什么的孩子离 scientist 最近。

📖 微型故事《蹲下来的十分钟》

院子里的蚂蚁在搬一块饼干屑。妞妞蹲下来，十分钟没说话。妈妈叫她，她摆手：“Fabre watched insects for hours. He was a great scientist!” 妞妞掏出小本子，画下蚂蚁的路线：“Ants can carry things heavier than themselves!” 妈妈凑近一看，真的！原来科学家不是都待在实验室，有的就蹲在院子里。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What was Niuniu watching? (Ants carrying a biscuit crumb.)
• Who is Fabre? (A great scientist who studied insects.)
• 问问孩子：你观察过什么小动物？它有什么“本领”？

🏛️ 词源故事

science 来自拉丁语 scientia（知识），词根 scire 是“知道”。有趣的是，scientist 这个词 1834 年才被英国学者休厄尔仿照 artist（艺术家）造出来——给“搞研究的人”起了个正式名字。在这之前，牛顿那一代人被叫作 natural philosopher（自然哲学家）。
🎯 讲给孩子：scientist 竟是仿着 artist 造的——科学和艺术，本是一家人。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“If I have seen further, it is by standing on the shoulders of giants.” 🍎
如果说我看得更远，那是因为我站在巨人的肩膀上。—— 课本科学家牛顿1675年写给同行的话：再牛的科学家，也要踩着前人的台阶。

🎵 歌词里的 scientist

🎵 Thomas Dolby《She Blinded Me with Science》（1982）：Billboard 前十的“科学神曲”，歌名直译“她用科学晃瞎了我的眼”——science 唱进了流行乐，实验室里全是节拍器。
🔗 与 science 页互链（同曲）；歌名里的 blinded 又与 blind 页撞了个满怀——三个词一家亲。

⚡ 冷知识

scientist 一词 1834 年才出生——比蒸汽火车还年轻！而课本里的法布尔更有意思：他写《昆虫记》一写十卷，观察一只虫子能蹲一整天，被称为“昆虫界的荷马”。科学的第一步不是仪器，是耐心。
