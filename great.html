Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and repeat

Binbin: That's great.

Lesson 2 · B. Do a survey
— What do you want to be in the future?
— I want to be a police officer.

Lesson 3 · A. Read and match（短文）
I want to do many jobs in the future.
On Mondays, I want to be a police officer. That must be a cool job.
On Tuesdays, I want to be a doctor. I can help sick people.
On Wednesdays and Thursdays, I want to be a teacher. I can teach English.
I want to be a farmer on Fridays.
On the weekend, I just want to be myself. I want to play from morning to evening. That sounds fun!

Let's Spell
ar: arm, bar · or: horn, pork · er: driver, tiger, turtle · ur: fur, dirt, bird

Let's Check · A. 听力（人物自述）
Hi, I'm Amenda. I want to be a police officer in the future. It's a cool job.
Hello, I'm Van. I like to eat. I want to be a cook in the future. Then I can eat any time.
Hello, I'm Alex. I want to be a farmer in the future. It's an interesting job.

Fun Time · A. Good to know

Fun Time · C. Let's sing

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写gr-ea-t：ea 组合发 /eɪ/
✏️ 口诀：ea 手拉手念 /eɪ/，great 好极啦
音·发音/ɡreɪt/ 与 mate、late 押韵
🎧 跟读：That's great! · a great idea · the greatest
意·释义① 好极了（夸奖）② 伟大的（great man 伟人）③ 很多的（a great many）
用·句型① That's great. (课文句：那好极了)
② That's a great idea! (好主意！)
③ You did a great job! (你干得漂亮！)
④ — Let's play! — Great! (——玩吧！——太好了！)
拓·同族词great job 干得好 · Great Wall 长城 · great-grandma 曾祖母 · the greatest 最伟大的

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
回应— I want to be a nurse. — That's great!

赞同Great! Let's go!
描述The Great Wall is great.
📋 功能句总结 · That's great! 万能回应术
对方说理想 → That's great!
对方出主意 → Great idea!
对方完成任务 → Great job!
对方邀请 → Great! Let's go!
⚠️ 一个 great 加不同尾巴，回应一切场景——高情商英语第一步
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

great 是课本教的第一句“高情商回应”。孩子说 I want to be a cook，家长立刻接 That's great! 形成条件反射。反过来也让孩子练：家长说梦想，孩子接 That's great! 注意 great 的赞美必须具体才真诚——夸 Great job! 时最好说明哪里好：Your pronunciation is great!

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《小小夸夸团》

🌟 班里成立“夸夸团”，规则只有一个：必须用 great 夸人。小胖说：Dad made noodles — great cook! 丽丽说：Grandma tells stories — great stories! 轮到小安，他挠挠头，看向窗外扫地的环卫阿姨：Workers clean our streets — great workers! 老师带头鼓掌：That's a great one. 原来 great 不只送给家人，也送给every worker in the city. 🏙️

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What is the rule of the club? (Say great things)
• Who did Xiao An praise? (A street cleaner)
• 问问孩子：今天你想对谁说一句 That's great?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 夸奖 ── That's great! · Great job!
├── 赞同 ── Great idea! · Great!
├── 伟大 ── a great man · the greatest
├── 地名 ── the Great Wall 长城
└── 辈分 ── great-grandma 曾祖母

🏛️ 词源故事

great 来自古英语 grēat，本义是“粗大的、厚实的”——先说块头大，才慢慢引申出“伟大、好极了”。英语里“伟大”从来都和“大”有关：Alexander the Great（亚历山大大帝）、Great Britain（大不列颠）。great-grandma 里的 great 是“隔一代”的记号：grandma 奶奶，great-grandma 曾祖母。🎯 讲给孩子：great 的老家是一个“大”字，夸人 great，就是说他“大写的好”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

"Great minds think alike." 🧠
英雄所见略同。—— 英语经典谚语。两人不约而同想到一处时来一句，比任何解释都帅。

🎵 歌词里的 great

🎵 Jerry Lee Lewis《Great Balls of Fire!》(1957)：摇滚元年传世神曲，钢琴砸奏名场面；歌名里的 great 蹦得比琴键还高。

⚡ 冷知识

Great Britain 的“大”不是自夸：历史上为了区别对岸的“小不列颠”（法国布列塔尼 Brittany），本岛才叫 Great Britain——大是地理位置的大。而长城为什么叫 the Great Wall？因为绵延万里，一眼望不到头，“大”到从太空传说里都能看见（虽然宇航员证实肉眼其实看不见）。大，就是 great 最诚实的含义。
