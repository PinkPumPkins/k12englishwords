📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写sub（下面）+ ject（扔、放）= 摆在面前要对付的题目→学科！
✏️ 口诀：sub 下 ject 放，摆下面的是学科。
音·发音/ˈsʌb.dʒɪkt/ 重音在前
🎧 跟读：all my subjects · my favourite subject · What subjects do you like?
意·释义学科、科目（English, maths, science, PE...）；也指主题；语法里是主语
用·句型① I like all my subjects. (我喜欢所有学科。)
② What's your favourite subject? (你最喜欢的科目是什么？)
③ We have six subjects this term. (这学期我们有六门课。)
④ My favourite subject is English. (我最喜欢的学科是英语。)
拓·同族词学科清单：English 英语 · maths 数学 · science 科学 · PE 体育；同根词 project 项目

🏛️ 词源故事

subject 来自拉丁语 subjectum：sub（在下面）+ jacere（扔、放）——字面是“被放在下面的东西”。古罗马人用它指臣民（被压在王权下的人），中世纪学者把它借来指摆在面前讨论的题目（话题），学校里又变成摆在课桌上的功课（学科），语法课还让它兼职主语。一个词被放了三次，就长出了三个意思。
🎯 讲给孩子：subject 是“被放下来给你做的事”——话题、功课、主语，全是放出来的。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Tell me and I forget. Teach me and I remember. Involve me and I learn.” 📚 告诉我我会忘，教我我能记，让我参与我才会。—— 这句常被归于富兰克林名下的名言，更接近中国《荀子》闻之不若见之、知之不若行之的意思：每个 subject 都要亲手参与才能学会。

⚡ 冷知识

课本 Lesson 3 的信是全单元的作文范本：Worried 用一句话自我诊断——I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks.——一个 but 转折，把喜欢学习和成绩不好的矛盾摆了出来。英语写作的精髓就藏在这里：先夸后转，问题浮现，琳达的建议才有处方可开。
