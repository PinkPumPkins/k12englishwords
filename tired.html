📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写t-i-r-e-d，动词 tire（使疲倦）+ d。
✏️ 口诀：tire 加 d，人就很 tired。
音·发音/ˈtaɪəd/ 注意是“泰厄德”，别读成“提尔德”
🎧 跟读：I'm tired. · feel tired · tired and sleepy
意·释义疲劳的、困倦的（人说累）；tiring 才是“令人疲倦的”（事累人）
用·句型① I'm tired. What should I do? (我累了，该怎么办？)
② He is always tired after school. (他放学后总是很累。)
③ The trip was tiring. (这趟旅行很累人。)
④ I'm tired of candy. (我吃糖吃腻了。)
拓·同族词家族词：tire 动词 · tiring 令人累的 · be tired of 对…厌倦

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
状态I'm tired. What should I do?
习惯He always feels tired in class.
对比tired（人累）/ tiring（事累）
短语I'm tired of watching TV.（厌倦）
📋 功能句总结 · 说累与给建议（本单元对话骨架）
说累：I'm tired. / I feel tired and sleepy.
问：What's wrong?（你怎么了？）
答：I'm tired. What should I do?
建议：You should go to bed early and exercise every day.
⚠️ 人用 tired，事用 tiring：I am tired. / The game is tiring.
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

课本用 I'm tired. 演了一段母子对话，在家可以原样重演：孩子喊 I'm tired，家长追问 What's wrong? 再给建议 You should go to bed early. 一轮下来，tired / What's wrong / should 三组句子全练了。重点盯 tired/tiring 的分工。

📖 微型故事《星期一的小豆芽》

💫 小豆芽最怕星期一：I'm always tired on Mondays! 上课眼皮打架，铅笔都拿不稳。同桌小松鼠递来一张纸条：Sleep early, not screen late!（早睡别熬夜）小豆芽决定试试：9点收起平板，9点半 go to bed。星期二早上，他跳下床：Where is my tired? 全班都笑了。原来 tired 是可以被早睡赶跑的小怪兽。🌱

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Why is Sprout tired on Mondays? (He sleeps late)
• What did the note say? (Sleep early, not screen late)
• 问问孩子：What makes YOU tired? 用英语说一说，再给一句 should 建议

🗺️ 思维导图

└── 处方 ── 早睡 + 锻炼 = 赶跑 tired

🏛️ 词源故事

tire 来自古英语 tēorian，本义是“力气被一点点用完”。tired 就是“力气用完的样子”。英语里 tired 身兼两职：既说身体累，也说心里腻（be tired of），还顺带说困——所以课本里 Bill 说 I'm tired，妈妈立刻懂了：该锻炼、该早睡。
🎯 讲给孩子：tired 像“电池用光”，充电方式就是睡觉和运动。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
tired累的（人）：I am tired.
tiring累人的（事）：The trip is tiring.
be tired of厌倦：I'm tired of eating candy.
sleepy困的（想睡）：I feel sleepy in class.

📜 名言 & 谚语

“A change is as good as a rest.” 🔄 换个花样如同休息。—— 英语老谚语：累了不一定只靠睡觉，换件事做做也是一种休息，正好呼应课本 Try to walk more and help your mum do some housework.

🎵 歌词里的 tired

🎵 The Beatles《A Hard Day's Night》(1964)：甲壳虫同名电影主题曲，开头一声和弦传奇；辛苦一天的歌名正是 tired 的绝佳注脚。
🔗 与 busy 页互链（同曲）——忙了一天，自然又累又困。

⚡ 冷知识

tired 一词在课本里出场四次以上，但英语里“累”其实分好几种：身体累用 tired，想睡用 sleepy，厌倦用 tired of。琳达给 Worried 的信里两个词都用上了：I always feel tired and sleepy——又累又困，正是熬夜党的标配。
