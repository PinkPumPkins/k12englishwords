📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写bore（使厌倦）+ ing → boring 令人乏味的；bore + ed → bored 感到厌倦的
✏️ 口诀：-ing 给物（boring film），-ed 给人（I'm bored）
音·发音/ˈbɔː.rɪŋ/ 两音节，重音在前
🎧 跟读：a boring day · How boring! · boring → bored
意·释义乏味的、无聊的（形容事物）。形容“人感到无聊”要用 bored
用·句型① The film was boring. (这部电影很乏味。)
② — How was your weekend? — Boring! I stayed at home all day.
③ I'm bored. Let's do something fun! (我感到无聊，找点乐子吧！)
④ What a boring rainy day! (多无聊的雨天！)
拓·同族词反义词：interesting 有趣的 · fun 好玩的
-ing/-ed 家族：boring/bored · interesting/interested · exciting/excited

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
评价The book was boring. I didn't finish it.
感受I'm bored. What can we do?
反义The cartoon isn't boring. It's interesting!
周末My Sunday was a little boring, but I read a good book.
功能句总结 · -ing 与 -ed 感情形容词

-ing 修饰物（性质）：The game is boring.（游戏很乏味）
-ed 修饰人（感受）：I am bored.（我感到无聊）

⚠️ 说 I'm boring 意思是“我这个人很乏味”——想表达“我好无聊”请说 I'm bored！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

孩子喊 I'm bored 时，正是最好的英语教学时机：教一句 Boredom is a choice — let's make something! 然后把无聊的下午变成本单元素材：climbed a hill / played chess / jumped rope。顺带纠正 I'm boring 这个经典错误——说错等于自评“我很无趣”。

📖 微型故事《The Boring Box》

下雨的周六，Leo 嚷嚷：I'm bored! 奶奶神秘地搬出一个旧盒子：这是“boring盒子”，专治无聊。里面没有玩具——只有纸、笔、绳子和一张挑战卡：用盒子里的东西发明一个游戏。Leo 用绳子跳了50下、用纸做了棋盘、和奶奶下了自创棋。傍晚他宣布：The boring box is not boring at all! 奶奶眨眨眼：因为无聊只是还没开始创造。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What was in the box? (Paper, a pen, a rope and a challenge card)
• What did Leo invent? (A jump rope game and a new chess game)
• 问问孩子：What would you invent with paper and rope?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 构词 ── bore+ing → boring(物) · bore+ed → bored(人)
├── 反义 ── interesting / fun
├── 周末感受 ── How was your weekend? → Boring!/Great!
├── 解药 ── climb · chess · rope · music（本单元全家桶）
└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── Are You Bored Yet? 对唱问答

🏛️ 词源故事

bore（使厌倦）这个词18世纪才出现在英语里，词源学家至今争论它的出身——有人猜和法语的“塞满、喂饱”有关（吃撑了也是种腻），但没有定论。巧的是，“无聊”成为被严肃讨论的现代话题也是同一时期：城市变大了，休闲时间变多了，人第一次有空“闲得发慌”。🎯 讲给孩子：boring 是个年轻的词，也是现代生活的一面镜子。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Variety is the spice of life.” 🌶
变化是生活的调味品。——出自英国诗人威廉·柯珀的长诗《任务》（1785）。怕 boring？给生活加点新花样。

🎵 歌词里的 boring

🎵 Wallows《Are You Bored Yet?》(2019)：年轻乐队的对唱热曲（与 Clairo 合作），歌名一句话就是课堂句型 Are you bored yet?——知名度中等，胜在句型满分。

🎵 与本单元 badminton / played chess 等“解药词”页互链；bored 的时候就来背单词呀。

⚡ 冷知识

心理学里有个有趣的“无聊效应”：人在无聊时容易走神，但走神恰恰是大脑“默认模式网络”活跃的时候——很多好点子是在洗澡、发呆时冒出来的。所以偶尔 boring 一下不是坏事，那是创造力在充电。
