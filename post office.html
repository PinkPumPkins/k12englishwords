📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写post(邮政) + office(办公室) → 管邮政的办公室就是邮局
✏️ 口诀：post 管寄件，office 是屋子，合起来寄信不用愁！
音·发音/ˈpəʊst/ 长音，office 重音在前 /ˈɒfɪs/
🎧 跟读：the post office · Is there a post office near here?
意·释义邮局 📮（寄信、寄包裹、买邮票的地方）
用·句型① Is there a post office in the neighbourhood? (这附近有邮局吗？)
② How can I get to the post office? (去邮局怎么走？)
③ It's next to Joy's home. (邮局紧邻Joy家。)
④ Mum mails a letter at the post office. (妈妈在邮局寄信。)
拓·同族词postman 邮递员 · postcard 明信片 · postcode 邮编；mail a present 寄礼物（与 mail a present 页互链）

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

Unit 1 的核心场景就是问路去邮局。在家用纸箱贴上 post office / bank / grocery 摆一条“小街”，家长当路人问 Is there a post office in the neighbourhood? 孩子当向导用 go straight / turn left 指路，一轮五分钟，句型全学会。

📖 微型故事《一封走遍街区的信》

📮 明明给奶奶写了一张明信片：“I want to send it to my grandma!” 他走进 post office，贴上一枚邮票。明信片装进绿色邮包，坐上邮车，穿过 crossroads，两天后到达奶奶家。奶奶说：“The little postman crossed the whole neighbourhood to see me!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who wrote the postcard? (明明)
• Where did he go? (The post office)
• 问问孩子：你想寄一张明信片给谁？

🏛️ 词源故事

post（邮政）来自拉丁语 ponere（安放）：古时驿道沿线“安放”的换马站就叫 poste，信件一站一站“接力安放”传递。mail（邮件）更有趣——它原本指邮差背的大皮袋（古法语 male），袋子里装的信后来也跟着叫 mail。post office 字面就是“邮政站点的办公室”。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
post officepost(驿站/邮政) + office(办公室) → 邮局
postmanpost + man → 邮递员
postcardpost + card → 明信片（不用信封直接寄的卡片）

📜 名言 & 谚语

纽约邮局大楼门楣上刻着美国邮政的座右铭：“Neither snow nor rain nor heat nor gloom of night stays these couriers from the swift completion of their appointed rounds.”（风雪雨热夜，都挡不住信使完成使命。）它改编自古希腊历史学家希罗多德对波斯驿使的描写。

🎵 歌词里的 post office

🎵 The Marvelettes《Please Mr. Postman》(1961)：Motown 第一支 Billboard 冠军单曲，“Please Mr. Postman, look and see”一开口就是等信的心情；卡朋特(The Carpenters)翻唱版后来也再度登顶。与 mail a present 页、send 页同曲互链。

⚡ 冷知识

世界上第一枚不干胶邮票是1840年英国的“黑便士”（Penny Black），上面印着年轻的维多利亚女王头像——从此寄信不再按里程手写计费，贴张邮票就能走遍全国。
