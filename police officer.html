Lesson 3 · A. Read and match（短文）
I want to do many jobs in the future.
On Mondays, I want to be a police officer. That must be a cool job.
On Tuesdays, I want to be a doctor. I can help sick people.
On Wednesdays and Thursdays, I want to be a teacher. I can teach English.
I want to be a farmer on Fridays.
On the weekend, I just want to be myself. I want to play from morning to evening. That sounds fun!

Let's Spell
ar: arm, bar · or: horn, pork · er: driver, tiger, turtle · ur: fur, dirt, bird

Let's Check · A. 听力（人物自述）
Hi, I'm Amenda. I want to be a police officer in the future. It's a cool job.
Hello, I'm Van. I like to eat. I want to be a cook in the future. Then I can eat any time.
Hello, I'm Alex. I want to be a farmer in the future. It's an interesting job.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写police + officer 两个词连写分读
✏️ 口诀：police 管治安，officer 是官，合体就是人民警察
音·发音/pəˈliːs ˈɒfɪsə(r)/ 注意 c 发 /s/
🎧 跟读：a police officer · the police · police station
意·释义警察 👮（police 常作集合名词 the police=警方；police officer 指一名警察）
用·句型① I want to be a police officer. (课文句)
② The police officer helps people. (警察帮助人们。)
③ That must be a cool job! (那一定很酷！课文句)
④ Call the police! (快报警！)
拓·同族词policeman 男警察 · policewoman 女警察 · police station 警察局 · officer 也可指军官

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
愿望I want to be a police officer in the future.
评价That must be a cool job.
求助Call the police, please!
描述The police officer keeps us safe.
📋 功能句总结 · cool job 评价句（课文同款）
夸职业三连：That must be a cool job!（那一定很酷！）
It's an interesting job.（这工作有意思。Let's Check 句）
It's a cool job.（Let's Check 句）
⚠️ 句式 = 主语 + must be / is + a + 形容词 + job
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

police officer 是本单元句型的“样板词”——调查、短文、听力全都用它示范。在家玩职业调查 Do a survey：轮流问 What do you want to be in the future? 答不出就借课文句 I want to be a police officer. It's a cool job. 顺带讲清 the police（警方整体）和 a police officer（一名警察）的差别。

🏛️ 词源故事

police 来自法语 police，一路追上去是希腊语 politeia——城邦的管理之道。它和 politics（政治）、policy（政策）、polite（有礼貌的）本是四兄弟，都管“怎么把一座城治理好”。officer 来自拉丁语 officium（职责）：担当职责的人。所以 police officer 的字面意思，就是“管治安的职责担当者”。🎯 讲给孩子：警察、政治、礼貌，原来说的是同一件事——让城市好好运转。

📜 名言 & 谚语

"To protect and to serve." 🛡️
保护与服务。—— 美国洛杉矶警局（LAPD）自1955年起的著名警训，后来成为全球警察文化的标志性口号。cool 的不是制服，是这两件天天要做的事。

🎵 歌词里的 police officer

🎵 The Police《Every Breath You Take》(1983)：Billboard 八周冠军；乐队名字就叫“警察”——“Every breath you take, I'll be watching you” 让 police 这个词自带BGM。

⚡ 冷知识

世界上最早的紧急电话号码是英国的999，1937年启用；美国是911，中国是110。英国多数警察执勤时不配枪，靠警棍和出镜率维持治安，是警务史上的独特传统。伦敦警察厅总部叫 Scotland Yard——它根本不在苏格兰，名字来自当年办公地点的一条旧路。
