📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写writ·er —— write（写）去 e 加 er；write 里有个安静的 e 不发音
✏️ 口诀：write 去尾 e 添 er，写字的人是 writer。
音·发音/ˈraɪ.tə/ 两个音节，重音在前；wr 开头 w 不发音
🎧 跟读：a famous writer · write stories for children.
意·释义作家 ✍️（写书、写故事的人；write 写 → writer 作家；“作者”也用 author）
用·句型① Andersen was a great writer. (安徒生是伟大的作家。)
② He wrote many stories for children. (他为孩子们写了很多故事。)
③ I want to be a writer. (我想当作家。)
④ The writer is writing a new book. (作家正在写新书。)
拓·同族词write 写 → writer 作家 → writing 写作；write-wrote-written 三态要记牢；author 作者（侧重出版作品）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
名人介绍Andersen was a Danish writer. He wrote many stories for children.
梦想I want to be a writer and write stories about animals.
作品Her favourite writer is J.K. Rowling.
动词三态write → wrote → written：He has written many books.
📋 功能句总结 · -er：做事的人

动词 + er = 职业：write 写 → writer 作家；teach 教 → teacher 教师；work 工作 → worker 工人。

课本句：He was a Danish writer. He wrote many stories for children.（他是一位丹麦作家，为孩子们写了很多故事。）

⚠️ write 去 e 加 er；author 也是“作者”，更书面。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

writer 一词连着课本里的安徒生与 Story Time 的 J.K. 罗琳（她被多家出版社拒绝仍坚持投稿）。陪学三步：1) 读一个安徒生童话，问 Who wrote this story?；2) 讲罗琳被拒绝不放弃——作家第一美德是“写完再改、改完再投”；3) 鼓励孩子用英文写三句话的小故事，署名 little writer。

📖 微型故事《给安徒生的信》

朵朵读完《丑小鸭》，写了一封不会寄出的信：“Dear Andersen, I was an ugly duckling in my class. But I will be a swan one day.” 妈妈看到，轻轻说：“这位 writer 30 岁才成名，150 年后的孩子还在读他。你这封信，就是最好的读书报告。” 朵朵把信夹进书里——也许多年后，她真的会成为 writer。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who did Duoduo write to? (Andersen, the writer.)
• What did she call herself? (An ugly duckling.)
• 问问孩子：如果你能给一位作家写信，你想写给谁？问什么？

🏛️ 词源故事

write 来自古英语 writan，本义是“刻划”——最早的“写”，是用刀在木头和石头上刻出来的。所以 writer 字面就是“刻字的人”。从刻痕到墨水，再到键盘，这个词跟了人类上千年，还是那双手的动作。
🎯 讲给孩子：每写一个词，都是小小“刻痕”，落在别人心上。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
write写（v.）：write a story 写故事
writing写作（n.）：good writing 好文笔
author作者（书面）：the author of this book
write → wrote → written不规则三态：I write, I wrote, I have written

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Brevity is the soul of wit.” 📜
简洁是智慧的灵魂。—— 莎士比亚《哈姆雷特》：最好的 writer，都懂得把多余的词删掉。

🎵 歌词里的 writer

🎵 The Beatles《Paperback Writer》（1966）：披头士经典单曲，书信体开场“Dear Sir or Madam”——一位 writer 想出版自己平装书的自荐信，唱得理直气壮。
🔗 与 write a letter 页互链：write 家族再添一员——letter（信）和 story（故事）都是 writer 的作品。

⚡ 冷知识

《哈利·波特》曾被 12 家出版社拒绝，第 13 家才接受它——那时 J.K. 罗琳的生活一度很拮据。另一个冷知识：安徒生一生写了 150 多篇童话，被译成 150 多种语言，是“被翻译最多的丹麦作家”。被拒绝不可怕，停笔才可怕。
