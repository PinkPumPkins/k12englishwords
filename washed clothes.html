📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

✅ 原文经51jiaoxi英语朗读宝跟读页、好多云课文翻译页、zxxk课件与教案摘要多源交叉核对（智慧山源页访问故障未采信）。本词与本单元家务词族同源（cleaned the window / clean the room）；Let's Spell 环节 washed 是 -ed 读 /t/ 的代表词。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写wash + ed —— wash 以 sh 结尾，直接加 ed（ed 读 /t/）
✏️ 口诀：wash 冲一冲，加 ed 洗干净
音·发音/wɒʃt/ 一个音节轻快收尾；clothes /kləʊðz/ 注意 th 咬舌
🎧 跟读：washed clothes · washed my hands · wash → washed
意·释义洗衣服（过去发生）。wash 洗；clothes 衣服（永远复数形式）
用·句型① I washed clothes with Mum. (我和妈妈一起洗了衣服。)
② I washed my schoolbag. It was dirty. (我洗了书包，它很脏。)
③ Wash your hands before dinner! (饭前洗手！)
④ Did you wash clothes last weekend?
拓·同族词wash 家族：wash hands 洗手 · wash dishes 洗碗 · washing machine 洗衣机
与 sweep the floor / clean the room 组成家务英语三件套

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
家务I washed clothes with my mum.
卫生I washed my hands before dinner.
现在对照I often wash clothes on Sundays.
询问Did you wash clothes or clean the room last weekend?
功能句总结 · 家务分工

询问：What chores do you do?（你做什么家务？）
汇报：I washed clothes. / I swept the floor. / I cleaned the room.
夸奖：Good job! You're a big help!（干得好！你是大帮手！）

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

家务是过去式的实战场：周末安排孩子一项家务，完成后用英语汇报 I washed clothes.，家长回应 Good job! 再互相交换：Mum washed dishes. Dad cleaned the car. 一句家务一句英语，全家的 weekend 都成了口语教材。注意提醒 clothes 的 th 发音。

📖 微型故事《The Pink Sock Surprise》

妈妈教 Amy 用洗衣机。Amy 把红T恤和白袜子一起塞进去——按钮一按，转呀转……开门一看：白袜子全变粉色了！Oops! Amy 红着脸把粉袜子排成一排：Look, Mum! I washed clothes... and made them pink! 妈妈笑出声：下次白色深色要分开洗哦。从此 Amy 成了家里的“分类洗衣小专家”。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Amy wash together? (A red T-shirt and white socks)
• What color were the socks after washing? (Pink)
• 问问孩子：Why do we wash white and dark clothes separately?

🏛️ 词源故事

wash 来自古英语 wæscan，就是“用水洗”。clothes 来自 cloth（布），衣服本义就是“布做的物件”——所以它天生是复数形式。洗衣机 washing machine 是19世纪末的伟大发明：在它出现前，洗一缸衣服要搓、拧、晾整整一天。🎯 讲给孩子：washed clothes 一词，记录了人类从搓衣板到洗衣机的百年进化。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Cleanliness is next to godliness.” 🧼
清洁近乎圣洁。——英语谚语（因约翰·卫斯理的布道而广为流传）。washed clothes 洗净的不只是衣服，还有一周的疲惫。

🎵 歌词里的 washed clothes

🎵 Taylor Swift《Clean》(2014)：《1989》专辑口碑曲，Rain came pouring... I'm clean, clean——暴雨过后，洗净重启。clean 既指“干净”也指“释怀”。

🎵 与 wash clothes 页互链（同源曲目）；和本单元 clean 家族（cleaned the window 等）一气相通。

⚡ 冷知识

世界上最早的电动洗衣机之一叫“雷神”（Thor）：1908年前后上市，像个铁皮桶加电动机。在那之前，“洗衣服”意味着烧水、搓板、拧干、晾晒的一整天劳动——所以一台“雷神”进家门，主妇们像迎接超级英雄。
