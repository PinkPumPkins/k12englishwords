📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 Keeping Healthy（保持健康）

Get ready.
What should/shouldn't you do?
eat too much candy · eat too much meat · go to bed early · exercise every day · tired · drink milk every day · drink too much juice · wash hands before eating · eat with dirty hands

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number
— Tom, you are a little fat. Do you often eat meat? — Yes. I often eat a lot of meat. I like to eat candy, too.
— That's bad for you. You shouldn't eat too much meat. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. You also shouldn't eat too much candy.
— Please do your homework, Bill. — Sorry, Mum. I'm tired.
— You are always tired. You should exercise every day and go to bed early.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and say
— Hello, Frank. What's wrong? — I've got a stomachache. I often have stomachaches. What should I do?
— You should drink some water. Do you eat a lot of candy? — Yes, I eat some after every class.
— You shouldn't eat too much candy. Do you wash your hands before eating? — Not always.
— You shouldn't eat with dirty hands. You should always wash your hands before eating.
B. Let's play（小护士给建议）：— I've got a headache. / I've got a toothache. / I'm tired.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（两封信）
Dear Linda, I always feel tired and sleepy. I sometimes sleep in class. I like my teachers and I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks. At night I can't sleep, so I go on the computer or watch TV. What should I do? — Worried
Dear Worried, Many students have this problem. I have some advice for you. First, you should exercise every day. Next, you shouldn't play too much on the computer or watch too much TV. Finally, you should go to bed before 9:30. Try these things. I think you'll soon feel better. — Linda

Let's Check · C（节选）
— What's your name? — Zhang Xiang. — What's wrong? — I have a toothache. — Do you often eat candy? — Yes, I like it very much. — You shouldn't eat too much candy. You should brush your teeth every day.

Story Time · The Best Wish（节选）
I wish for a big dinner. / I wish for a new dog. / Not too big. Not too expensive... I wish for good health.

✅ 原文经教习网英语朗读宝全课时跟读页、电子课本与同步练习资源多源交叉核对，智慧山确认单元目录。mark 在 Lesson 3 的信里一击即中：I like all my subjects, but I don't get good marks.——琳达的全部建议（锻炼、少玩电脑、早睡）都为这一句服务：让 marks 回来。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写m-a-r-k 四个字母，一竖一横就写完。
✏️ 口诀：mark 是记号，考试画圈圈，圈圈就是分。
音·发音/mɑːk/ 英音长音不卷舌；美音 /mɑːrk/ 卷舌
🎧 跟读：get good marks · full marks · make a mark
意·释义分数（英式，美式说 grade）；记号、痕迹（本义）
用·句型① I don't get good marks. (我拿不到好分数。)
② She got full marks in English. (她英语得了满分。)
③ Mark your name on the book. (在书上写名字做记号。——动词用法)
④ The teacher gave me a good mark. (老师给了我个好分数。)
拓·同族词家族词：full marks 满分 · make a mark 做记号；美式 grade

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句I don't get good marks.
满分She got full marks.
英美marks（英式）= grades（美式）
本义Make a mark here.（做个记号）
📋 功能句总结 · 谈分数（英美说法对照）
英式：get good marks（课本用法）
美式：get good grades
问：Did you get good marks?
答：Yes, I got full marks! / Not this time.
⚠️ mark 本义是记号——最早的分数就是老师在纸边画的记号！
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

孩子拿到试卷后，先别看分数先找 mark（记号）的本义：圈对的红圈、订正的叉，都是老师做的 marks。再引申到分数义：英式 marks / 美式 grades。最后呼应课本：I don't get good marks 的解法不是刷题，而是早睡加锻炼——身体好，分数自然会回来。

📖 微型故事《纸上的小红花》

💫 卷子王国里住着一位 mark 小精灵，专门住在老师红笔尖上。老师批卷，它就跳下来：对的画小花，错的画圈圈。小杰总说：I don't get good marks... 小精灵急了，托梦给他：mark 只是记号，真正得分的是你每天做的事——sleep early, exercise, turn off TV! 小杰照做一个月，红笔尖上跳下来的小花越来越多。小精灵眨眼：See? Good marks follow good habits! 🌸

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Where does the mark fairy live? (On the teacher's red pen)
• What three things did the fairy tell Xiaojie to do? (Sleep early, exercise, turn off TV)
• 问问孩子：What are good habits for good marks? 用英语列出你的三件事

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 本义 ── mark 记号、痕迹
├── 分数 ── get good marks（英式）
├── 美式 ── grade 分数
├── 满分 ── full marks
└── 课本 ── but I don't get good marks.

🏛️ 词源故事

mark 来自古英语 mearc，本义是边界、界标——古代人在地界立石头做记号，那块石头就叫 mearc。从边界记号长出任何记号，再长出老师打的分数。它的亲戚遍布地图：Denmark（丹麦）就是“丹人的边地”；动词 march（行军）最初就是向边界行进。
🎯 讲给孩子：mark 的祖先是界碑——分数只是它后来找到的新工作。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
mark（记号）本义：Make a mark on the paper.
mark（分数）英式：get good marks
grade（分数）美式：get good grades
full marks满分：She got full marks in maths.

📜 名言 & 谚语

“No pain, no gain.” 🌱 不劳无获。—— 健身房墙上的经典谚语，放在学习里同样成立：每天早睡、锻炼、认真听讲，好 marks 就是汗水的收成。

🎵 歌词里的 mark

🎵 Chuck Berry《School Days》(1957)：摇滚先驱经典，Ring ring goes the bell 上课铃一响，校园主题最强音——marks 就藏在 School Days 里。
🔗 与 grade 页互链（同曲 School Days）；与 school 页互链（同曲）。

⚡ 冷知识

英美对分数的叫法是一张方言地图：英国人说 marks（本义记号），美国人说 grades（等第）；英国满分的说法 full marks 到了美国常说 a perfect score。课本选用 marks，因为人教新起点教材以英式读音和用法为主——这也是为什么课本写 maths（英式）而不是 math（美式）。
