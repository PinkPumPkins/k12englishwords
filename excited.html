📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Feelings（情绪）

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number（小狗连环画，情绪九宫格）
My dog climbs on the table and eats Lily's lunch, so I am angry.（狗偷吃午餐→生气）
He's ill, and I'm worried.（狗病了→担心）
My dog suddenly runs over to Yaoyao, so Yaoyao is scared.（狗冲向瑶瑶→害怕）
I can't find my dog. It makes me cry. → I am very sad.（找不到狗→哭→难过）
He comes back home. I am very excited.（狗回家了→激动）
My dog has a running race. He runs very fast and wins the race. I am proud.（赢赛跑→自豪）

Lesson 1 · B. Let's talk（情绪接龙）
— I am going to sing for my class.（自豪）
— My grandma is ill.（担心）
— My cousin takes away my model plane.（生气）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（老照片里的为什么）
— You look sad in this picture. Why?
— I was sad because I was ill. I had to stay in bed.
— I was happy because Andy came to see me and helped me with my maths.
— I was worried because I wasn't good at maths. I didn't like maths tests.
— Did you do well in the maths test? — Yes. Look, I got an A. I was so proud. — Congratulations!

Lesson 2 · B. Choose and talk（because 句型操练）
— You look sad. Why?
— I am sad because I am ill. I can't play with my friends. I have to stay in bed.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Andy 的日记两则）
Wednesday：I got an A on my maths test. I felt very happy. … After school we went to a singing competition. We sang together and won first place, so I felt very proud.
Friday：My grandmother was ill. She went to hospital, and I was very worried. … I played basketball with my friend Jiayi. I hurt my leg, so I was sad.

Fun Time · Good to know（颜色与情绪）
To 'see red' means to get very angry.（see red＝勃然大怒）
In English, you 'feel blue' when you are sad.（feel blue＝心情低落）
但在中国，红色是喜庆的颜色：春节穿红衣、发红包。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写ex·cit·ed —— excite（使兴奋）+ d；三个音节，别漏中间的 e
✏️ 口诀：excite 点火，加 d 变心情！
音·发音/ɪkˈsaɪ.tɪd/ 重音在第二音节，cite 读 /saɪt/
🎧 跟读：be excited about · so excited · get excited
意·释义激动的、兴奋的 🤩（形容人的内心感受；形容事物本身令人兴奋，用 exciting）
用·句型① I am so excited about the trip. (想到旅行我就激动。)
② He comes back home. I am very excited. (他回家了，我非常激动。)
③ I was excited to meet my new friend. (见到新朋友我很兴奋。)
④ The football match is exciting. (这场足球赛很精彩。)
拓·同族词excite 使兴奋 → excited 感到兴奋的（人）→ exciting 令人兴奋的（物）→ excitement 兴奋（n.）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
表达期待I'm excited about the school trip.
原因句I was excited because our team won.
见面I was excited to meet her.
对比I am excited（我兴奋）；The game is exciting（比赛精彩）。
📋 功能句总结 · excited 与 exciting 的分水岭

excited 形容人（内心感受）：I am excited.（我很兴奋。）

exciting 形容物（带来兴奋）：The game is exciting.（比赛令人兴奋。）

⚠️ 一句话分清：被点燃的是人（excited），点火的是物（exciting）——The trip is exciting, so I'm excited.

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

excited 是孩子的高频情绪。出游、生日等场景前问一句 Are you excited? 引导孩子用 I'm so excited because... 完整表达。顺便辨析：人 excited，事 exciting——孩子能说对 The trip is exciting, so I'm excited，就是真懂了。

📖 微型故事《返校日的神秘嘉宾》

周一早晨，班门口贴了张海报：“神秘嘉宾来上课！”全班瞬间沸腾，小雨激动得转笔都掉了三次：“I'm so excited! Who is it?” 上课铃响，门开了——是市队的前锋，还带着签名的世界杯足球！他讲训练故事时，全班安静得能听见心跳。下课后小雨在日记里写：“I was excited because a real football star came to our class.” 激动这东西，像汽水，开盖那一下最上头。

• How did Xiaoyu feel? (Very excited.)
• 问问孩子：What makes you the most excited at school?

🗺️ 思维导图

excited 激动的
├── 搭配 ── be excited about · be excited to do
├── 分辨 ── 人 excited / 物 exciting
├── 家族 ── excite 使兴奋 · excitement 兴奋
├── 亲缘 ── cite 引用 · recite 背诵（拉丁同根）
└── 反义 ── bored 感到无聊的（物 boring）

🏛️ 词源故事

excite 来自拉丁语 excitare（唤起、点燃），由 ex-（向外）+ citare（召唤、催动）组成，和 cite（引用）、recite（背诵）是同门师兄弟——拉丁语的“召唤术”来到英语里，专门负责把心里的火点着。
🎯 讲给孩子：excited 就是“心里的火被点着了”，所以人才又跳又叫。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
exciting令人兴奋的（修饰事物：an exciting match）
excitement兴奋（名词：jump up in excitement）
excited about对……兴奋（be excited about the trip）
bored / boring反义对：人 bored（感到无聊）/ 物 boring（无聊的）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Nothing great was ever achieved without enthusiasm.” 🔥
没有热忱，成不了大事。—— 爱默生（1841）：excited 的火苗烧得久一点，就叫 enthusiasm。

🎵 歌词里的 excited

🎵 Pointer Sisters《I'm So Excited》（1982）：Billboard 前十的迪斯科金曲，三姐妹把“激动”唱成全场蹦跳的指令——I'm so excited, and I just can't hide it（我太激动了，根本藏不住）。
🔗 情绪家族页互链：与 proud 页、win 页同属“高能量”歌单。

⚡ 冷知识

心理学有个“吊桥效应”：心跳加速时，大脑有时分不清是害怕还是兴奋——在晃晃悠悠的吊桥上遇见的人，更容易被误认为“心动”。过山车上又怕又爽，也是同一个原理。所以英语里 excited 和 scared 的身体反应几乎一样：心跳、手心出汗、想大叫——区别只在你想给它起什么名字。
