📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Last Weekend

Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写climb + ed —— 规则过去式；⚠️ climb 里的 b 不发音
✏️ 口诀：爬坡不喘“波”（b 是哑巴），加 ed 变 climbed
音·发音/klaɪmd/ 一个音节收尾 /d/，千万别读出 b
🎧 跟读：climbed a hill · climbed the mountain · climb up
意·释义爬山（过去发生）。climb 爬（山、梯子、树）；hill 小山，mountain 大山
用·句型① I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday. (周六我和朋友们爬山了。——课本原句！)
② We climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park. (我们在公园爬山、划船。——课本原句！)
③ The hill is not high. Let's climb it!
④ Did you climb a hill last weekend?
拓·同族词climb a hill 爬小山 / climb a mountain 爬大山 / climb a tree 爬树
对比：hill 小山（本单元）· mountain 高山

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.
课本句We climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
描述The hill was green and beautiful.
询问Did you climb a hill last weekend?
功能句总结 · 山与高低

hill vs mountain：hill 小山丘 ⛰（本单元）· mountain 高大险峻
登顶表达：climb up / get to the top（到山顶）
景色感叹：What a beautiful view!（多美的风景！）

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

爬山是本单元最能“身体记忆”过去式的活动：上山时喊 climbing, climbing!，登顶后总结 I climbed a hill!，孩子把动作和 climbed 一绑定，过去式就不再是抽象语法。下山注意安全，顺势复习安全句 Be careful!

🎮 闯关游戏 (5关)

📣 关卡1 · 听音做动作
家长说 climbing，孩子原地做爬山摆臂；说 climbed，孩子站直举手喊 Top!（到顶了）

① Last Saturday we ___ (climb) a hill. → climbed
② Look! The monkey is ___ the tree. → climbing

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The Little Hill Race》

周末，Lily 和爸爸去爬小山。I will win! Lily 说着冲在最前面。爬到一半她气喘吁吁：Dad, my legs are tired! 爸爸说：Slow down, enjoy the climb. 两人边爬边数台阶、找松果。登顶那一刻，整个城市都在脚下：Wow! We climbed the hill! 风吹过来，Lily 觉得自己长高了。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who climbed faster at first? (Lily)
• What did Dad say when Lily was tired? (Slow down, enjoy the climb)
• 问问孩子：What can you see from the top of a hill?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 原形 ── climb a hill → climbed a hill
├── 山家族 ── hill 小山 · mountain 高山 · top 山顶
├── 哑巴字母 ── clim(b) · thum(b) · lam(b)

└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── The Climb 人生就像爬山

🏛️ 词源故事

climb 来自古英语 climban（攀爬）。有意思的是，最早拼写里并没有 b，16世纪人们仿照 thumb（拇指）、lamb（羊羔）等词给它“安”了一个不发音的 b——一个学究气的美丽错误，却沿用至今。hill 来自古英语 hyll，就是“小山包”。🎯 讲给孩子：climb 里的 b 是个哑巴，说话别理它，写作业别丢它。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“The best view comes after the hardest climb.” 🌄
最美的风景在最难走的爬坡之后。——英语励志俗语。登顶回头看，I climbed a hill 的每一步都值了。

🎵 歌词里的 climbed a hill

🎵 Miley Cyrus《The Climb》(2009)：迪士尼时期励志金曲，Ain't about how fast I get there——重要的不是多快到山顶，而是这一路向上。

🎵 与 climb a hill 页互链（同源曲目）；爬山如此，学过去式的爬坡也如此。

⚡ 冷知识

苏格兰有一项历史悠久的“冲山”传统：人们比谁先跑上小山山顶，这就是山地赛跑（hill race）的雏形之一。而 Miley Cyrus 的 The Climb 后来成了无数毕业典礼和运动会的背景音乐——爬坡这件事，全世界孩子都在做。
