📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 3 Animals

Lesson 1 · B. Let's play
— We're going to the zoo. What animals do you want to see? Why?
— I want to see tigers. They're big.
— I want to see pandas. They're cute.
— I want to see elephants. They're big and tall. I want to see their long noses.

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and answer
— Hi, Linda. What are you doing?
— I'm drawing a picture of my favourite animal. It's a big, grey animal.
— Yes, my favourite animal is the elephant.
— What's your favourite animal? — Guess!
— Wow, that's a beautiful picture! Let me see. Mmm, your favourite animal is the horse?
— No. Look. My favourite animal is the bee. — It's lovely!

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（节选）
Different children love different animals.
Bob likes elephants. An elephant eats leaves and grass. It lives on the grassland or in the forest. It is big and strong. It has two long teeth called tusks and a big nose called a trunk.
Amanda loves pandas. A panda lives in a bamboo forest and eats bamboo there. It's black and white.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写w·hy 三字母疑问词，wh + y；同族 what / where / when / who
✏️ 口诀：wh 一家最爱问，y 结尾的是 why
音·发音/waɪ/ 与字母 Y 的读音同音
🎧 跟读：Why do you like it? · Why not?
意·释义为什么（问原因）；口语里 Why! 还可表惊讶（哎呀）
用·句型① What animals do you want to see? Why? (课本原句)
② Why do you like pandas? — Because they're cute. (你为什么喜欢熊猫？——因为它们可爱。)
③ Why not? (为什么不呢？——表示同意)
④ I don't know why. (我不知道为什么。)
拓·同族词because 因为（why 的"回答搭档"）；疑问词家族：what / where / when / who / how

功能句总结 · Why...? 与 Because... 的问答配对

问原因：Why do you like pandas?

答原因：Because they're cute.（because 后接完整句子）

语序规则：Why + 一般疑问句语序（Why do you... ✔ · Why you do... ❌）

⚠️ 五年级考点：Why 问原因，Because 答原因，一问一答要配对！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

why 是本单元的思维发动机：课本 Lesson 1 用一个 Why? 让孩子说出喜欢动物的理由。建议把 Why? 变成家庭口头禅——孩子每说一个观点，家长追问 Why?，孩子用 Because... 回答，一次对话同时练句型和逻辑。注意纠正语序错误 "Why you like it?" → "Why do you like it?"。

📖 微型故事《Why? Why? Why?》

🐼 熊猫豆豆最爱问 "Why?"。"Why do we eat bamboo?" 妈妈答："Because it grows here." "Why is it black and white?" "Because... hmm, good question!" 豆豆又问小马："Why can you run fast?" 小马说："Because I practice!" 最后豆豆问老师："Why do I love asking why?" 老师笑着说："Because that's how we learn!" 原来，每个 Why 的后面，都藏着一扇新的门。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 豆豆最爱问什么？(Why)
• 谁说 "Because I practice"? (The horse)
• 问问孩子：Ask me a "Why" question about animals!

🏛️ 词源故事

why 来自古英语 hwȳ，和拉丁语 quo 是远古亲戚——人类问"为什么"的历史，和语言本身一样老。科学史上最有名的 "Why" 问题，要数牛顿问"苹果为什么往下掉"，一问问出了万有引力。
🎯 讲给孩子：会问 Why，是所有学问的第一步。

📜 名言 & 谚语

"The important thing is not to stop questioning."（重要的是不要停止提问。）—— 爱因斯坦的名言：每个 Why 都是好奇心的火苗。

🎵 歌词里的 why

🎵 Backstreet Boys《Tell Me Why》(1997)：后街男孩热单，hook 反复 "Tell me why"，是 why 问句的洗脑示范——与 because 页互链：Why...? Because... 一问一答。彩蛋：后街男孩名曲《I Want It That Way》副歌那句 "Tell me why—Ain't nothing but a heartache" 更是全球合唱名场面。

⚡ 冷知识

英语疑问词为什么大多是 wh- 开头？因为它们都出自古英语的 hw- 词根。另外，"Why did the chicken cross the road?"（鸡为什么过马路？）是英语世界最经典的老派笑话——标准答案 "To get to the other side."（为了到马路对面。）故意平淡得让人愣住，笑点就在"你以为有深意，其实没有"。
