📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 6 Winter Vacation（寒假）

Lesson 1 · A. 订计划
— What are you doing?
— I'm making a plan for my winter vacation. It starts this Monday.
— What are you going to do?
— From January sixteenth to twenty-eighth, I'm going to skating classes. I'm going to play in the snow there with my cousin.
— What else?
— Spring Festival is coming in February.

Lesson 1 · B. 大家的寒假计划
— What are you going to do in your winter vacation?
— I'm going to learn Chinese painting.
— I'm a sports fan. I'm going to train for a basketball match.
（计划短语：learn to make dumplings · visit my grandparents · do some chores · go to a maths club · meet my pen pal）

Lesson 2 · A. 放学最后一天
— Lily, today is the last day of school. Where are you going and what are you going to do there?
— I'm going to Harbin with my parents. We're going to see ice lanterns and play in the snow.
— I don't like cold weather. I'm afraid of getting a cold.
（短语：bring back Harbin sausages 带回哈尔滨红肠）

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（寒假心愿）
I am going to read some books about Canada. I hear there is a lot of snow in Canada, so I want to learn to ski. I think it will be hard, but I want to have a try!
I am going to prepare for a Peking Opera competition. I hope I can win the first prize this year.
I am looking forward to visiting my uncle. A wonderful winter vacation is waiting for me!

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写priz + e：不发音的 e 别丢！变复数 z 后加 es：prizes
✏️ 口诀：拿奖要带 e，复数别忘了 es。
音·发音/praɪz/ 一个音节，词尾浊 /z/；对比 price（价格）词尾清 /s/
🎧 跟读：win a prize · the first prize · a big prize
意·释义奖品；奖 🏆（比赛赢得的东西；win the first prize 得一等奖）
用·句型① I hope I can win the first prize this year. (希望今年我能得一等奖。——课本原句！)
② She won a prize in the singing competition. (她在唱歌比赛中得了奖。)
③ What's the prize for the winner? (赢家的奖品是什么？)
④ The prize is a trip to Harbin! (奖品是哈尔滨之旅！)
拓·同族词prize-winning 获奖的 · Nobel Prize 诺贝尔奖 · award 奖（正式）· medal 奖牌

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
愿望I hope I can win the first prize.
得奖She won a prize for her painting.
问答— What's the prize? — A new bike!
鼓励Have a try — you may win a prize!
📋 功能句总结 · 得奖怎么表达

win + prize：win the first prize（得一等奖）· win a prize（得奖）

祝贺：Congratulations on your prize!（恭喜获奖！）

颁奖句：The prize goes to ...!（奖品归……！）

⚠️ win 的宾语是比赛/奖项：win the game / win the prize

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

寒假里各种比赛、展示多，是练 prize 的好时机。孩子得了奖，全家说 Congratulations on your prize!；没得奖，就用名句安慰：重要的不是输赢，是怎么打的。可以玩“家庭颁奖礼”：给本周表现设 fun prize（最搞笑奖、最努力奖），让每个家人都 win a prize。

📖 微型故事《没有奖牌的第一名》

学校朗诵比赛，小薇练了一个月。比赛那天她发挥很好，可第一名只有一个。名单念完，没有她。好朋友凑过来：“I'm sorry. No prize this time.” 小薇吸吸鼻子，笑了：“It's OK. The prize isn't only a medal. I got braver — that's my prize.” 第二年，小薇又报名了。这一次，她捧回了 first prize 的奖状。但她说，最珍贵的 prize 还是去年那个：摔不坏的勇气。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Did Xiaowei win a prize the first year? (No, she didn't.)
• What was her real prize? (Getting braver —— 勇气)
• 问问孩子：If you don't win a prize, what can still be your prize?

🗺️ 思维导图

prize 奖品 🏆
├── 动词 ── win a prize 得奖 · get a prize 拿奖
├── 等级 ── the first prize 一等奖 · the second prize 二等奖
├── 名奖 ── Nobel Prize 诺贝尔奖 · prize-winning 获奖的
├── 近义 ── award 奖 · medal 奖牌
└── 心法 ── 重要的不是 prize，是拼过的自己

🏛️ 词源故事

prize 和 price 本是一家：都来自拉丁语 pretium（价值），经古法语 pris 进入英语。后来一个专指“奖品”（你赢得的东西），一个专指“价格”（你付出的东西），词形也分了家。发音上 prize 词尾是浊音 /z/，price 词尾是清音 /s/，听感一浊一脆。
🎯 讲给孩子：prize 和 price 是分家的兄弟——一个“赢来的价值”，一个“付出的价值”。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“For when the One Great Scorer comes to mark against your name, He writes — not that you won or lost — but how you played the Game.” 🏅
伟大的记分员记录的，不是你输赢，而是你如何比赛。—— 诗人格兰特兰德·赖斯（Grantland Rice）：prize 只属于赢家，但比赛的荣耀属于每个全力以赴的人。

🎵 歌词里的 prize

🎵 ABBA《The Winner Takes It All》（1980）：ABBA 传世金曲，“The winner takes it all, the loser standing small”——奖杯只有一座，歌里也唱出了输家的体面。
🔗 与 competition 页互链：比赛与奖项一页两开花——那边讲胜负，这边讲 prize。

⚡ 冷知识

世界上最著名的 prize 是诺贝尔奖：发明炸药的诺贝尔立遗嘱用遗产设奖，1901 年首次颁发，获奖者除了奖章和证书，还能拿到一笔奖金——有趣的是，诺贝尔奖没有设“数学奖”，原因至今众说纷纭。体育比赛的 prize 常是奖杯 trophy、奖牌 medal——奥运冠军咬奖牌的照片，全世界都熟悉。
