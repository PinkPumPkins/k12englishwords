📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Feelings（情绪）

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and number（小狗连环画，情绪九宫格）
My dog climbs on the table and eats Lily's lunch, so I am angry.（狗偷吃午餐→生气）
He's ill, and I'm worried.（狗病了→担心）
My dog suddenly runs over to Yaoyao, so Yaoyao is scared.（狗冲向瑶瑶→害怕）
I can't find my dog. It makes me cry. → I am very sad.（找不到狗→哭→难过）
He comes back home. I am very excited.（狗回家了→激动）
My dog has a running race. He runs very fast and wins the race. I am proud.（赢赛跑→自豪）

Lesson 1 · B. Let's talk（情绪接龙）
— I am going to sing for my class.（自豪）
— My grandma is ill.（担心）
— My cousin takes away my model plane.（生气）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（老照片里的为什么）
— You look sad in this picture. Why?
— I was sad because I was ill. I had to stay in bed.
— I was happy because Andy came to see me and helped me with my maths.
— I was worried because I wasn't good at maths. I didn't like maths tests.
— Did you do well in the maths test? — Yes. Look, I got an A. I was so proud. — Congratulations!

Lesson 2 · B. Choose and talk（because 句型操练）
— You look sad. Why?
— I am sad because I am ill. I can't play with my friends. I have to stay in bed.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Andy 的日记两则）
Wednesday：I got an A on my maths test. I felt very happy. … After school we went to a singing competition. We sang together and won first place, so I felt very proud.
Friday：My grandmother was ill. She went to hospital, and I was very worried. … I played basketball with my friend Jiayi. I hurt my leg, so I was sad.

Let's Spell · ough 的发音
bought 买 · brought 带来 · thought 想 · enough 足够 · tough 坚硬 · rough 粗糙

Fun Time · Good to know（颜色与情绪）
To 'see red' means to get very angry.（see red＝勃然大怒）
In English, you 'feel blue' when you are sad.（feel blue＝心情低落）
但在中国，红色是喜庆的颜色：春节穿红衣、发红包。

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写find —— i 后面 nd 收尾；过去式 found 把 i 变 ou
✏️ 口诀：find 变 found，i 开花成 ou！
音·发音/faɪnd/ 一个音节，i 读 /aɪ/；found 读 /faʊnd/
🎧 跟读：find my dog · find out the answer · can't find
意·释义发现、找到 🧭（强调“找到”的结果；“找的过程”用 look for，“查明真相”用 find out）
用·句型① I can't find my dog. (我找不到我的狗了。)
② I look all round the house, but I find nothing. (我找遍了房子四周，什么也没找到。)
③ Can you find out when the train leaves? (你能查一下火车几点开吗？)
④ The prince wanted to find the beautiful girl. (王子想找到那位美丽的姑娘。)
拓·同族词find → found（过去式；与“创立”的 found 同形不同源）→ finder 发现者 → findings 调查结果

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
寻物I can't find my keys.
过程I'm looking for my keys.
查明Let's find out the answer.
观点I find this book interesting.
📋 功能句总结 · find / look for / find out 三兄弟

look for（找的过程）：I am looking for my pen.（正在找。）

find（找到的结果）：I found my pen!（找到了。）

find out（查明、弄清）：Please find out the answer.（查明白。）

⚠️ 课本原句 I can't find my dog——苦苦找却没找到，用 can't find。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

find/look for/find out 是最容易混的三兄弟，用“藏宝游戏”一次讲清：家长藏东西，孩子边找边说 I'm looking for...；找到喊 I found it!；查资料时说 Let's find out. 三个词各归其位。U2 那首《I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For》就是最好的歌名记忆卡。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《谁动了我的口琴》

周四早晨，小林的口琴不翼而飞。他翻书包、掀沙发、钻床底：“I'm looking for my harmonica... but I can't find it!” 全家总动员，最后是妹妹举着口琴从阳台跑来：“It was in the flower pot. I found it there!” 原来昨天她想给花“吹吹风”。小林笑着吹响一个长音——寻找三部曲完成：looking for（过程）、find（结果）、find out（真相：花盆怪盗是妹妹）。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Where was the harmonica? (In the flower pot.)
• Who found it? (The little sister.)
• 问问孩子：What did you once lose and then find?

🗺️ 思维导图

find 发现
├── 变形 ── find → found → found
├── 三兄弟 ── look for 找的过程 · find 找到 · find out 查明
├── 陷阱 ── found ≠ 创立的 found（同形不同源）
├── 家族 ── finder 发现者 · findings 调查结果
└── 歌 ── U2《I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For》

🏛️ 词源故事

find 的远祖是印欧词根 *pent-（走、踏出），同根词里就有 path（小路）——“发现”的本义，是“替迷路的人踏出一条路”。而它的过去式 found 与“创立”动词 found（found a school 创办学校）同形不同源：后者来自拉丁语 fundare（打地基）。
🎯 讲给孩子：find 和 path 是亲戚——找到，就是找到了路。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
find out查明、弄清（找出真相/答案：find out the truth）
finding(s)调查结果（research findings）

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Not all those who wander are lost.” 🧭
并非所有流浪者都迷失方向。—— 托尔金《魔戒》诗行：寻找的人未必走丢，也许正在路上。

🎵 歌词里的 find

🎵 U2《I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For》（1987）：格莱美年度唱片，收在神专辑《The Joshua Tree》里——歌名本身就是一句最地道的现在完成时：我还没找到我正在寻找的。
🔗 与 look for 页互链（同曲同源：寻找主题）。

⚡ 冷知识

《The Joshua Tree》封面上那棵沙漠里的约书亚树，后来成了全球乐迷的朝圣地——2019 年它被人砍倒，乐迷在社交媒体上集体悼念一棵树。U2 用三十多年证明：有的“寻找”，找的早就不只是东西。
