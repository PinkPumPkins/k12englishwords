📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 4 Free Time

Lesson 1 · A. Listen and match / B. Let's talk
— What do you usually do in your free time, Bill?
— I like reading books. I have many story books.
Bill and Ted are talking about hobbies. He goes to a hockey club twice a week. He can't skate very well, but he likes playing hockey.

Lesson 2 · A. Listen and number / B. Let's talk
Bill and Ted are talking about their weekend plans.
Sometimes Tina, Ted and their parents go out together.

Lesson 3 · B. Let's talk / C. Let's read
— Did you have a special weekend?
I watched a nature show about Canadian animals—beavers, raccoons and moose. There are so many interesting things to do here. We sang and danced together and then we all ate birthday cake.

Project Time · Ted's Quiz
— I am good at hockey. / My favourite TV shows are nature shows.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写talk + about 两词一顿：talk about sth。✏️ 口诀：talk后接about，谈论话题跑不了；alk里l不发音
音·发音/tɔːk əˈbaʊt/；alk组合读/tɔːk/。
🎧 跟读：talk about hobbies · talk about our weekend plans
意·释义谈论；讨论（talk about + 话题：talk about the weather 谈天气）
用·句型① Bill and Ted are talking about hobbies.（比尔和泰德正在谈论爱好。——课本原句）
② What are you talking about?（你们在谈论什么？）
③ They are talking about their weekend plans.（——课本原句）
④ Let's talk about it after class.（下课后我们谈谈这件事。）
拓·同族词talk谈话 · talker健谈的人 · talk show脱口秀；对比搭配：talk with/to sb 与某人交谈；同单元句型：talking with Miss Jackson

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
谈论爱好They are talking about hobbies.
谈论计划We are talking about our weekend plans.
提问What are you talking about?
邀约Let's talk about it after class.
功能句总结 · 谈论话题 talk about

谈论内容：talk about + 话题 → We are talking about the weekend.

与某人谈：talk with/to sb → Bill is talking with Miss Jackson.

⚠️ talk about + 事情；talk with + 人，介词搭配别混！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

本单元围绕'自由时光'展开，talk about是串起三课的句型词。晚饭后玩'新闻主播'游戏：孩子用 We are talking about ... 播报今天聊过的话题；顺带纠正alk中l不发音的读法，再对比talk about（谈事）和talk with（对人）的介词分工。

👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The Talking Club》

🗣️ 六(1)班开了个"Talking Club"。Lily说："Today we talk about hobbies." Tom举手："I talk about hockey — I play twice a week!" Mia说："We talked about our weekend plans — we go out together." 老师笑："What are you talking about now?" 全班齐声："We are talking about TALK!" 原来，谈论本身就是最快乐的自由时光。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• 俱乐部里大家在谈论什么？(Hobbies 爱好)
• Tom每周去几次冰球俱乐部？(Twice a week)
• 问问孩子：What do you like to talk about with your friends?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 搭配 ── talk about + 话题 · talk with/to + 人 ├── 课本句 ── talking about hobbies · weekend plans ├── 时态 ── talk→talking（be + V-ing 进行时） ├── 家族 ── talker健谈者 · talk show脱口秀 └── 近义 ── say说内容 · speak说语言 · tell告诉

🏛️ 词源故事

talk与tale（故事）、tell（告诉）同属日耳曼'讲述'家族——tale的古英语形式talu本义就是'故事'，一般词源词典认为talk与它沾亲带故；talk show（脱口秀）20世纪中期在美国走红，'talk'由此成了'用嘴做节目'的代名词。about来自古英语onbufan（在外面围绕）——'围绕着一个话题说话'就是talk about。
🎯 讲给孩子：talk about＝围着话题转圈圈地说。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义

talk with/to sb与某人交谈（about接物，with接人）
talk show脱口秀（talk+show）
small talk寒暄、闲聊（small+talk）

📜 名言 & 谚语

"Talk is cheap." 🗣️ 空谈无益（说起来便宜）。—— 英语经典谚语：光说不做假把式；配套谚语 Actions speak louder than words（行动胜于言辞）——正是Free Time里'去做'的精神。

🎵 歌词里的 talk about

🎵 Coldplay《Talk》（2005）：酷玩乐队《X&Y》专辑摇滚名曲，歌名只有一个词——Talk！副歌 "Come back and talk to me" 一遍遍呼唤，正是talk about的灵魂：想聊，就一直聊下去。
🔗 与talk页同曲互链：talk家族两页一首歌，谈论话题永远是自由时光的主旋律。

⚡ 冷知识

英语里small talk指'寒暄'，聊天气是英国人最常见的small talk——所以英美人见面说 It's a lovely day, isn't it? 往往不是真聊天气，而是开启对话的钥匙；美国深夜脱口秀（late-night talk shows）一播几十年，主持人靠一张嘴撑起整个节目。
