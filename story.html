📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 5 Famous People（名人）

Lesson 1 · A. Look, listen and learn（名人墙）
— Hi children, look at these famous people. They did great things.
— This is Li Bai. He was Chinese. He was a poet. He wrote many famous poems. One of his famous poems is 'In the Quiet Night'.（李白·《静夜思》）
— This is Hans Christian Andersen. He was Danish. He was a writer. He wrote many stories for children. I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.（安徒生·《丑小鸭》）
— This is Xu Beihong. He was Chinese. He was an artist. He was good at painting horses.（徐悲鸿善画马）
— This is Thomas Edison. He was American. He was an inventor. He invented a lot of things.（爱迪生）
— This is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was French. He was a scientist. He studied insects. He is famous for his book named 'The Book of Insects'.（法布尔·《昆虫记》）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number（名人问答）
— Hi, Sam. Come and look at these pictures.
— OK. Who's that man?
— He's Thomas Edison. He's famous for his inventions.
— Who's this man? What did he do?
— He is Jean-Henri Fabre. He was a French scientist.
— Who is your favourite famous person, Joy?
— It's Hans Christian Andersen. He was a Danish writer.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（名人小传两篇）
Helen Keller was born in America in 1880. When she was very young, she got very sick. After that, she could not hear, speak or see. Helen worked very hard. She learned how to read, write and talk. When she was 20, she went to university. She is famous for writing many books and helping the deaf and blind.（海伦·凯勒）
Isaac Newton was a famous scientist. He was born in England in 1643. When he was a child, he was very quiet. He was always thinking about things. One day he was reading a book under an apple tree. He saw an apple fall from the tree. He was the first person to find out why.（牛顿·苹果落地）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写stor·y —— stor + y；复数 y 变 i 加 es → stories
✏️ 口诀：故事要 many，y 变 i 加 es！
音·发音/ˈstɔː.ri/ 两个音节，or 读长音 /ɔː/；美式 r 卷舌
🎧 跟读：tell a story · a story about animals · story books
意·释义故事 📖（可数：tell a story 讲故事；a story about... 一个关于……的故事；storybook 故事书）
用·句型① He wrote many stories for children. (他为孩子们写了许多故事。)
② I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'. (我喜欢他的故事《丑小鸭》。)
③ Grandpa tells us stories at night. (爷爷晚上给我们讲故事。)
④ Every picture tells a story. (每幅画都有一个故事。)
拓·同族词story 故事 → stories 复数 → storyteller 讲故事的人 → storybook 故事书；history 历史（同源亲戚！）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
课本句I like his story 'The Ugly Duckling'.
讲故事Grandma tells me a story every night.
内容It's a story about a little duck who becomes a swan.
写作I wrote a story about my cat for the class.
📋 功能句总结 · tell a story

讲/读故事：tell a story（讲故事）/ read a story（读故事）。

关于什么的故事：a story about + 名词：a story about a duckling.

⚠️ 复数 y 变 i 加 es：story → stories；一个故事是 a story。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

故事是本单元的暗线：安徒生写 story，Story Time 讲名人逆袭。睡前十分钟是最佳场景：孩子读一句英文，家长复述一句中文，轮流来。讲完问一句 What's the story about?（故事讲什么？）——一句话概括，是阅读理解的第一步。也讲讲“故事”和“历史”是亲戚（见词源）。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《外婆的故事会》

外婆不识几个英文单词，却是小区故事会冠军。今晚她讲丑小鸭：“有只小鸭，大家都笑它……”安安接：“Then he becomes a swan! It's Andersen's story!” 外婆惊讶：“你还知道作者？”“He was a Danish writer. He wrote many stories for children.” 外婆把安安搂紧：“故事不怕翻译，好故事哪儿都开花。”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who told the story tonight? (Grandma.)
• What did Anan add? (The writer was Andersen, a Danish writer.)
• 问问孩子：你会用英语讲哪个故事的开头？两句就够。

🏛️ 词源故事

story 和 history 是亲兄弟，都来自希腊语 historia——本义“打听来的事、调查的结果”。古人把“查清楚的事”讲出来：讲过去大事的是 history，讲人间悲欢的是 story。后来 story 走了民间路线，成了睡前故事的“故”事。
🎯 讲给孩子：每个 story 里都藏着一点 history。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Every picture tells a story.” 🖼️
每幅画都有一个故事。—— 19 世纪英语谚语：画是凝固的故事，故事是流动的画——正好接上本单元 artist 和 writer 两页。

🎵 歌词里的 story

🎵 Taylor Swift《Love Story》（2008）：全球热单——Taylor 把罗密欧与朱丽叶的故事改写成大团圆结局，“Romeo, take me somewhere we can be alone”，一首歌就是一个 story。
🔗 与 writer 页互链：写 story 的人是 writer；与 poem 页是近亲——诗和故事都是文字的孩子。

⚡ 冷知识

现存最古老的故事文学作品是《吉尔伽美什史诗》，刻在泥板上，距今约 4000 年——讲一位国王寻找永生的旅程。中文“故事”二字本义是“旧事”：过去的事讲给别人听，就成了 story。所以睡前故事，其实是人类最古老的“新闻联播”。
