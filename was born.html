Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— When did you start to use a computer, Joy?
— When I was four. What about you?
— I started when I was six.
— When did you start to buy things, Bill?
— When I was five. I bought an ice cream. It was delicious. What did you learn to do when you were five?
— When I was five, I learned to swim and started to study English.
— When did you go to kindergarten?
— Oh, I went to kindergarten when I was three.
— Me too.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（Nancy 成长自述）
My name is Nancy. I was born on April 5th, 2004. I learned to speak at six months old. I learned to climb when I was eight months old. I climbed out of my bed and hurt my head. I learned to walk when I was one year old. One day I walked out of my home and into the street! I learned to eat with chopsticks when I was three. I got food all over my face! I learned to swim when I was four, but I only went swimming in the bath. When I was five I learned to wash clothes, but I got myself all wet! In grade four I learned to cook – I cooked eggs, but they tasted very bad!! Now I'm in grade five.

Story Time · The Ugly Duckling（丑小鸭）
Once there was a mother duck. She was sitting on seven little eggs. One by one, six little yellow ducklings came out of the eggs. ... Two days later, out came a duckling. But this duckling wasn't yellow. It was grey, and it had a long, thin neck. ... Summer came. The ducklings all grew bigger, and the ugly duckling was the biggest. ... Finally, spring came. 🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写was + born —— born 是 bear（生）的过去分词，be born 固定搭配表示“出生”
✏️ 口诀：出生要用被动说，born 前面站 was/were
音·发音/wɒz bɔːn/ was 常弱读 /wəz/；born 长元音 /ɔː/ 别读成 /əʊ/
🎧 跟读：I was born in Beijing. · She was born on May 15th.
意·释义出生：说一个人何时何地出生必须用 be born，不能说 I born——出生是“被生下来的”，英语要用被动
用·句型① I was born on May 15th, 2004. (我出生于2004年5月15日。)
② I was born in Beijing. (我出生在北京。)
③ — When were you born? — I was born in 2014.
④ My little brother was born last year. (我小弟弟是去年出生的。)
拓·同族词be born 家族：was born / were born；birthday 生日 · born 天生的（a born singer 天生的歌手）
成长家族：started to speak · learned to walk（同在本单元）

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
出生I was born in 2014, and my sister was born in 2017.
日期She was born on a rainy day in June.
地点He was born in a small town near Shanghai.
问答— When were you born? — In May, on the fifteenth!
功能句总结 · 询问出生

问：When were you born?（你是什么时候出生的？）
答：I was born on May 15th, 2004.（具体日期用 on，年份月份用 in。）

口诀：具体到某天用 on，只到年月用 in。

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

本单元主线是“讲成长故事”。建议全家翻开孩子的出生照片：家长说 This is you. You were born on...，再让孩子学舌 I was born on...。重点纠正两个高频错误：一是漏掉 was（I born 是错的），二是 on/in 混用。弟弟妹妹的出生可以说 My sister was born...，自然复现 were。

🟡 中阶 · 家长加油站
👪 陪读故事 + 思维导图 + 词源小知识

📖 微型故事《The First Page》

妈妈拿来一本厚厚的成长册：This is your story book! 翻开第一页写着一行日期。Tom 念出来：I was born on a sunny morning in May! 妈妈笑说：那时候你只有这么小。Tom 量了量自己的胳膊——原来每个人都是从一个小小的“出生日”开始长大的，后面还有好多空白页等着他去填满。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What is on the first page? (The date Tom was born)
• What is the story book about? (Tom's growing-up story)
• 问问孩子：When were you born?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 结构 ── was/were + born（bear 的过去分词）
├── 时间 ── on+具体日 · in+年/月/地
├── 句型 ── When were you born? → I was born on...
├── 课本 ── Joy: I was born on May 15th, 2004.
└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── Born This Way：I was born this way

🏛️ 词源故事

born 来自古英语 boren，是动词 bear（生育、承载）的过去分词。bear 是个千岁老词，它还表示“熊”——古人不敢直呼熊的名字，就用“那褐色的家伙”代称，于是 bear 一词身兼两职。be born 字面是“被生下来”，所以英语里说出生必须用被动。🎯 讲给孩子：每个人都是“被生下来”的，所以 born 前面永远站着 was 或 were。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Great oaks from little acorns grow.” 🌰
巨大橡树从小橡果长成。——英语谚语。出生只是那颗小橡果，后面的年轮靠一天天长出来。

🎵 歌词里的 was born

🎵 Lady Gaga《Born This Way》(2011)：Billboard Hot 100 史上第1000首冠军单曲，副歌 I was born this way（我生来就是这样）传遍全球。课本里 was born 讲“何时出生”，歌里 born this way 讲“生来的样子”，同一个 born 两种自信用法。

🎵 互链：与 different 页互链（同曲《Born This Way》，主打“与众不同做自己”）。

⚡ 冷知识

英语世界登记出生的传统造就了 birth certificate（出生证明）——人生的第一张证件。英国从1837年起全国统一办理新生儿出生登记，表格第一栏永远是 date of birth（出生日期）。你脱口而出的 I was born on...，正是这句官方格式的口语版。
