📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 1 My Neighbourhood（我的街区）

Lesson 1 · 问位置
— Where is the bank?
— It's between the supermarket and Sunday Park.

Lesson 2 · 问路与指路
— Excuse me. Is there a post office in the neighbourhood?
— Yes, there is.
— How can I get there?
— Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. It's between the bookshop and the grocery.
— Thank you! — You're welcome.
（B部分：— Excuse me. How can I get to the post office? — Go straight and turn right at the first crossroads. It's on your right.）

Lesson 3 · Lucky的小任务（阅读）
Lucky, get some money from the bank, and then get to the post office before 4:30.
Lucky leaves at 3:30. He goes straight and turns left at the second crossroads. He sees the bank next to a restaurant. He gets some money and then goes into the restaurant. There he eats a hamburger. Then he sees a toy shop across from the restaurant. Lucky wants a toy, so he buys a ball. Finally, Lucky gets to the post office, but it's 5:00. Oh no!

Let's Check · 听力问答
— Excuse me. How can I get to the grocery?
— Go straight. Then turn right at the first crossroads. It's across from the supermarket.

Let's Spell · ai/ay 发音
It is a day in May. They say it will rain.

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写across = a + cross（十字），本义“横在十字路口上”→横过、对面
✏️ 口诀：一条马路一条杠，across from 隔街望！
音·发音/əˈkrɒs/ 两音节，重音在后：a-CROSS；from 弱读 /frəm/
🎧 跟读：across from the bank · across from the street
意·释义在…对面 🏪（隔着街道或空间正对着：The bank is across from the post office.）
用·句型① The toy shop is across from the restaurant. (玩具店在餐馆对面。)
② My school is across from the park. (我的学校在公园对面。)
③ We live across from each other. (我们住在对面。)
④ The bus stop is across from the bank. (公交站在银行对面。)
拓·同族词across 横过 → cross 十字 → crossroads 十字路口（本单元词！）；walk across the street 走过马路 · swim across 游过

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
方位The bank is across from the park.
动作Look both ways before you walk across the street.
指路Go straight, and you'll see it across from the supermarket.
生活I often buy toys in the shop across from my home.
功能句总结 · 问路与指路
问：
Where is the post office? 邮局在哪儿？
How can I get to the post office? 我怎么去邮局？
答：
It's across from the bank. 在银行对面。
Go straight and turn left at the first crossroads. 直走在第一个路口左转。
👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

四下 Unit 1 是方位与问路主题单元。建议带孩子在小区里玩“方位侦察兵”：走到银行前问 Where is the restaurant? 让孩子用 across from / next to / between 回答。重点区分 across from（隔街正对）与 next to（紧挨着），摆积木演示最直观。

📖 微型故事《马路对面的交换礼物》

🐕 乐乐和小狗 Lucky 分别住在一条小街的两边。Lucky 叼着一根骨头跑过马路，把骨头放在乐乐家门口；乐乐把一个玩具球放在 Lucky 家门口。妈妈笑说：“You two are neighbours across from each other!” 从此，马路对面成了两个好朋友每天打招呼的地方。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Who lives across from Lucky? (乐乐)
• What did Lucky take across the street? (一根骨头)
• 问问孩子：你家马路对面是什么？用 across from 说一说！

🗺️ 思维导图

across from 对面 ├── 位置 ── across from the bank 银行对面 ├── 动作 ── walk across the street 过马路 ├── 词根 ── a(在) + cross(十字) → 横过 ├── 对比 ── across from 对面 / next to 紧邻 / between 之间 └── 习语 ── across the pond 大西洋对岸

🏛️ 词源故事

across 来自 a（在）+ cross（十字），14世纪英语里写作 acros，本义“呈十字形横过”；cross 又来自拉丁语 crux（十字架）。一个“十字”里长出了整条街的方位词：横过是 across，十字路口是 crossroads。

🔴 高阶 · 知识珍宝库
📜 词源 · 名言 · 文学 · 冷知识

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
acrossa(在) + cross(十字) → 横过、对面
crossroadscross(十字) + roads(路) → 十字路口（本单元词）
across the board字面“覆盖整块板” → 全面地（源自赛马场赌注板）

📜 名言 & 谚语

短语 “across the board”（全面地、一视同仁）源自上世纪美国赛马场：赌注板 (board) 上对各档马龄押同样金额，后来引申为“对所有对象一个标准”——这就是它的“名言”身份。

🎵 歌词里的 across from

🎵 The Beatles《Across the Universe》(1970)：歌名即“穿越宇宙”，跨过星海也是 across！2008年 NASA 把这首歌向北极星方向发送进深空，是人类第一次给宇宙“发歌”。与 next to 页（Emeli Sandé《Next to Me》）互链。

⚡ 冷知识

英国人和美国人互称对方是 “across the pond”（池塘对岸）——把宽宽的大西洋幽默地叫成“小池塘”。所以“池塘对面的朋友”，就是大西洋彼岸的英美邻居啦。
