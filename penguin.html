📖 课本原文直通车 · Unit 3 Animal World

Get ready · 热身
— Do you often go to the zoo? What is your favourite animal?
— I like butterflies. They are insects. They can fly.

Lesson 1 · 课文短文（节选）
Lily and her classmates are going to see the animals in the zoo.
First, they are going to see the sharks. Sharks are fish. They can swim under the water. They are very big and like to eat small animals in the sea.
Then they want to see the snakes and the butterflies. Snakes are reptiles. They are very long and cold. Butterflies are beautiful animals. They are insects. They have six legs, and they can fly.
Next, they want to see some mammals, such as monkeys, pandas and tigers. Mammals give milk to their babies.
At last, they are going to see the penguins. Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They live on the ice. Look, how cute they are!

Lesson 1 · 分类知识（节选）
Reptiles have cold blood. They have scales. Most reptiles have four legs. All insects have six legs. Mother insects lay eggs. Most insects can fly.

Lesson 2 · 问答对话（节选）
— What kind of animals are monkeys?
— They are mammals.
— What can monkeys do?
— They can climb trees very well.
— What do monkeys like to eat?
— They like to eat peaches and bananas.

Lesson 3 · 短文 The Elephant（节选）
Elephants are mammals. They can be four metres tall. They don't eat meat. They only eat fruit, leaves and grass. They need a lot of food, so they spend most of their time eating. The elephant has two long, white teeth called tusks. It is the only animal with a trunk — a special long nose. The elephant can use its trunk to pick up a big tree or a small peanut. The elephant is also the mammal with the biggest ears. They look like two big fans. Elephants live in Africa and Asia. They are very strong, very smart and of course, very, very big.

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写pen·guin，注意开头是pen不是peng-。✏️口诀：penguin穿着“笔”挺的燕尾服
音·发音/ˈpeŋ.ɡwɪn/ 重音在前，注意/ŋɡ/连读。
🎧 跟读：penguins are birds · they live on the ice
意·释义企鹅🐧（南极绅士：是鸟却不会飞，游泳是健将）
用·句型① Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly.（课本原句）
② They live on the ice.（它们住在冰上。——课本原句）
③ Penguins can swim very fast.（企鹅游得很快。）
④ Look, how cute they are!（看，多可爱呀！——课本原句）
拓·同族词penguin→penguins（复数）；emperor penguin帝企鹅（企鹅里的巨人）；同单元分类词：bird鸟

📢 拓展跟读表（课外句 · 非课本原文）

句型句子
分类Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly.
住所They live on the ice.
本领Penguins can swim very fast.
感叹Look, how cute they are!
功能句总结 · but转折句

转折名片：Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly.

补充能力：They can swim very fast.

感叹句：How cute they are!

⚠️ cannot口语里常说can't；感叹句把How放句首！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

企鹅是最经典的“转折句”教材——是鸟却不会飞。让孩子连说三句：“Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They live on the ice. They can swim very fast.”再套用到自己身上：I am a child, but I can...。

📖 微型故事《The Flight Lesson》

🐧 小企鹅Pip看见海鸥飞过天空：“I want to fly, too!”妈妈说：“Penguins are birds, but we cannot fly in the sky.”Pip低下头。妈妈带他跳进海里：“Look!”Pip像小火箭一样冲出去：“Wow! I'm flying in the sea!”妈妈说：“Our wings are for swimming. We live on the ice, and we fly under the water.”Pip骄傲地说：“Every penguin is a pilot — an under-water pilot!”

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• Pip一开始想学什么？(To fly)
• 妈妈带他去了哪里？(Into the sea)
• 问问孩子：What can you do that many other kids can't?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 身份 ── birds鸟类 · cannot fly不会飞 ├── 本领 ── swim very fast游得快 · “水中飞行” ├── 家园 ── live on the ice冰上 · 南极 ├── 家族 ── emperor penguin帝企鹅（最大） └── 句型 ── ...,but...转折 · How cute they are!

🏛️ 词源故事

penguin的来历有几种说法，最流行的一种是它来自威尔士语pen gwyn（白头）——早年水手先用这个词称呼大海雀，后来大海雀灭绝了，名字就“传”给了长得有点像的企鹅。🎯 讲给孩子：企鹅的名字，可能是“借”来的。

🔍 构词密码

词构成与含义
penguin一说来自威尔士语pen（头）+ gwyn（白）→ “白头”
emperor penguin帝企鹅——企鹅家族的巨人，约1.2米高
king penguin王企鹅——皇帝下面还有“国王”
royal penguin皇家企鹅——企鹅家族自带“贵族封号”

📜 名言 & 谚语

纪录片《帝企鹅日记》(March of the Penguins)记录了帝企鹅爸爸在冰上孵蛋、几个月不吃不喝的故事，摘得奥斯卡最佳纪录片奖——这是企鹅自己“演”出来的名言级故事。

⚡ 冷知识

北极没有野生企鹅，企鹅住在南极及周边——所以北极熊和企鹅在野外永远不会见面；帝企鹅爸爸孵蛋时约两个月不吃东西，全靠体内脂肪支撑；帝企鹅能潜到500多米深，是鸟类里的潜水冠军。
