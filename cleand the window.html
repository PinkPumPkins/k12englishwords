Get ready · 词表
核心句型：What did you do last weekend?
词表：cleaned the window · watched TV · climbed a hill · visited grandparents · danced · jumped rope · listened to music · rowed a boat · played computer games · stayed at home · played the piano · played chess

Lesson 1
— What did you do last weekend? — I stayed at home.
On Sunday, I visited my grandparents. I cleaned the windows for them and watched TV.
I climbed a hill with my friends on Saturday.（B部分：What did you do last weekend? 问答歌谣）

Lesson 2 · A. Look, listen and number
— How was your weekend? — It was wonderful.
— What did you do last weekend? — I watched a football match with my friends, and then we played football together.
— On Sunday, I played the violin in a music club.
I played badminton with Joy in the morning. It was rainy in the afternoon. I played in the rain.
I stayed in bed all day. I was ill. — Oh, what a pity! I'm sorry to hear that.

Lesson 3 · A. Let's read（日记体短文）
I usually stay at home on Saturday morning and go to an English class on Saturday afternoon. But last weekend was special.
We danced and watched cartoons all morning. In the afternoon, we climbed a hill and rowed a boat in the park.
I watched a football match with my dad in the morning. My favourite team won the match. I was very happy.
I really enjoyed last weekend.

Let's Spell · Fun Time
-ed 发音三代表：cleaned /d/ · danced /t/ · visited /ɪd/（还有 played · rowed · jumped · washed 等）
Fun Time C 单元歌曲唱到：What did you do last weekend? On Saturday and Sunday, I listened to music and played football.

Story Time（原文待核：动物园里男孩与大象和名叫 Peanuts 的小男孩之间的笑话故事）

🟢 低阶 · 学生自习室
📌 背诵 + 自测 | 课本同步跟读

1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写clean + ed —— 规则动词过去式直接加 ed；⚠️ 站内文件名沿用历史拼写 cleand，正确书写是 cleaned，e 不能丢
✏️ 口诀：clean 干净加 ed，e-d 跟在后面飞
音·发音/kliːnd/ 尾音 /d/ 轻轻一带而过，不要读成“克林恩特”三连
🎧 跟读：cleaned the window · cleaned the windows（复数）
意·释义擦窗户（过去发生）：clean 擦拭、打扫，加 ed 表示“已经做完了”
用·句型① I cleaned the window yesterday. (我昨天擦了窗户。)
② I cleaned the windows for my grandparents. (我帮祖父母擦了窗户。)
③ — What did you do last weekend? — I cleaned the window. (你上周末做什么了？——我擦窗户了。)
④ The window is clean now. (现在窗户干净了。)
拓·同族词clean the room 打扫房间 · cleaner 清洁工 · dry-clean 干洗
家务家族：sweep the floor 扫地 · wash clothes 洗衣服（同在本单元）

句型句子

评价Look! The window is so clean.

功能句总结 · What did you do...?

问过去：
— What did you do last weekend?（你上周末做什么了？）
— I cleaned the window.（我擦窗户了。）

问答配对：did 来提问，动词过去式来回答！

👨‍👩‍👧 家长指导卡

本单元语法主线是一般过去时。建议在家让孩子边做家务边说英语：擦窗户时说 I cleaned the window，扫地时说 I swept the floor。重点提醒：clean 的过去式直接加 ed，不要写成 cleand——站内文件名是历史拼写，孩子书写一律以 cleaned 为准。

📖 微型故事《The Shiny Window》

周末早晨，Tom 看见窗户蒙着一层灰：The window is dirty! 他拿起抹布，喷上清洁剂，用力擦呀擦——I cleaned the window! 阳光一下子照进屋里，妹妹喊：Wow, I can see clearly now! Tom 看着亮晶晶的玻璃，心里像开了窗一样亮堂。

🧑‍🏫 家长提问卡：
• What did Tom clean? (The window)
• Who said I can see clearly now? (His sister)
• 问问孩子：What did you clean last weekend?

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 原形 ── clean the window → cleaned the window
├── 工具 ── cloth 抹布 · cleaner 清洁剂
├── 家务家族 ── sweep the floor · wash clothes
├── 句型 ── What did you do? → I cleaned the window.
└── 歌曲彩蛋 ── I Can See Clearly Now 雨过天晴

🏛️ 词源故事

window 来自古诺尔斯语 vindauga，字面是 wind（风）+ auga（眼睛）——“风之眼”！古时候窗户没有玻璃，就是墙上一个能看风景、能进风的洞。clean 来自古英语 clǣne，一千多年前就叫这个名字。clean 加 ed 变 cleaned，是英语里最守规矩的过去式。🎯 讲给孩子：窗户是房子的眼睛，擦窗户就是给房子洗眼睛。

📜 名言 & 谚语

“Every cloud has a silver lining.” 🌤
乌云背后都有一线阳光。——英语谚语。把窗擦亮，云开雾散，正好呼应 Johnny Nash 唱的 I can see clearly now。

🎵 歌词里的 cleand the window

🎵 Johnny Nash《I Can See Clearly Now》(1972)：Billboard 冠军曲，开场一句 I can see clearly now the rain is gone——雨停了、窗擦净了、天放晴了，三件事在歌里是同一件。它是雷鬼风格最早登上美国榜首的金曲之一。

🎵 记忆点：擦完窗户 = 看得清清楚楚，cleaned the window 与这首歌是同款心情。

⚡ 冷知识

窗户玻璃曾是奢侈品：几百年前的欧洲，很多房子装不起玻璃，普通人家用油纸或布挡风，只有富人才换得起“风之眼”上的玻璃。所以擦亮一扇玻璃窗，在当年可是相当值得炫耀的事。
